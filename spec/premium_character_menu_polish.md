# Character menu finish

The Character menu answers what the player has equipped, what can replace it, and which skills can be learned. Equipping, attuning, learning, and the existing close/back paths remain immediate. Gear, Magic and Skills retain their layout, type metrics, exact copy, icons, hit regions, state colors, item well shadows, and skill-node materials. No character animation changes are included.

The existing outer frame gains a neutral smoky base and contact shadow. Opt-in `UiSkin.apply_menu_finish` reuses the retained `SurfaceFinish` beneath content for a restrained warm crown, static grain, recessed edges, and reflected lips on the outer field, broad groups, resource chips and portrait backing. These decorations have no layout or input participation and never process while idle. The Skills graph keeps its full topology and focused rules visible.

Explicit Character opening settles opacity from 86% to 100% over 140 ms; explicit tab changes settle the new body over 120 ms. Positions and sizes do not animate, and no input lock is added. Ordinary inventory/progression rebuilds cancel and snap the response without replay. Closing, hiding, reopening or switching Reduced Motion on cannot leave inherited transparency. Reduced Motion presents the final state immediately, with no idle polling.

Proof uses the real renderer at 1920×1080 and 100% UI scale. Fresh baseline loadout and skill galleries are compared with the focused Character gallery, covering Gear/Magic/Skills, native focus and activation, keyboard/controller traversal and back, selected/locked states, lifecycle interruption, stable bounds, retained tree, live Reduced Motion, and settled motion cleanup. Rule, inventory, persistence and analytics behavior remains unchanged; this presentation adds no outcome events.
