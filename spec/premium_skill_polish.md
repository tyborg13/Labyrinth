# Skill medallion material and learning feedback

## Design

The Character/Skills and level-up tree answers which abilities are learned or available, their prerequisites, and what the selected ability does. The primary action remains Learn. Give the existing root circles, branch squares, junction diamonds, and keystone hexagons shape-matched depth with a quiet inset and directional metal rim. Preserve every node rectangle, icon size, connector, marker, exact rule, state color, and focus target. Successful persisted learning produces one short gold rim and incoming-route illumination, with no movement or delay; initial load, reopen, selection, and failed learning do not replay it. Reduced Motion shows the final state immediately and cancels any active confirmation. The persistent checkmark, learned styling, resource totals, and focused detail carry meaning without animation.

## Boundaries and proof

Only SkillTreeView presentation, a single post-success call in RunScene, and focused probes/tests change. No character/cutout artwork, map, recent pickup/chest/campfire work, or progression rules change. Code-native material primitives remain bounded; processing runs only during a confirmation and stops on completion, hide, reset, detach, or Reduced Motion.
