# Abilities and progression — complete design pass

Design only. Shared rules in DESIGN.md take precedence. This record covers all 30 skill definitions: 29 active, plus the retired Layaway entry. Enemy abilities are covered separately in ENEMY_MIGRATION_DESIGN.md; equipment-granted actions remain in the complete card/equipment manifests.

The pass includes deliberate ability reworks and a concrete interaction review of every retained skill. Existing hand management, defense, Light and reward identities remain valuable. A useful retained skill does not need an extra surface clause merely to be counted as reviewed.

## Direct reworks

**Prismatic Instinct supplies setup.** Replace the old intensity waiver with a choice of one real surface tile. It can prepare a relay or Fire for a later paid action, add Rubble to existing elemental terrain, or replace hostile elemental terrain. It never makes Ice active merely by painting beneath an actor.

**Connected-ground use:** one Prismatic Instinct tile can close a gap in an existing contiguous electrical patch, while Confluence can move a tile to complete or break that connection. Only Chain can bridge a bare-floor gap; neither skill silently grants Chain to the next attack. These are setup/support actions and never discharge a component merely by creating or moving its ground.

**Confluence manipulates existing setup.** Replace the highest-intensity substitution with moving a real surface layer. The original layer disappears; other layers and the occupant stay. Moving Ice away clears any Chill that loses its final supporting tile. Placing that Ice elsewhere still requires entry/start to activate it. Enemy-created terrain is eligible because ownership is not a separate rules currency.

These manual skills initially use the existing once-per-combat, no-card-play/no-Time activation model. Exact range and Confluence's keystone strength are provisional. Compare the single-tile relocation with Encore and Open Arsenal before locking its budget; do not declare that initial footprint balanced. Resolve any broader footprint or use-count change in the design rather than silently adding a global condition bypass.

**Sure-Footed changes with traps.** It protects against an actual center hit or Air push, while leaving the surrounding wake intact. Standing in a newly painted wake is not a blast hit and does not waste the use. This supports deliberate trap activation without turning subsequent Fire/Ice into private, harmless terrain.

## Activation, persistence and verification

- Use the existing SkillSigil and supported mouse/controller flows. Prismatic Instinct changes from hand selection to surface-kind and floor selection. Confluence selects a source layer and destination with an exact resulting-board preview. Do not add a replacement intensity meter or a competing ability button row.
- Show creation, removal, elemental replacement, lost Chill support, legal targets and immediate versus delayed effects. Do not allow activation beneath an unresolved card or movement preview. Cancellation and invalid targets spend nothing; consume a use only when a legal state-changing action commits. Selecting the same location/layer with no change is invalid. Preview recomputes against the committed board.
- Relocation is removal plus placement, not consumption by Detonate, Freeze or Chain. It does not itself trigger consumption-payoff relics. An actual new placement may trigger a placement relic only if that relic explicitly permits skill sources; the baseline is to keep existing card-specific relic hooks card-specific. Preserve surface creation source and the later manipulating action separately for analytics and kill credit.
- One committed skill action produces one use/event across preview, save/load and redraw. Keep learned IDs, prerequisites, exclusivity and progression investments. Remove obsolete armed-intensity state through an explicit migration. Confluence's old automatic classification and fatigue exception disappear with its old effect.
- Verify delayed Ice/Fire activation under both placement skills; immediately usable Electrified and later Detonate; removing the last Ice under a Chilled actor; layer choice on Rubble+elemental terrain; shared hazards for spawned decoys; trap protection versus wake creation; real movement distance in Rubble; capped recall and refund chains; and all noncombat reward/defeat paths.

This is a full review of the progression roster, not a claim that all 29 active skills need unrelated mechanics added. The two obsolete intensity skills receive new identities, trap/attack/movement hooks receive precise new contracts, and retained skills must work against the same board state as cards and enemies.
