# Enemy migration design — complete roster

Design-only companion to `DESIGN.md` v4, 2026-09-06. Covers all **18 current definitions** in `data/enemies.json`, including six bosses and three summoned/minion definitions. No implementation or numeric balance approval is implied. Keep internal IDs stable; displayed names and intent text can change where the old Poison/Burn identity no longer fits.

This is a full enemy design pass. Physical attackers, artillery and support retain distinct jobs; they do not all gain surface creation. Their new interaction comes from routes, defensive choices, displacement and denying prepared ground. Elemental specialists create or consume local board opportunities. Exact HP, damage, area size, Time, weights and encounter composition will be rescored together.

The v4 rules are binding: Rubble plus one elemental layer (Fire, Ice or Electrified); Fire/Ice occupant effects activate on entry or eligible turn start, never placement; successful Freeze consumes every supporting Ice tile under the target; Electrified is immediately usable and consumed only by explicit effects; shared hazards apply to every actor without owner exemptions. No Burn, Poison or intensity remains. Ordinary direct attacks retain their normal targets. Trap damage is center-only with a cardinal wake; Air traps push outward.

## Contracts that make these interactions fair

**Enemy intent commitment.** Show the chosen attack, area, direction and conditional ground consumption when the intent is revealed. Surface changes can reduce or remove its payoff. They must not trigger a hidden replacement attack, auto-select fresh distant fuel, or rotate a fixed announced boss pattern after the player has countered it. Exact current-state previews can update projected damage and reachable movement without hiding the reason.

**Crownfire replaces the old special trap mechanism.** Kindle Ground chooses a bounded set of floor tiles and paints Fire there; its following Crownfire intent displays those tiles and the cardinal union that surviving Fire would produce. The set is a telegraphed attack selection, not ownership of the Fire or a resource count. Fire from any source on a selected tile is eligible; Fire elsewhere is not silently added. If the player removes all selected Fire, Crownfire visibly fizzles rather than switching to a fresh damaging attack. Remove old `cinder_detonation_pending`/owner-trap immunity semantics or replace the pending record solely with this explicit attack plan. Preserve a real response opportunity in the initiative schedule; do not create and immediately force Crownfire before a player activation can occur.

**Local fuel is spatial, not a counter.** Shell, brace and specialist Shock reference actual selected tiles within their printed local reach. A shell may retain modest baseline defense if fuel disappears; the bonus is lost. The Wisp/Tempest empowered attack retains its disclosed ordinary shot if Electrified consumption fails, but loses Shock. The selected tile and bonus are visible before resolution; do not draw another nearby tile after the player removes the one shown. Consume before a printed movement step when the setup was selected at the old position. Remaining action order is explicit. Do not let an enemy pay for its current empowered attack with an after-hit tile it has not created yet.

**Ice remains two-step for enemies too.** An Ice painter does not immediately Chill its target. An eligible target's next start/entry activates Chill; it may leave or replace Ice before the Ice attack. Frozen cannot refresh, consumes all supporting Ice on success, and suppresses Chill acquisition through its skipped start. No enemy-only shortcut back to direct Freeze. Iskaldra's existing immunity rejects Freeze without consuming the player's Ice; it does not cancel Chill vulnerability.

**Immunities stay narrow and visible.** Retain Vaeloryx's Immobilize, Iskaldra's Freeze and Zekarion's Shock immunities. Delete Tharokh's Poison and Vyraketh's Burn entries because those statuses are retired. Propose no new elemental-hazard immunity. Neither artwork, element tag nor surface ownership makes an actor safe. Review existing frost-armor blocking of small damage separately and describe it accurately; do not add undisclosed hazard invulnerability.

**Traps stay the common system.** Ordinary enemy attacks may trigger a trap, but direct damage is only at its center and the wake affects the four neighbors. Wakes replace the elemental layer or add Rubble without immediate occupant damage/Chill. Air pushes use actual cardinal paths and can create visible cascades. Large footprints receive one center hit and one outward displacement per event, with the common deterministic direction rule. Remove all old neighboring-blast estimates and status riders from enemy planning.

## Required AI work

- **Cost and survival share the resolver.** Weighted Rubble movement, minimum progress for a fresh Move-1 allowance, entry Fire, start Fire, active Chill, Freeze, illusions, large footprints and trap wakes feed both intent evaluation and the exact displayed projection. Count actor contact once per step, not once for each footprint tile.
- **Value routes rather than forbidding hazards.** Prefer a safe route when it preserves the attack or support job. Accept finite Fire damage for a materially better threatening position when survivable. Never copy the old enormous one-use trap penalty onto every Fire tile. Include the next known start tick when evaluating whether stopping on Fire is survivable.
- **Maintain roles.** Crawlers pursue, artillery establishes firing lanes, Harriers retreat, Wardens screen and Surgeons support. Ground preferences do not override all these roles with universal kiting. Avoid intentionally suicidal moves when a useful nonlethal option exists; permit inevitable hazard deaths when genuinely trapped.
- **Evaluate setup locally.** Place Fire/Ice/Rubble to constrain a plausible route, cover an attack lane or prepare an actual follow-up—not to maximize painted tile count. Keep setup areas and empty-floor actions bounded. Penalize repeated identical placement and gratuitously replacing a useful allied setup.
- **Evaluate shared consequences.** Include collateral in Detonate, whether a wake gives the player better Fire/relay setup, and whether shell/brace consumption harms an ally's plan. Do not claim all enemy-created tiles as reserved inventory. A useful trap trigger may be area denial or an Air displacement even when its center is empty.
- **Consume only real accessible fuel.** Revalidate the revealed tile and range at resolution, keep the declared degraded result if denied, and never scan the whole board for substitute Fire/Rubble/Electrified. A fully erased Crownfire does not repaint before detonating.
- **Use the shared two-use Lightning solver.** Enemy Chain targets its opponents (player/illusions), never ordinary allied Wisps as hidden friendly actor conduits. Preserve normal Chain jump reach and individual relay nodes. Any damaging Lightning attack can separately discharge contiguous Electrified ground into its opponent occupants; ordinary non-Chain attacks cannot cross bare-floor gaps. Plan one Chain path and affected components from one board snapshot, with one damage set and no independent Chain per conduction hit. A component occupant may still be a route node without being damaged twice. Normal relay use consumes its nodes; Lightning conduction consumes the activated components. Explicit prior fuel spending is a separate earlier action, so spent Shock fuel cannot also serve the subsequent attack. Add no actor-count cap. A relay is not a hit/status/kill trigger.
- **Respect information and initiative.** Surface visuals do not reveal hidden actors or grant Light. Threat UI must match visibility rules. Setup/follow-up boss sequences require a real player response window despite relative initiative speed, Shock, Freeze or skipped activations. Do not rely on a vague 'next round.'

## Proof encounters for the full pass

1. Crawler/Warden approach through Rubble beside Fire: useful progress remains, while the threat changes correctly.
2. Ooze shell fuel denied; its split lands around shared Fire without retroactive AOE hits or summoned kill-credit farming.
3. Shale Bloomer + Gaoler: Rubble constrains escape, a visible pull crosses Fire, and changing the angle defeats the setup.
4. Surgeon clears a prepared Ice target; killing/displacing the Surgeon preserves the player's plan. Clearing ground never cleanses an already-Frozen ally.
5. Lancer lane paints Ice without immediate Chill; moving off the lane avoids Freeze, while pushing the Lancer into it enables the player's conversion.
6. Worldspines destroyed early cancel Faultline coverage and leave their explicitly local Rubble; no obscured surfaces survive under new spines.
7. Crownfire uses the shared selected union: player replacement/consumption reduces it, boss displacement causes self-harm, and a fully denied attack fizzles.
8. Iskaldra obeys Chill setup despite Freeze immunity herself; repeated attacks do not create an enemy-only stunlock loophole.
9. Zekarion/Wisps leave useful shared ground; denial removes specialist Shock. Verify ordinary contiguous conduction versus Chain air-gap relays, each side's opponent sets, and conductive Fire under the proposed relic. Removing one connecting tile splits an ordinary electrical network; a Chain may still bridge that gap within its normal reach.
10. Noctyrax tests lit safe ground: glowing Fire still does not protect from Eclipse. Vaeloryx push paths, Air trap cascades and large bodies match the exact preview.

These cases supplement whole-pool scoring and seeded encounters. They are acceptance targets for the authorized future implementation, not results from tests already run.
