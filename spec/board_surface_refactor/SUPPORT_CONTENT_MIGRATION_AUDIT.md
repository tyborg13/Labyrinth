# Gear, relic and skill migration audit

Read-only proposal, baseline `11a62f65e4070ae397d651778b550668bda0c854`. All **42 gear packages, 60 relics and 30 skills** are accounted for. The 13 gear packages below directly grant cards with retired intensity or Poison. Other gear still receives full surface compatibility and package-balance review through the card appendix.

**Current shared rules:** Rubble layer plus one elemental layer: Fire, Ice or Electrified. Light remains independent. Fire and Ice trigger only on actual tile entry or the affected actor turn start, never on placement. Fire initial tuning is 1 damage on entry and 2 on its own turn start, once per actor event even for a large footprint. Rubble instead changes ordinary entry movement cost directly; Electrified is a passive conduit until explicitly used. Entering Ice or beginning a non-Frozen activation on Ice activates Chilled while the actor remains on Ice. Painting Ice beneath a stationary actor does not activate Chilled. Leaving Ice or removing the supporting Ice clears Chilled. Ordinary non-Ice hits do not consume it. Frozen actors cannot gain Chilled, including during their skipped activation. Immediately before each individual hit, inspect active Chilled and Frozen state. Resolve damage using that pre-hit state, then an eligible Ice hit applies Freeze and consumes the enabling Ice beneath the actor. For a large actor, consume all Ice tiles it occupies once; clear Chilled. Ice painters place Ice only after their hit; this placement cannot activate Chilled or retroactively Freeze the target. Frozen is nonrefreshable and cannot coexist with regained Chilled. It skips its activation and thaws after that skipped activation; that Frozen turn start does not activate Ice. Newly painted Ice waits for a later actual entry or non-Frozen turn start. There is no additional recovery state, Thawing state or recovery guardrail.

**Electrified timing:** Electrified is immediately usable when placed; no entry, actor turn start, next-card or next-turn eligibility delay applies. Respect printed action order: placement before a later Chain can enable that Chain on the same card. A post-hit placement rider arrives after its attack and is immediately available to subsequent actions.

## Transformative relic direction

Nine existing slots now have concrete transformative replacement proposals, detailed in `RELIC_TRANSFORMATION_PROPOSALS.md`: conductive Fire, transported elemental ground, sideways force through spent Rubble, Stoneskin spent on melee geometry, Frozen finishers leaving Rubble, redistributed consumed Ice, remote attacks through spent Rubble, Rubble-fueled Detonate and exchanged Chain endpoints. Stable IDs remain; the table shows proposed display renames without changing baseline source descriptions.

These are varied rule changes, not nine new relics or an all-pairs reaction system. Keep simpler and non-elemental identities where useful. Each full-pool review must explain the action or drafting decision the relic enables; a generic extra damage/draw/Block reward is not automatically a transformative bridge. Optional geometric techniques are selected in the original targeting flow, with no interrupting post-hit decisions. Exact prices, rarity changes and final art remain implementation-design work.

## Relic event contract

Every relic row is reviewed, including retained identities. Card/action rewards require the player's own qualifying card action, not an enemy using shared ground. Turn caps reset on the player's activation cycle, not every actor activation. Real placement changes a layer; repainting an identical surface is not a fresh placement. Relocation is not Detonate/Freeze/Chain consumption.

Funeral Bell is explicitly reworked to count qualifying player-card-caused statused deaths; passive Fire deaths do not advance its reward or bank later card plays. The old off-turn pending-play path must not silently survive through this relic. Ordinary death, loot and outcome accounting remains intact.

Secondary relic damage is its own category. It respects defense and death resolution, but does not inherit direct-card attack riders or Chill/Frozen damage multipliers, recursively trigger other damage reactions, or award card plays from its secondary kills. Black Sun Dial has a single deterministic, previewed origin even when several tiles qualify, and its local pulse shows friendly danger. Preserve source/action/phase attribution in both live and projected resolution.

## Required numerical and systems audit

- Replace the heuristic intensity value, gate probabilities, spend costs, and Poison coefficients before scoring the new pool. Existing source-specific cohorts remain: rewards, equipment, starters, items. Current nominal score bands are not a numerical approval for new terrain mechanics.
- Evaluate setup cards on immediate value AND realized later benefit over representative initiative windows; do not award infinite value for permanent Fire. Count actual foot traffic and actor activation starts, no synthetic tick for repainting.
- Forced movement, Rubble and permanent Fire create markedly stronger control than today. Two movement points per activation make cost-2 Rubble a serious commitment; test exit/survival objectives and slow enemies, not only kill-all damage races.
- Entering Ice or beginning a non-Frozen activation on Ice activates Chilled while the actor remains on Ice. Painting Ice beneath a stationary actor does not activate Chilled. Leaving Ice or removing the supporting Ice clears Chilled. Ordinary non-Ice hits do not consume it. Frozen actors cannot gain Chilled, including during their skipped activation. Initial proposal: +1 damage on every direct hit against active Chilled. If already Frozen, use the shared x2 damage multiplier instead of adding the Chilled bonus. Neither numerical value is final balance signoff. Immediately before each individual hit, inspect active Chilled and Frozen state. Resolve damage using that pre-hit state, then an eligible Ice hit applies Freeze and consumes the enabling Ice beneath the actor. For a large actor, consume all Ice tiles it occupies once; clear Chilled. Ice painters place Ice only after their hit; this placement cannot activate Chilled or retroactively Freeze the target. Frozen is nonrefreshable and cannot coexist with regained Chilled. It skips its activation and thaws after that skipped activation; that Frozen turn start does not activate Ice. Newly painted Ice waits for a later actual entry or non-Frozen turn start. There is no additional recovery state, Thawing state or recovery guardrail.
- Preserve the Chain field as jump reach; correct the existing contradictory Grimoire entry instead of adding a target-count budget. Brightglass Lens can grant neutral attacks Chain relay use without making them Lightning component attacks. Ordinary damaging Lightning discharges contiguous ground without needing Chain. Count big actors once across Chain/AOE/conduction and forbid empty nodes from triggering hit/death rewards.
- Remove common automatic play refunds instead of replacing every intensity condition with unconditional flow. Review each exceptional refund against Flurry snapshots, kill bonuses, Gale Tabi, Vaulting Sigil, Storm Crown and high-draw cards.
- Surface damage and Detonate are separately attributable damage sources. They must not inherit every direct-card rider, Pierce, Expose, Sunder or on-hit relic automatically, and must not multiply those riders across every floor tile.
- Burn removal is confirmed, alongside Poison. Preserve top-level `burn: true` as Exhaust. Remove damaging-status fields, triggers, upgrade options, glossary entries and deprecated save status values; no specialist Burn exception remains.
- Generated upgrades need surface-aware budgets: AOE expansion now expands persistent territory; damage upgrades should state whether they affect direct damage, Fire ticks or Detonate. No upgrade may add per-tile stacks, duration counters, Poison or intensity.
- Review misleading neutral names/art (`ember_jab`, `cinderburst`, `ember_rain`, `cyclone_seal`) as identity work, not hidden mechanics. Retag/retheme deliberately if chosen; preserve stable IDs/save references and independently review changed art or names.
