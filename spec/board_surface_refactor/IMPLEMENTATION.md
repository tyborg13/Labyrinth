## Integration decisions and limits

- New rules are version 4. Historical logs and isolated migration/refund code may still mention retired fields; active content, HUD, combat and upgrade paths may not. Top-level boolean `burn` remains the existing Exhaust cost.
- Migration preserves an in-progress action boundary. Its only authored HP adjustment is an untouched v1 tutorial target from 17 to 15 HP, matching the new opening's fixed two-card lesson. Already injured/dead targets retain their damage history.
- Events use a bounded 256-event encounter tail and a monotonic sequence. Source card IDs describe the original painter; a separate trigger card identifies later displacement contact. Passive damage never banks future card plays.
- Final relic regressions preserve Faultline's legal native melee primary targets, evaluate Black Sun's Rubble underlay at actual consumption after payment, and prevent Updraft from transporting an original layer that was removed and recreated during the action. An identical no-op repaint or independent Rubble change does not invalidate surviving elemental ground.
- Release review also corrected board right-click cancellation for terrain abilities and conditional Detonate damage on cards. Hazardous enemy attack routes now forecast actual entry effects, prefer surviving routes, and use finite health/defense/Chill costs alongside movement cost. Safe detours preserve the paid attack; unavoidable or sufficiently cheap survivable hazards remain traversable.
- Seeded fixed-loadout greedy runs establish deterministic execution and useful interaction samples, not comparative build balance. The deliberate mixed run uses actual commands and generated rooms. These are not a complete human boss-campaign balance study. Preserve this distinction in future tuning.
- Controlled runs were refreshed after the release-review AI correction. Changed seeded policy outcomes are retained and explained in PLAYTESTS.md; deterministic replays and old-planner diagnostic comparisons distinguish intentional route changes from nondeterminism.
- On this Mac, the headless suite prints a CA-certificate diagnostic and renderer/ObjectDB shutdown warnings. The exact base-master suite passes with the same warning categories. These are not hidden failed assertions; candidate and base logs are retained separately.
- Steam copy and the existing trailer describe the old rules. The capture fixture has been migrated and checked against current card values. Recapture and store publication follow inspection/approval of the game pass; old media must not be labeled as footage of the new rules.

Evidence lives under `output/board-surface-refactor/`. Failed intermediate runs are retained to distinguish real regressions from retired-rule fixtures.
