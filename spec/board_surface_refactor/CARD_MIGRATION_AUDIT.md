# Complete card migration proposal

Read-only design audit at `11a62f65e4070ae397d651778b550668bda0c854`. No card data has been changed. This is a complete disposition of all **159** definitions, including every starter, gear card, consumable, neutral reward and inactive legacy entry. Retaining an already useful role means reviewing it against the new terrain rules, not skipping it.

**Counts:** 56 normal rewards; 78 nonstarter equipment cards; 14 starters; 10 consumables; one inactive legacy card. Reward elements: Fire 9, Ice 10, Lightning 9, Air 9, Earth 10, neutral 9. All definitions: Fire 17, Ice 15, Lightning 17, Air 11, Earth 12, neutral 87. There are 42 equipment definitions granting 88 distinct cards, including 10 starter definitions; `far_draw` is shared by two weapons.

**Direct removals:** 59 cards reference intensity; 10 reference Poison, with 6 overlapping, so 63 require at least one of those removals. All 159 still require compatibility/role/balance review. The user has confirmed complete removal of the damaging Burn status alongside Poison; no specialist Burn package remains. Preserve every top-level `burn: true`: that field means Exhaust, not the damaging Burn status.

**Shared contract:** Rubble layer plus one elemental layer containing Fire, Ice or Electrified; Light remains separate. Surfaces persist until consumed/replaced/cleared; repainting is idempotent. Intrinsic Chain N preserves its current maximum jump reach with no additional-target cap. Individual Electrified tiles can serve as nodes under that normal Chain reach. Current source descriptions remain baseline evidence, not proposed final text or numerical tuning.

**Electrified timing:** Electrified is immediately usable after placement. A later damaging Lightning attack can discharge its connected component, and intrinsic Chain can use individual nodes. An earlier separately printed placement action may enable a later attack on the same card. Ordinary post-hit placement arrives after the complete attack and cannot recursively expand that attack.

**Ordinary Lightning conduction:** Confirmed direction: a damaging Lightning impact touching Electrified discharges the cardinally contiguous connected component and hits its opponent occupants. No Chain keyword is required. Diagonal contact, nearby enemies off the component and air gaps do not conduct. Lightning support, movement and placement alone do not discharge.

**Consumption recommendation:** Design recommendation for tuning: discharge consumes the whole touched Electrified component, including its empty branches. Chain-only relay use consumes its actually used nodes. Neither surface visits nor consumption are actor hits. The full-component consumption policy is an explicit design recommendation, not numeric or playtest signoff.

**One attack, one target union:** Take one pre-attack snapshot. Plan intrinsic Chain independently; collect each touched cardinal component; union native, Chain and conduction opponent targets and hit each actor once. Conduction does not start a fresh Chain head from every affected actor. Consume the planned ground once; separate later attacks and Flurry repeats see the remaining board. Post-attack painting cannot extend its own already planned attack.

**Surface timing:** Fire and Ice activate only on actual tile entry or the affected actor turn start, never on placement. Fire initial tuning is 1 damage on entry and 2 on its own turn start, once per actor event for large footprints. Rubble changes ordinary entry movement cost directly; Electrified is a passive conduit until explicitly used.

**Chilled and Freeze:** Actual Ice entry or a non-Frozen turn start on Ice activates Chilled while the actor remains on Ice. Placement beneath a stationary actor does not. Leaving or removing its supporting Ice clears Chilled; ordinary non-Ice hits do not consume it. Initial tuning is +1 damage on every direct hit against active Chilled, with already-Frozen x2 replacing rather than adding that bonus.

**Ice hit order:** Check active Chilled immediately before each hit, resolve damage, then apply eligible Freeze and consume the enabling Ice. A large actor loses all Ice beneath its footprint once; Chilled clears. Painters lay Ice after the hit, with no placement trigger. Frozen cannot refresh or regain Chilled, including at its skipped turn start. It thaws after that skipped activation; subsequent actual movement or a later non-Frozen turn start can activate new Ice. No additional recovery or Thawing state is proposed.
