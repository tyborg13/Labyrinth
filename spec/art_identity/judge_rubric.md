# Art judge rubric (independent reviewer)

You are judging new board art for a dark-fantasy tactics game against its canon. You did not make this art; be strict.
You get: the canon sheet (approved sprites at game scale), the candidate review sheet (candidate on a real board beside
the hero and two canon peers at 1x and 2x, front/rear/mirrored views, landmark overlay, metric table), and the subject
description. Judge only what you see. For each criterion answer PASS or FAIL with one line of visual evidence.

1. View: front faces the lower-left (rear faces the upper-right) in a three-quarter view from above like the hero; the
   near side is lower-right; not head-on, not symmetrical. Objects: base seen corner-on as a 2:1 diamond, top visible.
2. Proportions: stylized and chunky like the hero/canon (big head, thick limbs, big hands/feet, heavy masses); not
   realistic, lanky or spindly. It must not read as a miniature beside the hero.
   Boxy is a FAIL (owner 2026-10-13, gaoler pilot): a creature whose silhouette is built from long straight parallel
   edges and right-angle corners (cube head, slab torso, column legs, brick boots) reads as a toy, even when chunky.
3. Detail: the same amount of detail per body part as canon at the same draw size; no filigree, thin strands, tiny
   repeated detail or noisy texture.
4. Palette and value: dark, muted, desaturated like canon; no large pale or bright area; exactly one accent colour.
5. Size: plausible in-game size next to the hero for what it is (state the height ratio you see).
6. Hand: one-block dark outline, chunky pixel clusters, light from the upper left; would pass as drawn by the same
   artist as the hero.
7. Cutout readiness (creatures): limbs, head and weapon separable from the torso; front and rear depict the same
   creature consistently.
8. Identity: reads at game size as the subject described, with a distinct silhouette from the canon peers.

9. Structure legibility (wings, extra limbs, anything the model must invent in 3D): for every wing and limb you can
   point to where it attaches, which side of the body it belongs to, and how front and rear show the same structure.
   A pile of feathers or limbs you cannot trace is a FAIL even if everything else passes (owner verdict 2026-10-13 on
   the gust shrike rear: "unable to figure out the wings").

Overall: PASS only if every criterion passes. End with `VERDICT: PASS` or `VERDICT: FAIL` and, for a fail, the
smallest concrete change that would fix each failed criterion.
