# View clauses (one per kit job; the kit pastes the section body verbatim)

## creature_front
A high three-quarter view seen from above (camera about 35 degrees above the floor), the same camera as the references.
The creature is turned a full 45 degrees so its chest and face point toward the image's LOWER-LEFT corner, exactly like
the hero reference. The side of its body on the viewer's right is nearer the camera: its right-hand shoulder, hip and
foot sit LOWER in the image than the far ones, so the line through both shoulders and the line through both feet slope
DOWN toward the right at about 1 in 2. We see the top of the head and shoulders slightly from above. It must NOT face
the viewer head-on and must NOT be a symmetrical front view. Limbs are separated from the torso by background so the
art can be cut into animation parts.

## creature_rear
The same creature, same camera, turned to face the image's UPPER-RIGHT: we see its back and the back of its head, as in
the hero rear reference. Its body on the viewer's right is still nearer the camera and lower in the image, so the
shoulder and foot lines still slope DOWN toward the right at about 1 in 2. Identical proportions, colours and
equipment to the approved front image (the first reference). Limbs separated from the torso by background.

## beast_front
A high three-quarter view seen from above (camera about 35 degrees above the floor), the same camera as the references.
The creature is turned 45 degrees: its head points toward the image's LOWER-LEFT and its hindquarters toward the
UPPER-RIGHT, so its body's long axis runs diagonally up to the right at about 1 in 2, exactly like the crawler
reference. The legs on the viewer's right are nearer the camera and lower in the image. We see its back from above.
It must NOT face the viewer head-on and must NOT be symmetrical. Legs separated from each other by background.

## beast_rear
The same creature, same camera, turned so its head points toward the image's UPPER-RIGHT and its hindquarters toward
the LOWER-LEFT; we see its back and rump. The legs on the viewer's right are still nearer the camera and lower in the
image. Identical proportions and colours to the approved front image (the first reference).

## object_plinth
A high three-quarter view seen from above (camera about 35 degrees above the floor), the same camera as the brazier
reference: top surfaces are visible from above, circles appear as wide ellipses. The object stands on a SQUARE base seen
CORNER-ON: the base's top and bottom are DIAMONDS twice as wide as they are tall (the footprint guide image's dark
diamond is the ground footprint; do not draw the guide lines). The base's front corner points straight down at the
bottom centre of the image and its two visible side faces slope away up-left and up-right at about 1 in 2. No edge of
the base is horizontal. Vertical parts rise straight up.
