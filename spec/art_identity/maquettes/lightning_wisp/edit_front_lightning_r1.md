The first reference is an approved painting of a storm-cloud creature on a flat #00FF00 background. Keep EVERYTHING in it

## Structure of the new lightning
The second reference is the creature's 3D maquette rendered from the same camera: its flat colours are part labels
(blue = the left arc, orange = the right arc, yellow = the crown). Follow their paths: each arc is an IRREGULAR jagged
line of five or six uneven segments with sharp direction changes and one or two short side forks; the crown is three
thin jagged forks of clearly different heights rising from the top of the cloud. The arcs reach only a short way past
the cloud, as in the maquette (shorter than in the first reference).

## Lightning look
- A pale lavender-white core (about RGB 230,215,255) ONE block wide, with a one-block violet fringe (about 130,90,250),
  like the crackle in the third reference.
- Jagged and irregular: uneven segment lengths, small forks. Never a regular zigzag, never a cartoon bolt, no glow halo.
- Never yellow, orange or gold anywhere.
- Lightning covers at most about one twelfth of the creature's painted area.
- The eye: one small round eye in the same lavender-white.
- Remove any orange or brown rims under the cloud's lobes; shadows there are neutral charcoal.
