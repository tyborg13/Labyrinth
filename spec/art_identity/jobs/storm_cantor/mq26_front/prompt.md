## References are for camera, scale and hand only
Never copy a reference's pose, silhouette, species or equipment. The new subject must have its own distinct silhouette
that could not be mistaken for any reference at game size.

## Scale and detail (most important)
The reference images are sprites from this game enlarged 4x with hard pixel edges: every small square block you see in
them is ONE game pixel. Paint the new subject in exactly the same block size and the same hand:
- every colour patch is at least one 4x4 block; contours step in 4-pixel blocks; outline is one dark block wide.
- the same LOW level of detail as the references: a few big, chunky, readable masses per body part. Nothing thinner
  than one block. No fine texture, no thin strands or filaments, no tiny repeated details (scales, stitches, chain
  links, rivets, cracks) unless each piece is at least 3 blocks wide.
- The image will be shrunk 4x back to game size; whatever needs a block smaller than 4 pixels will vanish, so leave it out.

## Proportions and forms
Follow the character's original painting for proportions and forms: a gaunt original stays gaunt, with long legs and a
narrow waist; an angular helm, face plate, spike or tine stays angular and sharp; a broad brute stays broad. Draw every
shape bold enough to read at game size: no limb thinner than 3 blocks, even at wrists and ankles, and hands and feet a
little oversized like the hero's.
- Joints are articulated, never balls. Armoured elbows and knees are pointed or cupped plate cops that overlap the limb
  above and below. Bare or cloth limbs taper and overlap at the bend. Never draw spheres, beads or sausage segments at
  a joint.
- Hands are real hands. A palm with separate, jointed fingers that taper, and a thumb. Fingers wrap around anything
  held; claws stay long and segmented. Never mittens, blobs or fused fingers.
- In the three-quarter view, chest pieces, faces and props turn with the body. A ribcage, belt, buckle or emblem is never
  left face-on to the viewer.
- Rounded where the original is rounded (cloth, muscle, domes), crisp where it is angular (plate edges, spikes,
  visors, tines). Neither boxy blocks nor clay-like balls and tubes.

## Palette and light
Muted, dark, desaturated dark-fantasy palette like the references. Values are mostly dark and mid-dark: no large area is
lighter than the hero's face; skin, bone and cloth are dusky, never pale, tan or bright. Light from the upper left.
Exactly ONE clear accent colour, used sparingly.
Paint like the references, not flat cartoon shading: every big mass has three or four value steps with warm brown-tinted
shadows, a little worn texture and grime inside the big shapes. Avoid flat neutral grey as a main colour (grey skin is
grey-brown and mottled); cool colours belong only to ice, steel or the accent.
Gaunt or wretched creatures stay gaunt in character, but are still drawn with bold shapes: limbs at least 3 blocks
thick, with big hands and feet. Bold does not mean muscular, swollen or boxy.

## Background
Perfectly flat opaque chroma green #00FF00 (RGB 0,255,0) on every background pixel, including gaps between limbs.
No floor, ground, cast shadow, glow halo, vignette, scenery, border, text or labels. Nothing touches the canvas edge.
