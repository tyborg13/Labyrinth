# Burrow mound, take 3: judge verdict (director, 2026-11-23)

Judged on `sheet_3.png` (source and as-shipped rows, board scale beside the hero, crag_outcrop and raised_cover), per the owner's split: art is judged by Claude directly, with no subagent.

1. View: PASS. The base is corner-on and follows the 2:1 diamond (base_v_left 0.41, base_v_right −0.38). The top of the heap is visible and the near side is lower-right.
2. Proportions: PASS. One low continuous heap filling about 2/3 of the footprint, with chunky clods. It is a ground feature, so it is meant to sit below the hero's knee.
3. Detail: PASS. Clods are 2 to 4 blocks; there are two half-buried stones and no filigree or strands.
4. Palette and value: PASS. Chroma 0.161 and value 0.206 are in band. The soil is dark umber, warmer than take 2 but muted. The only accent is the ochre hide (#c9a46a family), a small area.
5. Size: PASS. 137×53 px (about 0.30× the hero's height) is a low mound by design. `height_drawn` fails only the standing-prop band (120–180), which does not apply to a ground mound. That is an exception, with its reason recorded here.
6. Hand: PASS. Chunky clusters, upper-left light on the clod tops, and a dark lower edge that matches the canon props' outlines once processed.
7. Cutout readiness: N/A (static board state; the Burrow Grub rig hides at the end of `burrow`, and this mound is what stays).
8. Identity: PASS. At board size it reads as freshly churned earth with a creature's ringed back sunk in a dark crack along the crest. Its silhouette is distinct from the crag and the cover.
9. Structure legibility: PASS. The single hide arc dips under the soil at both ends.

Takes 1 and 2 were rejected:
- Take 1 was too orange (chroma 0.231) and read as a pile of stones.
- Take 2's hide sat on top of the soil like pebbles, and its base edges were too flat.

VERDICT: PASS. Production source: `processed_3.png` (from `raw_3.png`). The owner approves it from a real-game capture after adoption.
