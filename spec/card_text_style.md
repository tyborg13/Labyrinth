# Card text style

Use these house rules for card faces, card descriptions, glossary and tooltip definitions, the Grimoire, relics, skills, enemy intents and other rules text.

## Card face rules

1. Open with a damage token or a keyword command. Show targeting on the chip and area with a diagram.
2. Print an icon, the capitalized term, then its number. Capitalize any word printed with its icon. Ordinary object nouns in prose stay lowercase.
3. Put riders after the base effect when that is the resolution order. Use additive conditions and +N bonus tokens.
4. Name the thing a Burst destroys. If the trigger already destroyed the thing, make that thing the subject of “Bursts.”
5. Name a noun before using a pronoun for it. Repeat the noun when a new sentence refers back to it.

## Rules templates

**R1. Card sentences are commands.** Start with the verb and leave "you" implied.

**R2. Definitions come in two forms, and the term's kind decides which.** An *action* (something a card does: Burst, Push, Pull, Move, Blink, Swap, Draw, Heal, Stagger) is defined as a command, as if it were card text. A *state* (a status, ground, trait or defence) is defined as a present-tense fact, with the affected thing as the subject.

**R3. A state definition names its holder as the subject.** Status and trait definitions start with the holder in the plural: "Exposed units ...", "Anchored units ...". Ground definitions start with "A unit that enters [Ground] ...". Repeat the holder when a new sentence refers to the holder.

**R4. A definition repeats the term's own name.** Write the name again instead of "here", "it", "this" or "the shown".

**R5. Use X for the term’s number in static definitions.** Fill X from a card, intent or relic when the source supplies the term’s number.

**R6. Numbers are digits.** On faces the number follows the term's word (icon, word, number: "{expose}Expose 2"). In prose the number comes before its unit noun ("2 damage", "3 tiles", "1 Card Play").

**R7. Capitalize game terms, including any word printed with its icon.** Ordinary object nouns remain lowercase. Card face rule 2 governs icon and word order on faces.

**R8. A face never carries reminder text.** The tooltip defines the term, and the Grimoire holds the detail.

**R9. Use one sentence for a tooltip, plus a second only for how the term ends or decays.**

**R10. A tooltip lists no exceptions.** The exception lives on the unusual thing (a relic, a trait on that unit), in the preview, or in the Grimoire.

**R11. Use "each" for every effect that applies to many things.**

**R12. The target chip names what you click, and the sentence calls it "the target" or "the tile".**

**R13. Refer back with "that" plus the noun, and refer to the card itself with "this" plus a noun.** A bare pronoun is allowed only when its noun comes earlier in the same sentence, and repeating the noun is preferred.

**R14. "Adjacent" means the 4 tiles that share a side, and it is our only adjacency word.**

**R15. Push and Pull take the target, then the distance.** Name a direction when the origin differs from the source. The source for a card is you unless the card names a tile.

**R16. Timing words come from a fixed set and go first in the sentence.** Use “this turn,” “until your next turn,” “on its next turn,” “at the start of your turn,” “for X turns,” and “for the rest of combat.” Use “When” for a one-off, “Whenever” for a repeating event, and “On each [Rule] beat,” for battlefield rules.

**R17. Put conditions after the base effect and write the added effect.** Use the approved condition forms, including “On [Ground]: also …” and “Each [Ground] tile hit: also …”. Use “instead” for a true replacement.

**R18. Print each bonus as a +N token.** The preview shows the total.

**R19. Use periods to end sentences.** Join effects with “and” or “then” or write two sentences. Omit semicolons, dashes and parentheses on faces.

**R20. Use “then” when order matters, “and” when it does not, and “before” when a rider happens first.**

**R21. Use game verbs.** Deal, take, hit, gain, give, lose, draw, heal, restore, move, walk, place, leave, create, raise, destroy, remove, spend, swap and keyword verbs carry rules.

**R22. Avoid hedges and narration.** Use "can" or "may" only for a real player choice. Never write "sometimes", "possibly", "mostly", "simply" or "some X".

**R23. Elements are card families.** When a rule reads the element, write “Lightning card” or the appropriate family. Rules use damage without elemental damage types.

**R24. Faces show areas with diagrams.** Prose uses “the area,” “adjacent,” or “within X tiles of [tile].” Put independent diagrams where each area is named.

**R25. Each idea gets one word** (see the vocabulary table). Wind becomes Gust, actor and character become unit, battle and fight become combat, grant becomes give, and "next to" becomes adjacent.

## Definition template

> **[Subject or command] + [present-tense effect, with X for the term's number] + [optional second sentence: how it ends].** One sentence where possible, periods only, with no exceptions and no pronoun for a noun outside the sentence.

The subject slot depends on the kind of term:

| Kind | Subject slot | Model |
|---|---|---|
| Action keyword | Imperative verb | "Delay the target's next turn by X Time." |
| Status on a unit | "[Status]ed units" | "Immobilized units can't Move, Blink or Swap on their next turn." |
| Defence or resource | The term itself | "Block absorbs damage until the start of the holder's next turn." |
| Ground | "A unit that enters [Ground] ..." | "A unit that enters Ice or starts its turn on Ice gains Expose 2." |
| Card keyword | "This card ..." | "This card leaves your deck until the end of combat." |
| Enemy trait or intent | "This enemy ..." or "[Trait] units ..." | "This enemy hits each Marked tile, then ..." |
| Battlefield rule | "On each [Rule] beat, ..." | "On each Shadowstep beat, each hidden enemy Blinks toward you." |
| Element | "[Element] cards ..." | "Air cards Push, Pull and move units." |
| Topic or object | A noun phrase | "The 9×9 grid where combat happens." |


## Fixed vocabulary
| Idea | Our word | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Adjacency | **adjacent**: the 4 tiles that share a side with a tile | next to, around, beside, neighbouring, nearby, surrounding, near |
| A tile and its neighbours | **that tile and each adjacent tile** | on or next to, on or around, its tile and around |
| Area | faces: **the diagram**; prose: **the area**, **within X tiles of [tile]** | cross, diamond, ring, blast, splash, pattern, radius-N (prose) |
| Each/all | **each** | every, all, everyone, anyone, whoever, everything |
| Target | **the target** (chosen by the chip); **a target** in definitions | it, the struck enemy, the chosen one |
| Chosen tile | **the tile** (the chip says "Tile") | this tile, the chosen tile (except in definitions) |
| You | **you**, **your tile** | the hero, the player, under you |
| Enemy | **enemy** | foe, monster, hostile |
| Unit | **unit**: you, an enemy, or one of your illusions | character, actor, creature, everyone, anyone, whoever |
| Tile | **tile** | square, space, cell, spot, field |
| Ground | **ground**: Fire, Ice, Electrified, Rubble; write the name alone ("Fire", not "Fire ground") | surface, field, network, charged ground |
| Terrain | **terrain**: Outcrop, crate, keg | obstacle, object, piece |
| This turn | **this turn**: ends when your turn ends | this round |
| Next turn (yours) | **your next turn**; **until your next turn** | next round |
| A unit's next turn | **on its next turn** (after the unit is named) | during its next turn |
| Whole fight | **for the rest of combat** | the whole battle, this fight, this encounter |
| Self-reference | **this card, this enemy, this relic, this attack, this Burst** | it, its, the card's own name |
| Back-reference | **that** + noun: that tile, that Ice, that enemy, that illusion | it, them, there, here |
| Holder of a status or defence | **the holder** | its holder, whoever has it |
| Push/Pull origin | **the source**: you, unless the card names a tile | the attacker (for a non-attack), its origin |
| Receiving a term | **gain/gains** (self or holder), **give** (to another) | get, grant, receive, suffer, "is Exposed 2" |
| Damage | **deal X damage**, **takes X damage** | hurt, harm, strike (outside the melee Strike), hit for |
| Removing things | **destroy** (terrain, illusion, or ground by Burst); **remove** (ground or a status as a cost or cleanup) | blow up, shatter, explode, expend, clear, dispel |
| Random | **a random enemy** | any enemy (unless you choose) |
| Seeing | **visible**, **hidden** | seen, concealed, revealed (except for Light) |
