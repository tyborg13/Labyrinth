## Preparation and next-pass boundary

`sources/` retains original generator outputs; `paint/` retains their native-resolution alpha conversion; `units/`, `portraits/`, `relics/`, `map/`, `props/` and `icons/` contain proposed exports. The manifest records prompts, references and the original generator path. Superseded originals remain available at their provenance paths.

`prepare_assets.js` performs only technical matte/alpha conversion, uniform scaling and placement. `register_views.js` records common body-height/foot baselines and explicit landmarks in `registration.json`. `build_review.js` and `build_anatomy_audit.js` compose inspection sheets from those outputs. No generated anatomy is painted or corrected by those scripts.

The next implementation pass must turn accepted front/rear paint into editable cutouts, author idle/move/attack/hit/death or summon clips, fill hidden joints as required and validate actual draw order. Static front/rear source art is not evidence that an animation rig is already usable. No additional new actor, relic, map-head or prop identity is currently unaccounted for.

If a later design revision introduces a new summon, status, command or prop, give that concept a distinct asset and register it under the production icon policy when implementing it.
