# Menu material finish

Pre-battle answers which foes await and whether the current loadout is ready; Start remains its primary action. Character answers what is equipped, attuned or learned; its existing Gear, Magic and Skills actions retain their exact rules and layout. This pass adds an opt-in finish to the existing large menu backplates, resource plates and portrait wells: quiet bronze reflection, a dark cool lower field, fine static grain and an inner contact edge. Icons, foes, skill state, quantities and native focus remain above the surface; exact mechanics stay in their established inspection views. Authored outer ornaments remain authoritative.

Extend UiSkin and its retained SurfaceFinish helper instead of adding another panel family. The new outer, section, chip and portrait treatments participate in neither container layout nor input, and have no process loop. Existing global dialog, choice and parchment treatments are unchanged. Menu owners opt in after their normal shared frame styling and preserve margins, radii, sizes and font metrics. New bitmap art and icon identities are unnecessary for these code-native materials.

Pointer, keyboard and controller paths remain available, including focus/detail, selection, activation, back and input handoff. Explicit menu entry/tab feedback is brief and optional; Reduced Motion and interrupted lifecycle changes snap to the complete readable state. Each menu owner documents its transition and affected interaction proof.

Acceptance uses fresh original native 1920×1080/UI100 before/after for pre-battle and Character Gear/Magic/Skills, relevant focused/selected/disabled/detail and motion states, focused input/lifecycle assertions, the full regression suite and independent exact-HEAD review. Every UI rubric gate applies. The shared high-risk contract and worker Git preflight passed on the published baseline 539a74976ceddcc8ff42c41843cedabb2e1cb610 before edits. Character animation, combat presentation, pickup light, treasure and campfire are outside this pass.
