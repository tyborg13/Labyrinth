# Visual pass 4 painted assets: sources and provenance

Generated 2026-10-04 with Codex's built-in image generation tool (`codex exec`,
Codex CLI 0.159.2) by a dedicated image-generation run, reviewed and approved by
Claude (the pass's design owner) before implementation. The originals are copied
here unchanged; they live under `spec/` so they are excluded from exports.
`tools/process_visual_pass_4_assets.py` derives the medallion ring, ink pool and
price tag textures under `assets/art/ui/visual_pass_4/`; the two 64 × 64 resource
icons ship directly from the image run's own nearest-neighbour output (see the
last section).

A fifth asset (a cloth ribbon banner) was generated and rejected as too generic;
the objective plate is drawn in code instead.

## medallion_ring.png (1024 × 1024), v2 (the shipped source)

The first ring (prompts below) had a band so thick, and so orange-copper, that
icons shrank to about 15 px in a 50 px socket. Claude rejected it in review and
commissioned this thinner, darker version, generated with the first ring
supplied as a reference:

```text
Use your built-in image generation tool (real image generation, not code drawing). Look first at ref_ring_v1.png (a ring you made earlier), ref_card_frame.png and ref_pocket_watch.png in this directory.

Create medallion_ring_v2.png (1024x1024): a single circular ornamental bezel ring seen perfectly face-on, a refinement of ref_ring_v1.png with these changes:
- MUCH THINNER band: the inner opening diameter must be about 78% of the outer diameter (the metal band is only about 11% of the outer diameter on each side). Outer diameter about 760 px, centered.
- Colour: darker, desaturated ANTIQUE BRONZE like the dark bronze rails of ref_card_frame.png: deep umber-brown metal with restrained warm gold highlights only on the upper-left rim and on the fine milled inner edge; lower-right in deep shadow. Not orange, not copper, not shiny new brass.
- Keep: a fine milled/knurled inner edge, a thin raised outer lip, four tiny engraved diamond studs at 12/3/6/9 o'clock sitting on the band, slight hand-painted wear.
- The entire inside opening and the whole background outside the ring must be one perfectly flat chroma green #00FF00, no shadow, no glass, no dial, no text, no green light on the metal. Nothing touches the image edges.
Save it in this directory as medallion_ring_v2.png and report its size.
```

### v1 (superseded)

Match that slightly worn hand-painted game-asset finish, not a photographic or glossy 3D render.

The ENTIRE inside opening must be one absolutely flat untextured #00FF00 chroma-green disk, exactly matching the outside background. No objects whatsoever in the opening, no dark center, no face, no glass, no dial, no plate. Fine milled inner edge belongs only to the metal band. ```text

Generate one 1536x768 wide PNG. The entire background is uniform PURE BLACK RGB(0,0,0), no texture.
One centered wide flat elliptical pool of WHITE wet-brush ink wash viewed from a low three-quarter angle, like a soft ground shadow beneath a standing figure. Its visible width is about 3 TIMES its height, approximately 1100 pixels wide by 365 pixels high within the requested canvas. Generous completely black empty margin all around, at least 130 px left/right and 160 px top/bottom, including ALL splatter.
Hand-painted brushwork based on ref_brush.png's broken dry-brush silhouette: irregular soft feathered rim with a few restrained thin dry-brush streaks and tiny white splatter flecks at the perimeter. Preserve this smoother near-symmetric variant A, broad wet-brush strokes, feathered soft edges, restrained dry-brush rim and tiny white splatter flecks. ## Resource icons: skill_point.png and moltshard.png (64 × 64)

The character menu's Skill Points and Moltshards chips had no icons; the icon
identity policy requires purpose-built ones. The image run generated large
chroma-key sources (`skill_point_source.png`, `moltshard_source.png`) and
nearest-neighbour 64 × 64 finals, which ship unchanged as
`assets/art/icons/skill_point.png` and `assets/art/icons/moltshard.png`
(registered as `resource:*` concepts in `tests/test_icon_identity_policy.py`).
They were shown the existing ember, defiance, empower, time and health icons
as style references.

```text
Use your built-in image generation tool (real image generation, not code drawing). First look at ref_icon_sheet.png and the ref_icon_*.png files in this directory: they are the game's existing 64x64 pixel-art resource icons (ember, defiance, empower, time, health) for a dark-fantasy dungeon deckbuilder. Match that exact style: chunky readable pixel art, dark outline, warm torch-lit shading, a strong single silhouette that reads at 20 px, no text.

Create two NEW, purpose-built icons with silhouettes clearly different from each other and from every reference icon:
1. skill_point.png: the "Skill Point" resource (spent to learn skills on a skill tree). A small upright four-pointed star-sigil / compass-rose of pale burnished gold set inside a thin open circle of engraved gold, with a tiny warm glow at its centre. NOT a flame, NOT a shield, NOT a crown.
2. moltshard.png: the "Moltshard" resource (a shed fragment of a dragon's scale, violet Umbra-touched). A single jagged, curved, iridescent dragon-scale shard, deep violet to lilac with a pale lavender edge highlight and a faint violet inner glow. NOT a gem with facets like a diamond, NOT a feather.

For each: generate at a large size on a perfectly flat chroma green #00FF00 background with the subject centred and filling about 70% of the canvas and nothing touching the edges, then ALSO produce the final 64x64 RGBA PNG yourself by downscaling with nearest-neighbour onto transparency (key out the green, no green fringe). Save the large sources as skill_point_source.png and moltshard_source.png and the 64x64 finals as skill_point.png and moltshard.png in this directory. Report all sizes.
```
