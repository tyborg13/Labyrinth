## Raw-output checks and limitations

All 30 requested PNGs are present, readable, and byte-identical to their selected raw tool outputs. Original reference images were never edited, moved, or deleted.

The tool was prompted for the largest supported size and closest aspect ratio for every image; the actual returned dimensions are recorded above. Final native sizes are reduction targets, not the saved raw dimensions.

The generated backgrounds contain colour variation rather than exact uniform RGB values. Some shin material/cuff transitions also remain lower than the requested top-quarter boundary. These raw generative outputs have not been normalized or externally corrected.
