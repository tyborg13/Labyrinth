# mq-03 — round 2 (director review of lancer r3 renders)

Big step up: real anatomy, cloth cloak with natural folds, collar that wraps, fit gate PASS (IoU 0.804). The pieces
now need to read like the source's armour (front clay vs `.../refs/03_identity_lancer_current.png`). Rule for every
armour piece: it hugs the body (Shrinkwrap offset ≤ 0.015, conforming), nothing floats in front of the body, edges
stay sharp.
1. Face: no human face may show. Fill the hood opening with a dark face plate set slightly back inside the hood, with
   a narrow vertical slit groove and a short cross-bar (the source's glowing cross); the slit becomes the accent.
2. Hood: tip ~40% lower than now, wider base flowing into the collar; seen from the rear it must not read as a tall
   wizard hat.
3. Breastplate: conforms to the chest from the collarbones to the belt, centre ridge, slight taper at the waist (not a
   floating hexagon). Gem: 4-sided diamond, centred mid-chest, slightly proud, ~1.3× the current size.
4. Pauldrons: clearly visible 3-lame stacks capping each shoulder, overlapping the collar edge, reaching mid upper-arm;
   pointed trapezoid outline, each lame slightly smaller and lower; the top lame broadest.
5. Belt + strap conform to the waist (no floating torus ring); strap runs diagonally from the right hip.
6. Tabard: two front panels from the belt to just above the knees, each about one thigh wide, ragged hem — not shorts.
   Tassets (2 lames) over the hips, short and conforming.
7. Legs: cuisses conform to the thighs; poleyns are proud, pointed diamond plates on the knees (like the source);
   greaves wrap the shins; sabatons are 3 overlapping lames over the boots; the feet must look like armoured boots
   (no spikes).
8. Arms: vambraces conform to the forearms; gauntlets with flared cuffs; the near fist rests at the hip.
9. Spear head: much bigger crystal — ~22% of the spear length, widest ≈ 3× shaft diameter, 5 sides, irregular jags,
   continuing the shaft.
10. Keep: base body, cloak cloth (also keep the far-side cloak visible behind the spear arm), collar, stance, fit gate.
Iterate through the bridge; deliver front/rear parts + clay, overlay, gallery. Do not commit.
