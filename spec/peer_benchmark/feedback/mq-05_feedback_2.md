# mq-05 feedback 2: sculpted, organic forms (director review of the clay sheet)

Fit, gates, delivery and curation pass; keep all of that. The **form quality** is below batch A's bar. The owner has
rejected "toy soldier" shapes, and the paint-over follows the clay's forms, so these need real modelling detail.
Compare against batch A (Seer, Herald, Thrall, Gloam) and the Lancer in `roster_contact_sheet.webp`, and match their
level of surface relief.

## Ember Tick
- **Legs** (the biggest problem): each leg is a straight cylinder with a cone tip, like a pin. Make each leg a jointed
  arthropod leg:
  - a thick coxa/femur that rises from the body and bends at a clear knee;
  - a tapering tibia curving down to a small hooked foot that touches the ground;
  - slight curvature, and a few small spines or bumps along each segment.
  
  Legs splay outward and down. The near legs are larger in perspective. Keep `_upper`/`_lower` split at the knee.
- **Shell:** the dome needs sculpted relief:
  - deep, wide crack grooves where the ember cracks are painted;
  - a slightly flattened, plated top with a rim where the shell meets the body;
  - surface pitting. No smooth ball.
- **Head:** a small capitulum with two short palps and mouthparts, not a smooth bulb.

## Burrow Grub
- **Body:** give the body a gentle curl along the diagonal, not a rigid straight tube. Each segment needs:
  - slightly irregular, asymmetric volume;
  - wrinkled skin folds in the creases between segments;
  - fine bumps and dimples (displacement), and a soft underside that's flatter than the back.
- **Legs:** short, hooked, segmented claws (2 small segments with a curved hook), not smooth spikes.
- **Maw:** a fleshy lipped ring with irregular inward-curving teeth.

## Glacial Mirror
- **Boulders:** irregular, chipped stones with fractured planes, eroded edges and surface displacement, at varied
  sizes. Not smooth low-poly blobs with flat shading facets. Use real bevel, fracture and displacement modifiers, then
  apply them.
- **Mirror:** set the ice mirror slab into the chest as a distinct crystal inset with a chipped stone frame around it
  (`torso` owns it).
- **Defect:** in the rear clay a foot appears as a separate thin disc floating under the body. Every part must read as
  attached: the feet are boulders under the shins, touching the ground.
