## Change (exact values; match the mock)
1. **Defense plates.** Block, then Stoneskin, attached to the right end of the HP bar frame: 2 px after the frame's
   right cap, 3 px apart, vertically centred on the frame.
   - Plate: 24 px tall; width = 4 + 20 (icon) + 3 + number width + 9.
   - Rounded corners, radius 5. Fill `UiPalette.INK_1` at 0.93 alpha. 2 px border, plus a 1 px inner top highlight
     in the border colour at 0.35 alpha.
   - Border: `UiPalette.STEEL` for Block. The existing Earth element accent (`ElementData.accent(EARTH)`) for
     Stoneskin.
   - Icon: the painted icon (`block`, `stoneskin`) at full colour, trimmed to its opaque bounds, fit into 20 px.
   - Number: `UiTypography.heavy_ui_font()` at 17 px, `UiPalette.TEXT`, 2 px `UiPalette.TEXT_OUTLINE` outline.
   - The spent/negative state the tags have today (the `spent` red) keeps its meaning. Use `UiPalette.DANGER` for the
     border with the same layout.
2. **Status pills.** One row directly under the HP bar frame: left edge at the frame's left edge, top 2 px below the
   frame, 4 px apart.
   - Pill: 22 px tall, fully rounded (radius 11). Fill `UiPalette.INK_1` at 0.93 alpha, 2 px border in the class
     colour.
   - Icon: the painted icon at full colour, trimmed, fit into 18 px, at x + 3.
   - Label at x + 24, then 4 px right padding. A counted status shows its count in `heavy_ui_font` 15 px. A
     no-count status shows its word in `heavy_ui_font` 14 px. Both use `TEXT` with a 1 px `TEXT_OUTLINE` outline.
   - Classes, in this order, and their border colours:

     | Class | Statuses | Border | Label |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | can't act normally | Freeze, Petrify, Shock, Immobilize | `UiPalette.EMBER` | Frozen, Petrified, Shocked, Immobilized |
     | vulnerable | Chilled, Expose, Bleed | `UiPalette.DANGER_BRIGHT` | "Chilled"; Expose and Bleed show the count |
     | buff | Retaliate, Quicken, Next Attack, Truesight, Crystal Mantle, and the other player keyword badges | `UiPalette.GOLD_BRIGHT` | the count if any (Truesight keeps "∞"), else icon only |
     | trait | enemy mechanic badges (Reflect, Dive, Capacitor charge, Veil, Burrow, Burst, wind/Anchored) | `UiPalette.TEXT_2` | the count if any, else icon only |

   - Within a class, keep today's order.
   - Predicted statuses ("after this action: Frozen/Chilled/Shocked") draw as the same pill at 0.7 alpha, with the
     label prefixed by "→ ".
   - Each pill wraps to a second row if the row would run past the plates' right end.
3. **Tooltips:** every plate and pill registers its existing tooltip over its whole rect.
4. **Collision:** plates and pills join the board HUD's collision geometry (the one fb-01's callouts and the intent
   popup use), so callouts, intent popups and threat markers avoid them. The turn-order number keeps its place left
   of the bar.
5. **Remove** the old circles, count chips and floating tags. Keep `CapacitorView.draw_pips` as it is.
