# rr-17 — Storm Capacitor cutout rig (roster wave 1)

## Header
- Roster wave 1. The Storm Capacitor skips the maquette and the paint-over (decisions 2026-10-17 batch B): its body is
  rotationally symmetric, so the current painting `assets/art/enemies/storm_capacitor.png` is correct from every
  angle. It is rigged straight from that painting.
- Pipeline: the rr-05 one the owner approved on the Bloomer and Wisp, as rr-12 used it for the Grub and Tick: rigid
  parts, hidden fills, the stepped pixel-snapped presentation, native agreement with tie handling. The humanoid v2
  spec does NOT apply.
- Protected director data, imported byte for byte and never edited (`spec/peer_benchmark/staged/rr-17/`):
  - `motion_tracks.json`: clips, keys, contacts, intents and the channel semantics in its `_doc`;
  - `cap_mask.png`: the cut (opaque = cap), made by `make_cap_mask.py` from the painting;
  - `vent_fill.png`: the painted hidden fill under the cap, made by `make_vent_fill.py` from the painting.
  - Unclear semantics → `TODO_DESIGN_<what>`, and report it.
- Owned:
  - new `experiments/cutouts/storm_capacitor/**`;
  - production `assets/units/storm_capacitor_cutout/`;
  - the Capacitor's board, pre-battle and portrait registration, density, rest and shadow records;
  - minimal runtime support for the two new channel kinds below, used by no other creature;
  - tests.
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark`. Other creatures stay byte-identical in rendering.

## Change
1. **Pieces** (all rigid; one view):

   | Piece | Pixels | Parent | Pivot |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `column` | every opaque painting pixel not in `cap_mask.png` (plinth, glass, arcs, all three bands) | `body` | — |
   | `cap` | the opaque pixels of `cap_mask.png` (dome and spike) | `column` | the dome's bottom centre, (131, 75) |

   - `vent_fill.png` is the column's hidden fill under the cap. It is drawn beneath the column's own pixels and beneath
     the cap, so it shows only where a lifted or tipped cap uncovers it. Rest identity is 0 px against the painting.
   - The rear facing shows the same front rig. Never mirror it.
2. **Channels** (beyond the rr-05 ones):
   - `cap.lift_px`, `cap.lean_deg` (rotation about the cap pivot; positive tips its top toward the facing's forward;
     1.5° steps as every rigid sprite) and `body.forward_px` (the whole actor).
   - `column.glow`: an integer light level for the arcs and the vent glow, changed only by hold keys. The arc pixels are
     the column's and the vent fill's pixels with B − R > 20 and B − G > 40 (8-bit RGB). Each level recolours only
     those pixels, alpha unchanged, rounded per channel:

     | Level | Meaning | Colour |
     |---|---|---|
     | 2 | flare | lerp(c, #F2E6FF, 0.70) |
     | 1 | bright | lerp(c, #F2E6FF, 0.35) |
     | 0 | as painted | c |
     | −1 | dim | lerp(c, #2A1218, 0.55) |
     | −2 | dead | lerp(c, #2A1218, 0.90) |

     Pre-bake the five column textures (vent fill included) and swap them; no shader resampling.
   - `hold` interpolation: the previous value holds until the key's phase, then jumps (the existing
     `scripts/maquette_cutout_runtime/motion.gd` meaning).
3. **Production.** Replace the static board art with the rig. Map intents exactly as each clip's `intents` lists:

   | Intent | Clip |
   |---|---|
   | `charge` (gain_charge) | `charge` |
   | `discharge` (chain_discharge, then reset_charge) | `discharge` |
   | death | `death`, then the existing fade |

   - The Capacitor is immobile: no move clip. Hit plays `hit`.
   - The existing charge and chain-lightning VFX and sounds sync at each clip's `contact`.
   - Board actors keep their per-actor idle phase seed, so neighbouring capacitors never buzz in step.
   - Pre-battle and portraits use the same renderer. Everything plays through the rr-05 stepped, pixel-snapped
     presentation (idle at 10 fps holds).
4. **Gates.** The rr-12 set:
   - bone-level signed motion at authored keys and midpoints;
   - production capture from real clip progression, ≥ 6 changing frames per action, contact included;
   - zero shimmer residual on held frames;
   - native agreement with exact-tie handling;
   - the stray-pixel gate (no opaque component of ≤ 8 px detached from the silhouette in any presented frame);
   - mutation regressions.

   Plus:
   - glow levels: every presented frame's arc pixels equal the table's colour for the level the clip holds at that
     phase, and no non-arc pixel changes with the level;
   - the dark-slab gate skips the vent fill (it is painted dark on purpose) and applies everywhere else.

## Proof
- Headless: focused tests, gates and mutations, the three SFX sync programs and `tests.test_test_registration_audit`.
  Don't run the full Godot or Python suites; the director runs them at merge.
- Windowed, run by the director: the exact command sequence for native rest, render, verify, motion gates,
  production capture and the real-time review MP4.
  - MP4 format (rr-04): the Capacitor centred, 2× nearest-neighbour, #1E1E24 background, clip name at top centre.
  - Clips: idle ×3, charge ×2, discharge ×2, hit ×2, death once plus a 1 s hold.
  - Plus a contact sheet of every presented frame per clip at 3×.

## Out of scope
- Any other creature. No image generation. No changes to the charge rules or the chain-lightning VFX.
