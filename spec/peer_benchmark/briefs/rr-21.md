## Change
1. **Cut (spec §1):** `head` (the hood with the face and the green eye, rigid); `torso` with the
   leaf mantle and its thorns fused in (rigid); `pelvis`; `tatters`, the leaf fringe, as one skinned cloth chain on the
   pelvis (`tatters_mid`, `tatters_tip`; at most 4 px of follow-through, settled by the end of each clip); per arm one
   skinned root limb over upper and fore (forearm thorns included) and a rigid hooked claw `hand_<side>`; per leg one
   skinned root leg and a rigid clawed foot. Feature: the green eye, owned by `head` (front only).
   - Hands and feet are two-bone targets; elbows and knees keep their rest bend side.
   - Knees bend toward the facing (§3). Declare the knee policy per leg and view in the case anatomy.
   - `foot_stride` is the camera-side long leg in each view and `foot_trailing` the other.
   - Rest identity is 0 px against both paintings (source space). The production rest is the density-processed rest.
2. **Idle:** the §2 bob. The upper body is torso, head and both arms (all free arms); the lower body stays still.
3. **Production.** Replace the static Briar Weaver on the board, pre-battle and portraits in both views:

   | Intent | Clip |
