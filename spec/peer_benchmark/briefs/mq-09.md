# mq-09 — Fitted Blender maquettes for roster wave 1, batch C (Hollow Mimic, Briar Weaver, Slag Mortar)

## Header
- Roster wave 1, batch C: the last three new enemies still drawn as static images. Batch A (mq-04), batch B (mq-05),
  the true-turn rear (mq-06) and the Thrall r2 (mq-07) are merged; use them as the reference for the builder, gates,
  delivery and curation.
- **Pipeline (unchanged).**
  1. The maquette fits the approved art: `spec/art_identity/jobs/<id>/front/processed.png` and `rear/processed.png`.
  2. The director paints over its clays and part renders.
  3. The rig is cut along its parts on humanoid rig v2 (`spec/peer_benchmark/humanoid_cutout_rig.md`).

  No image generation in this unit.
- All three are humanoids, so the rules learned since batch A apply from the start:
  - **Shoulders at the real shoulder** (spec §3): level in 3D, and in the front `joints_px.json` the far shoulder sits
    0.15 × |x_near − x_far| above the near one, ±10 px. Batch A and B missed this; don't.
  - **True 180° rear** (mq-06): the rear is the same posed body turned 180°, both feet planted. Never a mirror.
  - **3/4 facing** (owner on the Lancer): the chest faces the board's 3/4 direction, not the camera. The mq-04
    facing and torso-asymmetry checks must pass.
  - **Quality bar**: the Lancer r3 and the mq-07 Thrall. Cloth hoods, mantles and aprons are real cloth or sculpted
    folds; real anatomy; no primitive cones or cylinders for cloth or limbs.
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark`. Owned:
  - `spec/art_identity/maquettes/{hollow_mimic,briar_weaver,slag_mortar}/**`;
  - builder additions under `spec/art_identity/maquettes/blender/**`, only where a figure needs something the builder
    lacks: generic, tested, and every existing figure's compiled spec and delivery unchanged;
  - tests.
- Blender runs only through the bridge (`spec/art_identity/maquettes/blender/bridge_client.py`). The director runs the
  bridge for this worktree. If a job gets no answer in 120 s, stop and report. Jobs set their own `timeout_s`.

## Part lists (director, exact labels; `_near`/`_far` are body sides, as in batch A)
Every figure has the 15 humanoid labels: `head`, `torso`, `pelvis`, `upper_near`, `fore_near`, `hand_near`,
`upper_far`, `fore_far`, `hand_far`, `thigh_near`, `shin_near`, `foot_near`, `thigh_far`, `shin_far`, `foot_far`.
Extra labels and features below.
- **hollow_mimic**: a shadow copy of the hero. A lean figure in a dark, segmented, faintly glossy bodysuit, with the
  hero's spiky hair as a black silhouette and a pale cracked mask for a face. No weapon, no cape.
  - The head is the hair and the mask together.
  - Feature: the mask's violet crack is one feature owned by `head`. It's front only; the rear shows at most the
    mask's edge.
  - Proportions follow the painting (they copy the hero's build). Hands are open, clawless gloves.
- **briar_weaver**: a hunched bramble creature. A ragged bark-and-leaf hood over a dark face with one green eye; a
  shaggy mantle of torn leaves with thorn spikes over the shoulders and back; long, gnarled root arms ending in big
  hooked-claw hands; thick twisted root legs on clawed root feet.
  - The mantle and its thorns fuse into `torso` (spec §1). Forearm thorns belong to `fore_<side>`.
  - Extra label: `tatters`, the leaf fringe hanging below the mantle at the waist, as cloth on `pelvis`.
  - Feature: the green eye is one feature owned by `head`, front only.
  - The hunch is in the pose (spine and neck bent forward), not a shorter torso.
- **slag_mortar**: a hulking brute with an iron-banded crucible of glowing slag strapped high on its back. Leather
  harness straps, a ragged leather apron, heavy iron wrist bracers, bare thick arms, big fists, wrapped shins.
  - The harness straps fuse into `torso`. The bracers belong to `fore_<side>`.
  - Extra labels: `mortar`, the crucible with its iron bands, a rigid held item hung from `torso` at the harness
    between the shoulder blades (that point is its pivot); and `apron`, cloth on `pelvis`.
  - Feature: the molten slag surface is one feature owned by `mortar`. It shows in both views, because the opening
    faces up and back.
  - The mortar must not hide the head in the front view. In the rear it covers the upper back and the back of the head,
    as the painting shows.

## Change
1. **One Blender-native spec per figure**, from real geometry on the CC0 humanoid armature, fitted to the 3/4 board
   facing with the facing check passing. Proportions follow the paintings.
2. **Fit gate** against the approved processed art: IoU ≥ 0.70 front and ≥ 0.65 rear.
   - Every label present in each view where the painting shows it, or an explicit occlusion.
   - Per-part coverage, including each arm and leg part. No limb vanishes into the body.
3. **Rules gates**: the shoulder rule (front joints) and the true-turn regression, both views.
4. **Delivery per figure, in the mq-04 curated format:**
   - `delivery/` with front/rear clays (1024), part maps, solos, `joints_px.json`, manifests and the fit overlay;
   - `COMMIT_MANIFEST.txt` (≤ 6 MB per figure; no `.blend`, caches, candidates or determinism copies);
   - `discarded_proof_sha256.json`;
   - a fresh regeneration that matches clays and part maps.
5. Determinism, cache replay, ownership and mutation gates, and the hash-bound delivery validator, all as mq-04.

## Proof

- One contact sheet: the three fit overlays, plus a clay comparison against the Lancer, the Thrall and batch B at the

- Focused maquette, delivery and art-identity tests, and the regeneration checks for every figure a builder change
