## Director measurements (front `delivery/joints_px.json`, 1024 px)
The stance is a splayed straddle:
- feet 336 px apart (ankles (730, 888) and (394, 752)) on hips only 158 px apart ((594, 612) and (436, 604));

## Change
All targets are in front `joints_px` at 1024 px. The rear is the same posed body turned 180° (the mq-06 rule).
1. **Stalking crouch.** Hips stay where they are.
   - Move the ankles to `ankle_near` (650, 900) ±12 and `ankle_far` (430, 790) ±12: about 220 px apart, the near foot a
     half step forward.
   - Re-solve both legs with their existing thigh and shin lengths. The knees bend forward, toward the facing (screen
     lower-left), never outward. Feet stay flat on the floor plane.
2. **A real hunch.**
   - Bend the spine and neck forward (the Briar Weaver's way, never a sideways roll) until `head` reaches (440, 330) ±10.
   - The shoulders move with the bend but keep the level-shoulder rule (far shoulder 0.15·|dx| above the near one,
     ±10).
   - Both arms keep their elbow bend and claw angle relative to their shoulder, as mq-12 did.
   - From behind, the hood must now sit at or below the top of the back.
3. **Flush shoulder plates.**
   - Scale the `carapace` plates to 60% and seat them flush on the shoulders and upper back, following the cloak, as the
     approved r1 painting shows (`spec/art_identity/jobs/gloam_stalker/mq12_front/refs/01_approved_painting_r1.png`).
   - No plate may extend more than 40 px beyond the shoulder silhouette in either view.
   - The `carapace` label stays; the rig will own it with the torso.
4. **Labels unchanged.** Everything else (hood, drapes, claws, legs' wraps) stays as it is.
