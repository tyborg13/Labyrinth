# rr-11 — Arc Thrall on humanoid rig v2

## Header
- The owner rejected the rr-07 Thrall rig (feedback.md, 2026-10-18). Quoted verbatim: "Right arm doesn't seem to connect
  high enough to match the left. Walk is unnatural with no knee bend. Idle is more of a ripple than a bob. Attack (I
  assume) swings the gun arm backwards so it makes no sense. Rear view shows the inside of the gun thing where it should
  be going into the arm. Front view just paints the hand over it, which makes no sense. Generally, these cutouts are not
  reaching our standard."
  - The maquette (mq-07) and the paintings (`jobs/arc_thrall/mq7_front`, `mq7_rear`) fixed the shoulder and the tube.
  - This unit rebuilds the rig on humanoid rig v2.
- **Binding spec:** `spec/peer_benchmark/humanoid_cutout_rig.md`. It includes knee-bend walking and the shoulder rule.
- **Reference implementation:** rr-10, the Hoarfrost Seer on v2.
  - Reuse its generic modules: `tools/humanoid_cutout/**` and `scripts/maquette_cutout_runtime/humanoid_*.gd`.
  - Reuse its gates: idle rigidity, clean pieces, the calibrated joint-seam hole and stray-pixel gates, the limits,
    signed motion, and the native checks on rigid sprites.
  - Reuse its tuck depths:
    - wrist 5 px, with rigid cuff rows;
    - foot 4 px under any cloth;
    - torso continuation under the shoulders and armpits 4 px;
    - sleeve inner edge 3 px.
- **Protected director data:** `spec/peer_benchmark/staged/rr-11/motion_tracks.json`. Import it byte for byte; unclear
  semantics → `TODO_DESIGN_<channel>`.
- **Inputs:**
  - the mq-07 maquette delivery: `spec/art_identity/maquettes/arc_thrall/delivery/`, front and rear, with the tube as
    `fore_near` and no `hand_near` or `club`;
  - the approved paintings: `jobs/arc_thrall/mq7_front` and `mq7_rear`.
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark` after rr-10 and mq-07 have merged.
- Owned:
  - `experiments/cutouts/arc_thrall/**`
  - `assets/units/arc_thrall_cutout/**`
  - the Thrall's registration, density and shadow records
  - tests
  - generic extensions to the rr-10 humanoid modules, listed and tested, without changing the Seer's output
- Keep every other creature byte-identical in rendering.

## Change
1. **Cut (spec §1, Thrall row):**

   | Piece | Source labels | Bones | Kind |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `head` | head | `head` | rigid |
   | `torso` | torso ∪ collar | `torso` | rigid; the collar ring and chain stub are part of the torso |
   | `upper_near` | upper_near | `upper_near` | rigid |
   | `tube` | fore_near | `fore_near` | rigid |
   | `sleeve_far` (bare arm) | upper_far ∪ fore_far | `upper_far`, `fore_far` | skinned |
   | `claw` | hand_far | `hand_far` | rigid |
   | legs | thigh ∪ shin, per side | `thigh_<side>`, `shin_<side>` | skinned |
   | feet | foot, per side | `foot_<side>` | rigid |
   | `loincloth` | loincloth | `pelvis` → `loincloth_tip` | skinned |

   - `upper_near`: its distal end tucks into the tube's mouth.
   - `tube`: the banded mouth covers the elbow. A target bone `tube_tip` sits at the cap, as a child of `fore_near`.
   - Legs: the feet are two-bone targets.
   - `pelvis` sits at the waist.
   - Rest identity is 0 px against both approved paintings.
2. **Runtime and motion.**
   - The rr-10 runtime, plus anything new that the data needs: `tube_tip` as the near arm's two-bone target, and
     `world_step` feet with knee bend.
   - Walking must show a clearly bent stance knee and swing knee (spec §3).
   - The idle weapon-arm lag is pre-computed in the data (`upper_near.lift_px`).
3. **Production.** Replace the rr-07 Thrall on the board, pre-battle and portraits in both views. Intents map as in
   rr-07:

   | Intent | Clips |
   |---|---|
   | `tether` | `tether` (the acting Thrall only) |
   | `arc_jab` | `move`, then `jab` |

   Contact sync is unchanged.
4. **Gates:** the rr-10 set, plus a knee-bend check. In every `move` frame where a foot is in swing, its knee angle
   differs from straight by at least 15°.
