## Change
1. **Re-cut from the new paintings.** Keep the rr-33 piece set (spec §1), with these changes:
   - **Carapace.** The torso owns every carapace plate pixel, in both views. No plate pixel may belong to an arm, the
     head or a cape. A plate must never move apart from the torso.
   - **Rests.** Each rest is 0 px against its painting (source space). The production rest is the density-processed
     rest.
2. **Front draw order.** The camera-side arm must draw in front of the body.
   - In the front view, `arm_near` and `hand_near` (the blade) draw above `torso`, `pelvis`, `cape_near` and `cape_far`
     in every frame.
   - In the rear view, the camera-side arm is `arm_far` / `hand_far`. It already draws above the body; keep that.
   - The head stays above both arms in both views.
   - The non-camera arm stays behind the torso in both views.
   - Give every piece whose cover changes the hidden coverage it needs, so no hole opens.
3. **Clips.** Import the motion data, including the new `slash` and its rear override. The intent mapping and the
   production wiring stay as in rr-33.
4. **Knees.** The r3 crouch bends the stance knees more. Re-declare `knee_policy` (including `crouched_stance_deg`) per
   leg and view from the new maquette. Report the measured stance bends.
5. **Gates.** Keep the rr-33 set in both views. Add three:
   - **No floating pieces:** in every native frame of every clip, in both views, the figure's alpha has no more
     8-connected components than its rest. Prove the gate fails on a mutation that detaches a carapace plate.
   - **Draw order:** a test that fails if the camera-side arm or blade drops below the torso or a cape, in either view.
   - **Slash reach:** at contact, the leading blade tip is at least 18 source px from its rest position in both views.
     This replaces the rr-33 slash, which barely moved.
