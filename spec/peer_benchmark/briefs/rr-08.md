## Change
1. **Solos through the bridge.** Render each maquette's front and rear part solos through the Blender bridge. They
   must be byte-identical to the mq-04 delivery.
2. **Rigs.** Build them the rr-04/rr-05 way:
   - ownership from the part renders;
   - hidden-material requests where parts overlap;
   - connected parts and seam underlays;
   - rest identity 0 px against the approved processed art, in both views.
   
   The approved paintings were registered to the maquette by the identity gate. If a part's painted pixels sit outside
   its label area, report it; never move paint.
3. **Feature masks** (rr-05 format). Each feature is owned by one part, includes a 1 px rim, and has its holes filled.
   - **Herald:**
     - the brass mask (front) is one feature owned by `head` (rear: occluded);
     - each banner's insignia is a feature owned by its banner part, in both views.
   - **Gloam:** the masklike face with its violet eye holes (front) is one feature owned by `head` (rear: occluded).
   
   Report each mask's pixel count with a zoomed outline crop. If a mask is ambiguous, stop with TODO_DESIGN.
4. **Production.** Replace both static board arts with the rigs and map intents exactly as each clip's `intents`
   lists:

   | Creature | Intent | Clips |
   |---|---|---|
   | Herald | `call_the_wind` | `call` |
   | Herald | `gust_bolt` | `bolt` |
   | Herald | `still_air` | `still` |
   | Herald | repositioning | `move` |
   | Gloam | `fade` | `fade` (the blink happens at contact) |
   | Gloam | `ambush` | `ambush` |
   | Gloam | `slash` | `move`, then `slash` |

   - Sound and VFX sync at each clip's `contact`.
   - Pre-battle and portraits use the same renderer.
   - Play everything through the rr-05 stepped, pixel-snapped presentation.
5. **Gates.** Apply the rr-04/rr-05 gates as they are, for both creatures:
   - bone-level signed motion at authored keys and midpoints, both views;
   - production capture from real clip progression, with ≥ 6 changing frames per action including contact;
   - feature integrity;
   - zero shimmer residual on held frames;
   - the dark-slab gate on exposed hidden material;
   - native agreement with exact-tie handling;
   - mutation regressions.
