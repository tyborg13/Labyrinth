4. **Production.** Replace the Lancer's static board art with the rig. Map intents to clips exactly as each clip's
   `intents` lists:
   - `glass_lunge`: move, then lunge (contact 0.48);
   - `spear_cast`: cast;

   Pre-battle and portraits use the same renderer (int-02 routing). Play everything through the rr-05 stepped,

5. **Gates.** Apply the rr-04/rr-05 gates as they are:
   - bone-level signed motion at authored keys and midpoints, both views;
   - production capture from real clip progression, with ≥ 6 changing frames per action including contact;
   - feature integrity;
   - zero shimmer residual on held frames;
   - mutation regressions.
