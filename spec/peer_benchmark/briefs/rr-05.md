# rr-05 — Maquette rig polish: no shimmer, no split features (owner, Shale Bloomer review)

## Header
- Owner (review inbox, 2026-10-17) on the Shale Bloomer 60 fps video: right direction, details need iteration —
  (a) "the idle still has some of that shimmer effect I've been asking to avoid", (b) "wrong breakages from the cutout
  ... the top gem breaking in half during a number of the animations."
- Director diagnosis: (a) small continuous rotations/offsets of pixel-art parts resample every frame → pixel crawl;
  (b) the crown's amber gem pixels are owned partly by `core` and partly by petal parts, so petal motion tears it.
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark` (rr-04 + int-02 merged). Owned: `scripts/maquette_cutout_runtime/**`, the generic
  compiler/gates under tools, `experiments/cutouts/shale_bloomer/**`, `experiments/cutouts/lightning_wisp/**`,
  production assets for both, tests. Do not touch the shrike.

## Change (director decisions)
1. **Feature integrity:** declare per creature a list of named features that must stay whole (bloomer: the crown gem
   and each amber nodule; wisp: the eye) as masks on the approved art. Each feature is owned by exactly one part in both
   views; the parts that overlap it render behind it. Re-derive ownership/hidden fills accordingly. Add a gate: for
   every sampled pose, each feature's pixels move rigidly with one bone (fail on any split), with a mutation test.
2. **No shimmer — stepped, pixel-snapped presentation for idle (and any looping clip):**
   - Idle poses advance on a 10 fps step clock (each sampled pose held 6 frames at 60 fps; deterministic phase).
     Action clips (move, attacks, hit, death) keep full rate.
   - Part translations snap to whole source pixels at render; rotations quantise to 1.5° steps; the composed rig is
     drawn pixel-aligned to the board grid (no half-pixel positions).
   - A shimmer gate: over one idle loop, count per-frame changed pixels inside each part that are NOT explained by that
     part's rigid snapped transform (resampling noise); it must be zero for held frames and below a small threshold at
     step boundaries (choose and report the metric; show before/after numbers).
3. Apply to both creatures; keep the director motion data unchanged (amplitudes stay; only the presentation steps).
4. Regenerate both real-time review videos (tools review MP4s) and production captures.
