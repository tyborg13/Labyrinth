## Change
1. **Cut (spec §1), following the motion data's `pieces_note`.**
   - The torso owns every pauldron pixel and the chest crystal; no pauldron pixel may belong to an arm. The pelvis
     owns the belt and pouches.
   - The tabard is a cloth chain on the pelvis; the two cloak halves are cloth chains on the torso.
   - Each arm is a skinned root limb with the gauntlet as a rigid hand. Each leg is a skinned root leg with a rigid
     boot.
   - The spear (shaft and crystal head) is one rigid held item in `hand_far`, pivoting at the grip and planted at
     rest.
   - Features: the cyan visor belongs to `head` (front only); the chest crystal belongs to `torso` (front only).
   - Rests are 0 px against both paintings in source space. The production rest is the density-processed rest.
2. **Draw order.** The camera-side arm draws above the torso, pelvis and both cloak halves in every frame: `arm_near`
   in the front and `arm_far` in the rear. The head stays above both arms. The spear's crystal head is never hidden
   behind the body at the contact of `lunge`, `jab`, `cast` or `pin`, in either view.
3. **Production.** Replace the static Lancer on the board, pre-battle and portraits, in both views:

   | Intent | Clip |
   |---|---|
   | `glass_lunge` (move_toward + aoe) | `move` for the steps, then `lunge`; the existing aoe hit at its contact |
   | `spear_advance` (move_toward + melee) | `move`, then `jab`; the existing melee hit at its contact |
   | `spear_cast` (ranged) | `cast`; the existing projectile at its contact |
   | `frost_pin` (ranged, ice) | `pin`; the existing projectile at its contact |
   | `refract_guard` (move_away + block) | `move`, then `guard`; the existing block at its contact |
   | hit / death | `hit` / `death`, then the existing fade |

   - `move` plays for every movement step, whatever the intent.
   - Remove `frostglass_lancer` from the static roster lists, the data-driven way rr-17/rr-19 settled. No new
     creature-name branches in shared code.
4. **Knees:** declare `knee_policy` per leg and view from the maquette, and report the measured stance bends.
5. **Gates.** The rr-35 set in both views:
   - idle rigidity, clean pieces, seams and stray pixels;
   - signed motion and action direction, with coverage for every action clip;
   - the knee and minimum-reach gates on every `move` swing, and supports;
   - the dense reach gate (overreach reported, not failed);
   - native checks: no more 8-connected alpha components than rest, in every frame;
   - draw order;
   - the spear stays rigid and attached at the grip in every frame until `release_at`.

   Also generalize the rr-36 native-defect checks to this case: floor shards from gait continuations, cloth fringes
   or foreign texels in hidden coverage, outline bands revealed under a hem, seam lines across one cloth surface, and
   fraying hems. Each must fail on a constructed bad frame. They are diagnostics: the director's 2× judgment of the
   native clips is acceptance.
