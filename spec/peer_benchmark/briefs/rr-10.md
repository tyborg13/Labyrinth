## Change
1. **Cut (spec §1).** Build these Seer pieces from the painting's ownership, using the maquette solos:

   | Piece | Source labels | Bones | Kind |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `head` | head | `head` | rigid |
   | `torso` | torso ∪ collar | `torso` | rigid; it includes the rope belt |
   | `sleeve_near`, `sleeve_far` | upper ∪ fore, per side | `upper_<side>`, `fore_<side>` | skinned |
   | `hand_near`, `hand_far` | hand | `hand_<side>` | rigid |
   | `staff` | staff_shaft ∪ staff_crystal | `staff` (child of `hand_far` at the grip) | rigid; the ice crystal stays one feature |
   | `robe` | skirt_near ∪ skirt_far | `pelvis` + `hem_near`, `hem_far` | skinned |
   | `foot_near`, `foot_far` | foot | children of `hem_<side>` | rigid |

   - `pelvis` sits at the belt, as a child of `root`. Place `hem_near` and `hem_far` at the robe's lower corners on the
     floor line.
   - Rest identity against the approved front painting: 0 px.
2. **Skinned meshes** go through the existing `joint_meshes` path (2 px vertex grid, as the Acolyte's), with these
   weights:
   - **Robe:**
     - the top 4 px of rows are 100% `pelvis`, and the hem rows are 100% hem;
     - in between, a linear blend top to bottom;
     - the hem weight splits near/far by x, with a smoothstep blend across the middle third of the robe width.
   - **Sleeves:** weight by projection onto the `upper` and `fore` segments, with a ±4 px smoothstep blend centred on
     the elbow.
   - If a weight rule visibly tears or folds at a key pose, report it with crops. Don't invent another.
3. **Tucks and fills (spec §1).**
   - At least 3 px of painted continuation, hidden at rest, under every sliding edge:
     - the robe top under the torso;
     - the shoulder tops under the collar;
     - the wrists under the cuffs;
     - the hood's lower edge under the collar.
   - Revealed areas (the torso behind a raised arm, the robe behind the staff) use the rr-05 hidden-fill machinery and
     the dark-slab gate.
4. **Runtime (spec §4).** Selected by `"rig": "humanoid_v2"` in compiled motion:
   - source-pixel units;
   - `inward_px`;
   - world-relative `angle_deg` for held items;
   - `release_at` (the item pivots about its floor end after release);
   - `world_step` feet and hem anchors;
   - two-bone hand targets with the elbow on its rest bend side;
   - forward kinematics for arms without hand keys.
   
   It plays through the rr-05 presentation: idle at 10 fps holds and whole pixels; actions at full rate; rigid sprites
   in whole pixels and 1.5° steps; skinned meshes driven by the snapped bone transforms.
5. **Production.** The v2 Seer replaces the rr-07 Seer on the board, pre-battle and portraits. Intents map as rr-07:

   | Intent | Clips |
   |---|---|
   | `icefall` | `mark` |
   | `icefall_land` | `land` |
   | `rime_bolt` | `bolt` |
   | `withdraw` | `move` (away), then `ward` |

   - Sound and VFX sync at each clip's `contact`.
   - The idle phase seed per actor stays.
6. **Gates.** These replace the rr-07 Seer gates that assume rigid-only pieces:
   - **Idle rigidity:** in every presented idle frame, each visible pixel equals its group's rest pixel shifted by that
     group's whole-pixel offset. Mismatches: 0. There are exactly the three spec groups (upper body, still staff arm,
     still lower body).
   - **Clean pieces:** in every presented frame of every clip, no piece shows pixels outside its own silhouette and
     tuck, and the tuck stays covered. Revealed fill passes the dark-slab gate. No new interior hole larger than 1 px
     appears inside the union silhouette.
   - **Limits** from the sampled poses:
     - torso lean ≤ 4°, head ≤ 6° (death: 25° and 20°);
     - the staff ≤ 35° from rest (death excepted);
     - feet and hems planted outside move and death;
     - no rotation or scale on any bone in idle.
   - **Signed motion:** bone-level signed motion at authored keys and midpoints, both views (existing gate).
   - **rr-05 checks on rigid sprites:** feature integrity and native agreement stay. Skinned meshes are exempt from
     the CPU raster oracle; don't build one for them.
   - **Mutations:** each gate fails on a planted regression, for example:
     - a 1° idle torso rotation;
     - a robe split back into rotating halves;
     - a 2 px tuck;
     - the staff turned 40°;
     - a hem moved in `ward`.
