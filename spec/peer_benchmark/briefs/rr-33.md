## Change
1. **Cut (spec §1):** `head` (the hood with the pale mask and violet eye, rigid); `torso` with the carapace plates fused in (rigid); `pelvis`; `cape_near` and `cape_far`, the ragged drapes, as skinned cloth chains on the torso (`cape_near_mid`, `cape_near_tip`, `cape_far_mid`, `cape_far_tip`; at most 4 px of follow-through, settled by clip end); per arm one skinned root limb over upper and fore, with the long blade-claw as the rigid hand (`hand_near`, `hand_far`; the maquette labels them `claw_near`, `claw_far`), following its forearm; per leg one skinned root leg and a rigid wrapped foot.
   - Hands and feet are two-bone targets; elbows and knees keep their rest bend side.
   - Knees bend toward the facing (§3). Declare the knee policy per leg and view in the case anatomy.
   - Rest identity is 0 px against both paintings (source space). The production rest is the density-processed rest.
   - Feature: the violet eye belongs to `head` (front only); the violet glints on the drapes belong to their chains.
2. **Idle:** the §2 bob, as the motion data keys it.
3. **Production.** Replace the static Gloam Stalker on the board, pre-battle and portraits in both views:

   | Intent | Clip |
   |---|---|
   | `fade` (veil_blink) | `fade`; the existing Blink/Veil effect starts at its contact |
   | `ambush` (melee) | `ambush`; the existing melee hit at its contact |
   | `slash` (move_toward + melee) | `move` for the steps, then `slash`; the existing melee hit at its contact |
   | hit / death | `hit` / `death`, then the existing fade |

   - `move` plays for every movement step, whatever the intent.
   - Remove `gloam_stalker` from the static roster lists, the data-driven way rr-17/rr-19 settled. No new creature-name
     branches in shared code.
4. **Gates:** the Thrall/Mirror/Weaver set in both views:
   - idle rigidity, clean pieces, seams and stray pixels;
   - signed motion and action direction, with coverage for every action clip;
   - the knee gate on every `move` swing, and supports;
   - the dense reach gate (overreach reported, not failed);
   - native checks.
   - each blade-claw stays rigid and attached to its forearm in every frame (no tear at the wrist).
