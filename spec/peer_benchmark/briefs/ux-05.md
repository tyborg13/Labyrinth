# ux-05 — Previews mark destruction instead of hiding it (owner playtest 9 note 2)

## Header
- Owner: "Some attack previews (aoe?) show enemies or destroyed terrain as gone instead of indicating that it will be
  killed/destroyed, which is very confusing."
- Triage (spec/peer_benchmark/triage_playtest9.md §2): lethal ENEMIES are already retained with an empty HP bar and the
  pulsing death mark (`combat_board_view.gd:9120`, `:9208`). Still disappearing: destroyed terrain/objects (e.g. a
  2-HP crate at HP 0 in the targetless-card confirmation of Whirlwind Slash / Cinderweave Guard / Immolation), triggered
  traps (removed from the array), lethal illusions (skipped at HP 0), and intermediate multi-action previews that show
  earlier simulated destruction; lethal forced-movement destination ghosts are omitted.
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark`. Owned: `scripts/run_scene.gd` preview display adapter regions (`:18517`–`:21519`,
  `:34444`), `scripts/combat_board_view.gd` destruction markers, tests. No card data or combat rule changes.

## Change (director's design)
1. One rule for every preview path: **nothing that exists now disappears during a preview.** Anything the preview
   destroys, kills, triggers or consumes stays drawn at its committed position with a marker.
2. Markers (reuse the existing visual language; one signal per concept):
   - Enemies: unchanged (empty HP bar + pulsing death mark).
   - Destructible terrain/objects (crates, kegs, walls, rubble piles, ice): the same pulsing death mark used for enemies,
     centred on the object, plus its HP bar shown empty if it has one. No new icon.
   - Illusions: treated exactly like enemies (retained, empty bar, death mark).
   - Traps that will trigger: stay drawn, with the existing trap-trigger highlight; a trap consumed by the trigger also
     gets the death mark.
   - Lethal forced movement: keep the destination ghost, drawn with the death mark (the original keeps its mark too).
3. Surfaces the preview consumes (e.g. Fire tiles detonated) keep showing until resolution; their blast is already
   shown by the AoE overlay — no extra marker.
4. Applies in limited and unrestricted Umbra sight alike (respect existing information-safety rules: never reveal
   something the player cannot currently see).

## Proof
- Regression tests that fail before / pass after for: the crate in Whirlwind Slash confirmation, a triggered trap,
  a lethal illusion, a lethal pull destination, and a two-action card whose first action destroys terrain.
- Real-renderer capture at 1920x1080 (director runs): list a probe command that shows each case with its marker.
- Full Godot suite PASS.
