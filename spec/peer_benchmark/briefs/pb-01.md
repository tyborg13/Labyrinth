## Change
1. **Objective text (director-authored, exact):** the prominent line is the heading `OBJECTIVE · <TITLE>` (titles
   unchanged: Defeat All Enemies / Defeat the Leader / Survive / Reach the Exit / Defeat <Name>). Under it, one short
   line at the existing secondary size, never wrapping at 1920x1080:
   - kill_all: "Defeat every enemy."
   - kill_leader: "Defeat the marked leader. The rest flee."
   - survive: "Survive until the marked time."
   - reach_exit: "Reach a marked door to escape."
   - dragon/Guardian: no second line (as today).
   Replace the shared descriptions in `combat_objective_rules.gd` with these lines so tooltips and logs match; keep the
   gold keyword tokens where these words are glossary terms.
2. **Living foes:** each foe on the pre-battle screen plays its board idle in place of the static texture, using the
   same renderer and idle data as the board (cutout renderer for the 32 rigged identities; legacy idle sheet only if
   an identity has no renderer), at the same scale, feet alignment, facing (front view), ink pool and badges as today.
   Each foe starts its idle at a different phase (deterministic per slot) so a pack does not breathe in unison.
   Reduced motion: rest pose. The 11 unrigged identities stay static. The inspection dialog keeps its cropped still.
3. Performance: no frame-time regression at 1920x1080 with 6 foes (measure before/after).
