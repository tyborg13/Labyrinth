# sb-03 — Status icons fit and centre in their pills and plates (owner follow-up)

## Header
- The owner approved the built status plates and pills (inbox `status-badges-built`, 2026-10-26) with one note: some
  icons don't fit inside their pills; they overrun or sit slightly off-centre vertically.
- The director confirmed it at 4× on the real renderer:
  - the Immobilized icon (the red X over a boot) spills past its pill's left edge;
  - the Chilled ice-crystal icon on icon-only (compact) pills is wider than the pill and touches the border;
  - several icons sit 1–2 px high because their art has uneven transparent padding.
  The cause: each icon is drawn at a fixed nominal size and centred on its texture canvas, not on its visible pixels.
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark`. Risk tier: low. Owned: the icon drawing in `scripts/board_status_plates.gd` (pills,
  compact pills and Block/Stoneskin plates) and its tests. No change to pill or plate sizes, borders, colours, words,
  counts, order or layout.

## Change (director rule)
1. For every status/defense icon, measure its **visible bounds**: the alpha > 10% bounding box of the texture, cached
   per texture.
2. **Fit box:** the pill's inner icon slot with 1 px clearance from the inner edge of the border on every side; the
   same rule applies to the plate's icon slot. Scale the icon so its visible bounds fit inside the fit box. Scale down
   only, never up; an icon that already fits keeps its current scale.
3. **Centre** the visible bounds on the fit box's centre, snapped to whole pixels. Use the existing texture filter.
4. Tooltip icons are out of scope.

## Tests
- Over every status, keyword-status and defense icon the board can show, in a full pill, a compact pill and a plate:
  the drawn visible bounds lie inside the fit box (0 px overrun) and are centred within 1 px on both axes. The mock
  geometry comparison (`status_capture_compare.py`) stays within 3 px.
- A mutation that restores canvas-centring must fail.

## Proof
- Focused sb suites, the capture probe `--check-only`, the UI identity lint. Windowed: the status capture probe
  (fresh manifest path) plus a new 4× sheet of every icon in a full pill, a compact pill and a plate, before and after.
  The director judges it.
