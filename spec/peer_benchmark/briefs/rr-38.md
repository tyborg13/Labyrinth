## Change
1. **Cut (spec §1), following the motion data's `pieces_note`.**
   - The torso owns the whole cowl; no cowl pixel belongs to an arm. The pelvis owns the belt and buckle.
   - The tabard and both coat tails are cloth chains on the pelvis.
   - Each arm is a skinned root limb with the gloved fist as a rigid hand. Each leg is a skinned root leg with a rigid
     boot.
   - The chain and hook form one rigid held item in `hand_far`, pivoting at the grip and hanging at rest.
   - Features: the visor slit belongs to `head` (front only), the tower emblem to `tabard` (front only).
   - Rests are 0 px against both paintings in source space. The production rest is the density-processed rest.
2. **Hidden fills (spec §1, the rr-37 lesson), from the start.** Every area a moving piece uncovers shows its covered
   piece's own painted continuation, taken from at least 2 px inside that piece's rest outline:
   - the torso flank under each arm's full rest footprint;
   - the near coat tail and trousers under the near fist and forearm (`press`, `guard`);
   - the cowl under the raised chain arm (`reel`, `pin`);
   - the coat and trousers under the chain's rest footprint.

   Never a dark slab, an outline fragment, a speck or a hole.
   - Procedural fills from a piece's own texels failed twice for the Lancer's far arm. If a fill fails the director's
     native judgment, report it with frames: the director supplies a painted underlay (as rr-39), and you wire it in
     with the density partition hook. Don't iterate procedurally.
3. **Draw order.** The camera-side arm draws above the torso, pelvis, tabard and both coat tails in every frame:
   `arm_near` in the front, `arm_far` in the rear. The helm stays above both arms. The hook is never hidden behind the
   body at the contact of `reel` or `pin`, in either view.
4. **Production.** Replace the old Gaoler on the board, pre-battle and portraits, in both views:

   | Intent | Clip |
   |---|---|
   | `chain_reel` (pull) | `reel`; the existing pull and its chain effect at its contact |
   | `manacle_pin` (ranged, Immobilize) | `pin`; the existing projectile at its contact |
   | `cudgel_press` (move_toward + melee) | `move` for the steps, then `press`; the existing melee hit at its contact |
   | `iron_guard` (move_toward + block) | `move`, then `guard`; the existing block at its contact |
   | hit / death | `hit` / `death`, then the existing fade |

   - `move` plays for every movement step, whatever the intent.
   - The melee strike trail follows the near fist (`hand_near`) through `press`, as the trail spec does for fists.
   - Retire the old renderer and its name branches. No new creature-name branches in shared code.
5. **Knees:** declare `knee_policy` per leg and view from the maquette, and report the measured stance bends.
6. **Gates.** The rr-37 set in both views:
   - idle rigidity, clean pieces, seams and stray pixels;
   - signed motion and action direction, with coverage for every action clip;
   - the knee, minimum-reach and support gates on every `move` swing;
   - dense reach (overreach reported, not failed);
   - native component counts and draw order;
   - the chain stays rigid and attached at the grip until `release_at`;
   - the generic uncovered-surface gate from rr-39 (full vacated footprint, native ties), which must also fail on a
     constructed frame with a missing fill;
   - the rr-36 native-defect diagnostics.

   The director's 2–4× judgment of the native clips is acceptance.

## Lessons from the Lancer merge (added 2026-10-25, base bbb11242f)
- **Every moving limb's vacated footprint needs a painted fill, legs included.** The Lancer passed the gate yet shows a
  flat, untextured cloak lining behind the near leg in the front lunge and Frost Pin (director-found; rr-41). Run the
  uncovered-surface gate over the vacated footprint of every limb that moves (both legs in the walk, `cudgel_press`,
  `chain_reel` and `iron_guard`), and flag any exposed region whose fill is flat (low texture variance against the
  painting around it), not only empty pixels. Report each flagged region with a crop. The director decides whether
  it needs an underlay.
- **Transforms:** use the rr-40 full `Transform2D` assignment path (no separate scale after a reflected restore); the
  new sprite-geometry regression (`tests/rr40/board_sprite_geometry.gd`) must include the Gaoler in all four facings.
- **Runner budget:** keep each new headless program under 90 s (the runner's limit is 180 s, and the integration
  suites run while other units load the machine). Shard anything longer.
