## Part lists (director, exact labels; `_near`/`_far` are body sides, as in batch A)
- **burrow_grub** — a fat segmented larva, about 1.5 tiles long, lying along the board diagonal, with its head to the
  viewer's lower-left in the front view.
  - Parts: `head` (round toothed maw ring), `seg_1` to `seg_6` (body segments, front to tail), `tail`.
  - Legs: `leg_near_1`, `leg_near_2`, `leg_near_3` and `leg_far_1`, `leg_far_2`, `leg_far_3`. The short hooked legs
    are on `seg_1` to `seg_3`; three per side, one part each.
  - Each segment is one rounded, slightly squashed ellipsoid ring with a soft crease between segments, so the rig can
    flex the body.
  - Feature: the maw's tooth ring stays whole on `head`.
- **ember_tick** — a low, wide tick, about 1 tile, with its head toward the viewer's lower-left in the front view.
  - Parts: `head` (small capitulum with mouthparts), `shell` (the big domed, cracked abdomen).
  - Legs: `leg_near_1`–`leg_near_4` and `leg_far_1`–`leg_far_4`, each split into `_upper` and `_lower`, so 16 leg
    parts (for example `leg_near_2_lower`). Leg 1 is the frontmost.
  - Feature: the glowing ember cracks stay on `shell`, and each crack is whole.
- **glacial_mirror** — a massive ice-stone golem made of heavy, rough stone masses, with a pale ice mirror slab set in
  its chest.
  - Parts: `head`, `torso`, `pelvis`, `upper_near`, `fore_near`, `hand_near` (fist), `upper_far`, `fore_far`,
    `hand_far` (fist), `thigh_near`, `shin_near`, `foot_near`, `thigh_far`, `shin_far`, `foot_far`.
  - Feature: the ice mirror slab is one feature owned by `torso`, in front only (rear: occluded).
  - **Style note (owner taste).** The owner rejects "toy soldier" shapes. Build the stone masses as irregular,
    chipped, rounded boulders with real modelled bevels and surface relief (Blender modifiers plus sculpted or
    displaced detail), never clean cubes. The current painting's blockiness is only a silhouette guide.

## Change
1. **One Blender-native spec per figure**, from real geometry:
   - Grub segments: subdivided, displaced ellipsoids.
   - Legs: tapered, curved tubes or skinned chains with joints at the leg bends.
   - Tick shell: a sculpted dome with crack grooves.
   - Golem: rigged boulders on the CC0 humanoid armature.
   
   Fit the 3/4 board facing exactly, with the facing check passing. Proportions follow the paintings.
2. **Fit gate** against the approved processed art: IoU ≥ 0.70 front and ≥ 0.65 rear, the bar batch A accepted.
   - Every part label is present in each view where the painting shows it, or has an explicit occlusion.
   - Report per-part coverage.
   - For leg-heavy figures, also report each leg part's coverage. A leg must not vanish into the body.
3. **Delivery per figure, in the mq-04 curated format:**
   - `delivery/` with front/rear clays (1024), part maps, solos, `joints_px.json`, manifests and the fit overlay;
   - `COMMIT_MANIFEST.txt` (≤ 6 MB per figure; no `.blend`, caches, candidates or determinism copies);
   - `discarded_proof_sha256.json`;
   - a fresh regeneration that matches clays and part maps.
4. Determinism, cache replay, ownership and mutation gates, and the hash-bound delivery validator, all as mq-04.
