## Glacial Mirror `glacial_mirror` (`construct: true` → anchored vs wind, not pushed)
1. **Reflect** (passive, badge always visible): a single-target ranged attack or projectile card that targets the
   mirror (from a non-adjacent tile) is reflected: the attacker takes the damage the attack would have dealt to the
   mirror before the mirror's Block, applied to the attacker normally (the attacker's own Block absorbs it first); the
   mirror takes none. Melee/adjacent, area, surface, chain and push damage hit it normally. The card targeting preview
   shows the reflection: the damage number appears on the hero with a bent-arrow marker and the tooltip line
   "Reflected: you take this damage." Tooltip: "Reflect: ranged attacks against it bounce back to the attacker. Hit it
   up close or with area effects." Reflected projectile animation: the projectile reaches the mirror, flashes white
   0.08 s, returns along the same path at 1.5× speed.
2. **Refraction**: a piercing line toward the hero, range 4, from the mirror's tile: every unit on the 4 tiles takes 4
   ice damage + Chilled. Telegraph: threat tiles on the line (normal attack telegraph, resolves this activation).
   Animation: a pale ice-blue beam (`#9cc8e6`, 6 px core + 14 px 30% glow) for 0.3 s.
3. **Crystal Guard**: Block 6 to the adjacent ally with the least Block (itself if none). **Grind Forward**: move 2.

## Gust Shrike `gust_shrike` (`flying: true`)
4. **Flying** (passive, badge): ignores surfaces, traps, Bramble and Ice Pillars while moving (passes over them;
   can't end a move on impassable terrain); takes no surface damage while flying; pushed normally.
5. **Dive** (`dive_line`, max 4): the straight board line from the shrike to the hero must be ≤ 4 tiles with no
   impassable terrain (units are allowed); else the shrike first moves up to 2 tiles to a tile that has such a line, or
   uses Talon Rake/Hover. Telegraph: dashed grey-blue `#8ea3b8` line along the path (2 px, dash 10/6) with threat tiles
   on the path; each unit on the path shows a side-push preview. Resolution: the shrike flies along the line (0.35 s);
   each unit it passes is pushed 1 tile perpendicular to the line (away from the side the shrike entered from; if
   blocked, collision rules apply); the hero takes 4 air damage; the shrike ends on the tile before the hero.
6. **Talon Rake**: move 2 + melee 3 + Bleed 1. **Hover**: move away 2 + Block 3.
