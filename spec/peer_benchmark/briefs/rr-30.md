## Change
1. **Identity-gate jobs.** Same procedure as rr-29 §1:
   - create `slag_mortar/mq11_front` and `mq11_rear`;
   - method: "mq-11 maquette paint-over (belt and loincloth only), composited below y 560 into the approved mq9
     painting";
   - process with K=4: the front matched like mq9, the rear matched to the new front;
   - build the sheet and register both jobs, with the director's judge `PASS` dated 2026-10-20 (re-judged before
     merge).
2. **Re-cut from the new paintings.**
   - The rest pose is 0 px against both (source space).
   - **Loincloth:**
     - `belt`, rigid on the pelvis;
     - `flap`, a rigid piece hinged at the belt's front centre (front view) or back centre (rear sash);
     - the flap's swing channel and the per-step sway come from the data (lag and amplitude as given).
   - **Bracers:** `bracer_<side>` are rigid pieces, children of the forearm, cut over the painted iron bracer.
     - The skinned bare arm underneath gets hidden material painted from the solo renders.
     - A bracer never skins or stretches.
   - Head, torso, crucible, fists and feet stay rigid as before.
3. **The owner's "tearing and stretching around his face and arms".**
   - First find and list the worst frames, with before crops of the face/neck seam and both arms.
   - Then close them:
     - **Joint coverage:** the shoulder and elbow seams, and the neck seam under the head, are covered by overlapping
       own-piece material (rr-27/rr-28 continuations), with zero tears under the canonical gate.
     - **Stretch gate (new, generic, data-driven):** in every presented frame of every clip, in both views, no skinned
       piece's texel edge may stretch more than **12%** from rest. Report the maximum per piece. Exceeding it fails.
4. **Gates:** rr-22's full set plus the canonical tear gate and the stretch gate, in both views and every clip. Every
   other figure stays byte-identical.
