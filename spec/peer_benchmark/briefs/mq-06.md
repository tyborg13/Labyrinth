## Change
1. **Root cause first.** State exactly why the Seer's rear came out mirrored. Candidates: a mirror transform, swapped
   side labels, or the rear fit flipping to match the old rear painting. Show the evidence.
2. **Fix.**
   - The rear is the fitted front pose turned exactly 180° about the vertical axis through `root`, with the same
     camera and the same body-side part labels (near/far named from the front fit, as mq-04).
   - Rebuild the rear clay, part map, solos, `joints_px.json` rear entries and the rear reports.
   - The facing check must pass with the rear forward screen vector pointing upper-right.
3. **Fit.**
   - Rear fit IoU against the old approved rear painting must be ≥ 0.65, as mq-04 required. Report the before and
     after values.
   - Per-part coverage: every label has genuine part-colour pixels or a recorded occlusion. In the rear, the body's
     far-side arm and the staff are nearer the camera, so expect the near-side pieces to be the occluded ones now.
4. **Regression test** over every figure with a committed maquette delivery (Seer, Lancer, Thrall, Herald, Gloam, and
   any others with front and rear deliveries). For each one, prove the rear is a turn and not a mirror:
   - the camera-near foot (lower on screen) is to the RIGHT of the far foot in the rear, and to the right in the front
     too;
   - parts of the body's far side occlude the torso in the rear (depth order flips relative to the front).
   
   It must fail on the current Seer delivery and pass after the fix.
