## Change
1. **`measure PATH [--art-scale S] [--kind creature|object] [--anatomy A]`** prints JSON with: `height`, `width`
   (alpha > 128 bbox, canvas px), `height_drawn` (= height × S), `symmetry`, `thin_abs` (opening, disk r = 2),
   `thin_rel` (disk r = round(0.03 × height)), `feature_px`, `regions`, `chroma`, `value`, `base_v_left`,
   `base_v_right` (objects), `orphan_share` and `run_length` (call `tools/pixel_density.py metrics()`, do not
   reimplement). Definitions exactly as the prototype; pad the mask by r + 2 before morphology so edges are not eroded
   by the image border.
2. **`kit --id ID --subject FILE --view {creature_front,creature_rear,beast_front,beast_rear,object_plinth}
   --size-class {small,medium,large} --anatomy A --peers CANON_ID CANON_ID [--front-ref RAW.png] [--target-h H]
   [--target-w W] --out-dir DIR`**:
   - Refuses peers that are not in `canon.json` and peers of a different `kind`.
   - K from `bands.json` `kit_factor` by target height; default target heights small 150, medium 205, large 238.
   - Refs in DIR/refs/: `01_front_ref.png` (only for rear views: `--front-ref` copied unchanged, required for rear),
     then `hero_front` (or `hero_rear` for rear views) and each peer (front or rear to match the view), each cropped to
     its alpha bbox, enlarged ×K NEAREST, centred on an opaque `#00FF00` canvas of max(1024, size + 64) with the
     bottom 32 px clear; for `object_plinth` also `footprint_guide.png`: 1024² `#00FF00`, a 6 px `#282828` outline of a
     2:1 diamond 0.8 × 248 × K px wide whose bottom corner sits at (512, 960), plus two 3 px `#5a5a5a` vertical lines
     640 px tall rising from its left and right corners.
   - DIR/prompt.md = preamble (with `{K}` substituted) + `## View` + the `views.md` section body + `## Subject` + the
     subject file + `## Size`: "The whole subject is about {H×K} pixels tall ({H} game pixels){, W×K pixels wide} and
     centred horizontally, its lowest point about 60 pixels above the bottom edge. It is shown at its natural size, not
     a miniature."
   - DIR/kit.json: id, view, size class, K, target h/w, peers, ref files with pixel digests, prompt sha256,
     `kit_version` = sha256 over preamble bytes + view section bytes + sorted ref pixel digests.
   - Prints the exact orchestrator command: `<CU> imagegen <job> --prompt-file DIR/prompt.md --ref DIR/refs/*.png`
     (job = `id-<ID>-<view>`; CU from `$CU` or the literal `python3 ~/.claude/skills/orchestrate-codex/scripts/codex_unit.py`).
3. **`process RAW --kit DIR/kit.json --out OUT.png`**: key as the prototype (Chebyshev distance d from `#00FF00`,
   alpha = clamp((d − 40) / 40, 0, 1), despill G to max(R, B) where alpha < 1), trim, pad to a multiple of K anchored at
   the bottom-left of the bbox, BOX-downscale premultiplied by exactly K, binarize alpha at 0.5, crop, place on the
   255×255 canvas with bbox centre x = 128 and bottom y = 246 (shift up/left only if it would leave the canvas, and
   report it). Writes OUT and OUT.json (raw sha256, out pixel digest using the board-density digest function, K,
   drawn size, metrics).
4. **`sheet --front OUT.png [--rear OUT_REAR.png] --kit kit.json [--art-scale S] [--landmarks JSON] --out SHEET.png`**:
   - Backdrop: `spec/art_identity/backdrop/empty_board.png` + `slots.json` (written by the probe, item 7); until those
     exist (and in tests) a synthetic backdrop: `#14100c` with a row of 2:1 diamond tiles 170 px wide, `#2a221a` fill,
     `#3a2f24` 2 px outline, screen scale 0.634.
   - Row A (1×) and Row B (same pixels ×2 NEAREST): slots left→right hero, peer 1, candidate front, candidate rear,
     candidate front mirrored, peer 2 — each drawn at screen scale × its art_scale (LANCZOS), canvas y 246 on the slot's
     tile centre.
   - Row C: candidate front and rear canvases ×2 NEAREST on `#14100c`; if landmarks given, a 2 px `#e0483c` line far→
     near with 6 px dots, and a dashed 1 px `#c9a25e` line through the near point at the ideal +26.57°; for
     `object_plinth`, the two fitted base lines in `#e0483c`.
   - Row D: metric table (metric, value, band, PASS/FAIL in `#5fae62`/`#e0483c`), plus kit_version and file digests.
     Any legible monospace font available to Pillow; nothing in the sheet is player-facing.
5. **`check [--id ID] [--strict]`**: validates `spec/art_identity/registry.json` against `bands.json`:
   - Coverage: every `art_path` in `data/enemies.json` and every cutout `front/rest.png` / `rear/rest.png` used for an
     enemy or guardian rig (reuse the board-density registry's rig list), plus the canon props, has a registry entry.
   - Entry kinds: `legacy` (grandfathered: stores its measured metrics and pixel digest; fails only if the file's digest
     changes), `pending_reart` (the twelve 2026-10-12 enemies: listed as warnings; `--strict` turns them into failures),
     `kit` (new art: requires kit job id, kit_version that matches the job's kit.json, prompt path, raw sha256, pixel
     digest equal to the file, sheet path, `judge` {verdict: PASS, date}, `owner_approval` note, landmarks for creatures
     and legged objects, and every metric inside its band or covered by an `exceptions` entry {metric, value, reason}
     within the hard limits such as `thin_rel_max_with_exception`).
   - Prints a readable per-entry report; exit 1 on any failure.
6. **`adopt-legacy`**: writes `registry.json` with a `legacy` entry (metrics + digest) for every currently covered path
   except the twelve 2026-10-12 enemies (`pending_reart`), sorted by path. Run it once and commit the output.
7. **`tests/bestiary_lineup_probe.gd`** (windowed, SubViewport 1920×1080 `UPDATE_ALWAYS` like
   `tests/movement_undo_probe.gd`; never `root.get_texture()`): a neutral-biome board with no props; captures
   `00_empty_board.png` and `slots.json` (for each tile in two rows the screen position of the tile centre and the
   screen px per canvas px at art_scale 1.0); then `01_lineup_normals.png` with the hero and every non-boss enemy in
   `data/enemies.json` idle in rows on tiles (front facing toward the hero, health bars and chips hidden), and
   `02_lineup_bosses.png` the same for bosses. List the windowed command; the director runs it and commits the backdrop.
8. **Tests** `python3 tests/test_art_identity_gate.py` (unittest): metric unit tests on synthetic masks (disk symmetry
   ≥ 0.99; 1-px lines → thin_abs ≈ 1; corner-on diamond base passes `base_v`, square base fails); prototype parity on
   three canon files (hero front, crawler front, crag outcrop) within 0.005; fixtures in `tests/fixtures/art_identity/`
   copied from the integration tree: the current Storm Capacitor (fails `base_v`), Arc Thrall with landmarks
   far (88, 247) near (181, 250) (fails `lateral_axis`, 1.8°), hero front with far (106, 209) near (160, 222) (passes,
   13.5°); `kit` determinism (two runs → identical kit.json and refs); `process` places a synthetic K=4 sprite at
   centre 128 / bottom 246 at the right size; `check` passes on the committed registry and fails when a legacy file's
   pixels change or a kit entry lacks a judge verdict.
