  his face and arms. We need to clean all that up."

  rewrote the motion, and the gates move onto rendered frames.

## Change
1. **Import the new motion** and rebuild everything derived from it: compiled motion, samples, records and bindings.
2. **Joint budgets, measured.**
   - Sweep each motion channel alone in the CPU renderer, both views, to find the largest value each joint takes
     before the image gates in §3 fail.
   - Write the budgets per channel and view to `experiments/cutouts/slag_mortar/rr31/joint_budgets.json`.
   - Add a validator that checks the director's keys against them.
   - If any director key exceeds its budget, don't change the data. Report it as `TODO_DESIGN_budget_<clip>_<channel>`
     with the measured limit, and the director will re-author it.
3. **Image gates on rendered frames.** Run them on every frame of every clip in both views, on the CPU sweep and also
   on the native review-capture frames (flat background, so the figure segments exactly). Use native pixels and
   compare against the rest frame of the same view.
   - **Notches:** `closing(alpha, disk r=2) AND NOT alpha`, counted within 14 px of each joint's posed contact centroid.
     No joint zone may have more notch pixels than at rest.
   - **Frayed edges:** `alpha AND NOT opening(alpha, disk r=1)`, counted per joint zone. At most 2 more than at rest.
   - **Interior contour** (CPU, with your attribution): no texel from a piece's exterior contour at rest may appear 2 px
     or more inside the frame's silhouette.
   - Keep round 3's cover bound and dark-donor checks.
   - **They must fail today.** Run the image gates on the round-3 native review frames from the rr-30 worktree: they
     must fail on the lob heave, the brace hold and the death. Record which frames fail.
   - Register them in the Mortar's tests, with mutation checks.
4. If, with the new motion, a gate still fails in some frame, fix the construction at the source as in round 3 (pivot
   registration at the painted contact, joint-local covers from adjacent material), never by masking captures.
