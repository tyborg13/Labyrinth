   - the green residue rule;
   - production rest measured against the density-processed rest;

2. **Collar.**
   - The collar, with the front lock plate and chain, is part of `torso` (rigid).
   - It must never overlap the head piece's jaw or mouth in any presented frame of any clip, including the idle bob,
     the jab, the tether and the death pitch. Report the minimum clearance in px per view.
   - The head draws above the collar only where the painting shows the jaw over it.
3. **Loincloth (the skirt).**
   - The whole wrapped skirt and its rope belt belong to `pelvis` and the existing `loincloth` cloth chain
     (`loincloth_tip`).
   - The skirt draws above both thighs, so a swinging thigh passes behind its hem, never in front of it.
   - The hem follows through at most 4 px and settles by the end of each clip.
   - Hidden fills under the skirt use the thighs' own skin texels, so no hole shows when a thigh swings.
4. Rebuild the density-processed rests and shadow records. Then every Thrall gate passes headless in both views:
   - the gait-01 walk gates;
