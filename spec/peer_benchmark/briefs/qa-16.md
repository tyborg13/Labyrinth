## Change (director decisions; exact)
1. **Driver.** A `SceneTree` script that instantiates `res://scenes/run_scene.tscn`, starts a real new run, and plays it through the scene's own input handlers (the `_on_*` handlers and `_gui_input` paths, as `tools/fight_soak.gd` does). It never edits state to skip anything. Combat speed is the fastest setting and the soak's headless clock acceleration may be used. Settle on `_animation_lock` as `fight_soak` does.
2. **Behaviour: seeded and exploring.** At each decision, pick uniformly from what the screen offers: a legal card and target, a Move, Blink or attack, Pass, a map room, a reward card or Skip, a reroll, a Hearth option, a merchant buy, sell or Hide, a relic, an event choice, an equipment action. To let runs get deep, the hero has 9,999 max Health and full Health from the start of the run. This is the only state override, and it's set through the same field a fixture uses. Prefer attacking in fights so they end: 80% attack-or-move-toward-enemy if legal, 20% uniform.
3. **Side actions, each at most once per room, with 10% chance:** hover every card in hand; open and close the deck view, the map, the settings menu and the heirloom view if available; save, tear down the scene and re-enter it from the saved run (the real resume path); press Escape; toggle reduced motion.
4. **Detection:**
   - **Errors:** every `SCRIPT ERROR`, `ERROR:`, `USER ERROR`, failed `assert` and `push_error`/`push_warning` line in the Godot log, plus any crash (non-zero exit, signal).
   - **Softlock:** no legal decision offered and no animation lock for 20 seconds of scene time, or one fight lasting past 200 decisions, or one screen past 120 decisions.
   - **Leaks:** tree node count and orphan node count at each room entry; flag growth of more than 25% from section 0 to section 5.
   - Every finding keeps the seed, section, room kind, last 20 decisions, and the error line with its file and line number.
5. **Sample:** first seed 1, at most 3 Godot processes, stop launching at 75 minutes of wall clock. Report runs started, runs completed, sections reached and room kinds visited. **If a room kind was never visited, say so.** Guardian, dragon, Warband, Snare, Hoarder, Last Light, every shop kind and the Graftwright should all appear.
6. **Report `run_hunt_2026-11/report.md`:** findings deduplicated by error text plus file and line, worst first (crash, softlock, script error, warning, leak), with counts, first seed, room kind and one exact command that replays that seed to that point. Then the coverage table. No fixes, no opinions on design.
