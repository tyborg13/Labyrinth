## Change
0. **The far-arm fill comes from the director underlay.** `front_far_underlay.png` is the approved r5 front, repainted
   with the far arm and spear removed. It is registered to `mq14_front/processed.png` in source space (255 px), so
   use it as-is.
   - Under the full rest footprint of `arm_far`, `hand_far` and `spear` (`front_far_footprint_mask.png`, the union of
     their rest alphas), the hidden fills of `torso` and `cape_far` take their texels from the underlay. The torso
     owns what lies inside the torso's extended flank, and `cape_far` owns the teal cloak.
   - Underlay air inside the footprint stays air (true negative space). Everything opaque in the underlay there is
     covered.
   - Rest identity stays 0 px. Outside the footprint, nothing changes.
   - Retire the procedural far-arm fills that the underlay replaces.
   - Rear: remove the 1 px hem outline texel by the same uncovered-surface rule (hem edge texels continue the cloth's
     own edge).
   - Proof: front `cast`, `lunge`, `guard`, `jab` and `pin` with zero uncovered metrics, and the gate (item 2) failing
     on the rr-37 frames above.
1. **Retire the legacy Lancer renderer.** `combat_board_view.gd` (~3051) still creates the old renderer beside v2,
   and (~11374) takes the projectile origin from its socket. A single-Lancer probe has two active canvases, and at
   `cast` contact (0.55) the projectile leaves 64 board pixels from the spear tip actually drawn (reproduction:
   `/private/tmp/rr37-review-projectile.log`).
   - Remove the legacy pool, its preparation and its effect-decoration paths.
   - Take projectile sockets (`cast`, `pin`), the strike trail and any other effect anchor from the selected v2
     renderer's posed rig, in both views.
   - No new creature-name branches: use the data-driven route the other v2 figures use (the Gloam, the Herald).
   - Regressions: exactly one renderer per actor, in both views. The socket lies within 2 board px of the drawn spear
     tip at the contact of `cast`, `pin`, `jab` and `lunge`, in both views.
2. **Uncovered-surface gate breadth.** `rr37_uncovered.py` (~156, ~184) restricts its colour and tiny-component checks
   to the receiving fill's area. A spear texel left just outside it passes (rear `lunge` 36, presented `[378, 311]`;
   `/private/tmp/rr37-review-isolated-breadth.log`).
   - Measure the complete vacated footprint of every moving occluder, independent of fill support.
   - Add the reviewer's outside-cloak remnant as a mutation that must fail.
   - Make the gate generic enough for rr-38 (the Gaoler) to reuse: a figure-agnostic module under
     `tools/humanoid_cutout/`, optional and additive, with the Lancer as its first user.

3. **Re-runnable proof script.** `rr37_proof.sh` runs under `set -eu`, and the `density` stage aborts on a re-run
   because `visual_probe_runner.py` refuses to overwrite `proof/logs/density-result.json`. That skips the rest
   records, the shadow refresh, the density check and the seal, while the rest PNGs are already rewritten, so every
   later stage proves a half-installed rest. Give every stage a fresh per-run manifest path (or an explicit
   `--overwrite-result-manifest` where the stage owns the file). Add a test that runs the stage's command list twice
   against stub manifests, which must not abort.
