# fb-01 — Say why an enemy didn't act (owner request, 2026-10-24)

## Header
- Owner: when an enemy can't attack, the game says one generic word. It's out of range sometimes, or a status stops
  it. "Improve our feedback to the player as to what exactly is happening when the enemy either moves or doesn't move
  and then does nothing … make sure that's playing at a speed where it's readable."
- Today:
  - `scripts/intent_callouts.gd` shows **"Cancelled"** for every announced attack that doesn't resolve, whatever the
    cause, and "Redirected" when the target was displaced.
  - Freeze, Petrify, Shock and Immobilize already emit a start-of-turn `status` step labelled Frozen, Petrified,
    Shocked or Immobilized (`combat_engine.gd` ~7733), and they suppress the callout.
  - The callout is a floating text of 0.9 s.
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark`. Risk tier: standard. Owned: `scripts/intent_callouts.gd`, the reason data the resolver
  attaches to enemy steps (`scripts/combat_engine.gd`, additive fields only), the step playback timing in
  `scripts/run_scene.gd`, analytics (additive fields, `spec/analytics.md`), and tests.

## Change
1. **The resolver says why.** When an enemy's announced attack does not resolve, attach `no_action_reason` to its
   steps, decided by the resolver and never inferred from positions in presentation. Values:
   - `out_of_range`: no target within the attack's range;
   - `no_line_of_sight`: a target is in range, but line of sight is blocked;
   - `no_target`: the target is gone (dead, or a vanished illusion) and nothing else is targetable;
   - `shocked`: Shock allows only Move or Blink, so the attack is lost;
   - `frozen` / `petrified`: the turn is skipped;
   - `immobilized`: only when its plan included a move it couldn't make. If it then attacks anyway, show this at the
     start of the turn.
2. **Exact callout text**, over the enemy, in the existing callout style:

   | Reason | Text |
   |---|---|
   | `out_of_range` | Out of range |
   | `no_line_of_sight` | No line of sight |
   | `no_target` | No target |
   | `shocked` | Shocked |
   | `frozen` | Frozen |
   | `petrified` | Petrified |
   | `immobilized` | Immobilized |

   - The four status reasons draw the status's existing glossary icon (`shock`, `freeze`, `petrify`, `immobilize`)
     just left of the word, at the callout's cap height. One signal per concept: the status looks like the status.
   - "Cancelled" is retired. "Redirected" stays as it is.
   - An enemy that moves and then can't attack shows its reason after the move ends, never before.
   - Umbra-hidden enemies show nothing, as today.
3. **Readable at any speed.**
   - A reason callout stays fully opaque for **0.8 s**, then fades over the existing tail. That is 1.1 s in total,
     in real time, unscaled by combat speed (Normal, Fast, Fastest) or the enemy-phase action speed.
   - The next enemy's turn, and the hero's turn, do not start until the callout's opaque 0.8 s is over.
   - Reduced motion keeps the same hold and drops the float.
4. **Analytics.** Add `no_action_reason` to the existing enemy-action event (additive), and document it in
   `spec/analytics.md`.

## Proof
- Engine tests, one per reason, from a constructed state: the right `no_action_reason` and the right text.
  - Include a move-then-out-of-range case, and a Shock case that still moves.
- A timing test: at Fastest, the callout is opaque for 0.8 s of real time, and the next actor waits for it.
- The UI identity lint passes (no new colour literals or font sizes; reuse the callout's tokens).
- Real renderer, 1920×1080: one capture each of Out of range, No line of sight and Shocked, mid-hold, plus the
  windowed command. The director judges them.
- Focused only; the director runs the full suites at merge.

## Out of scope
- New statuses or rules. Hero-side messages. Guardian `Interrupted` labels (`guardian_combat_rules.gd`). Report any
  other no-action path you find, but don't change it.
