## Change
1. **Solos from the committed delivery; no Blender.** The mq-05 delivery has the front and rear `*_solo_*.png`,
   `*_parts.png`, `*_clay.png` and `joints_px.json`. If anything is missing, stop and report.
2. **Rigs, the rr-05/rr-07 way.**
   - Ownership from the part renders; hidden-material requests where parts overlap; connected parts and seam
     underlays.
   - Rest identity 0 px against the approved processed art, in both views.
   - Root (`body`) and hierarchy:
     - Grub: chain the rings from `seg_3` outward, head-ward and tail-ward, so each ring pivots where it meets its
       neighbour. Legs are children of the ring they hang from.
     - Tick: every leg's upper segment is a child of `shell`; each lower segment is a child of its upper; the head is a
       child of the shell.
3. **Features** (rr-05 format, owned by one part, 1 px rim, holes filled):
   - Grub: the maw's tooth ring is one feature owned by `head` (front; the rear is occluded).
   - Tick: the ember cracks are features owned by `shell` in both views. Each connected crack stays whole.

   Report pixel counts with zoomed outline crops.
4. **Production.** Replace both static board arts with the rigs, and map intents exactly as each clip's `intents`
   lists:

   | Creature | Intent | Clips |
   |---|---|---|
   | Grub | `gnaw` | `move`, then `gnaw` |
   | Grub | `burrow` | `burrow`; when it ends, the existing burrowed presentation (the mound) takes over |
   | Grub | `erupt` | `erupt` at the marked tile, starting hidden and fading in, before the existing erupt VFX contact |
   | Tick | `scuttle` | `move`, then `bite` |
   | Tick | `kindle` | `kindle` |
   | Tick | death | `death` under the existing death-burst VFX |

   - Sound and VFX sync at each clip's `contact`.
   - Pre-battle and portraits use the same renderer.
   - Everything plays through the rr-05 stepped, pixel-snapped presentation.
   - If the burrow or erupt hooks need engine changes beyond presentation, stop and report.
5. **Gates.** The rr-07 set, for both creatures:
   - bone-level signed motion at authored keys and midpoints, both views;
   - production capture from real clip progression, ≥ 6 changing frames per action including contact;
   - feature integrity;
   - zero shimmer residual on held frames;
   - the dark-slab gate on exposed hidden material;
   - native agreement with exact-tie handling;
   - mutation regressions.

   Plus a stray-pixel gate: no opaque component of ≤ 8 px detached from the silhouette in any presented frame.

## Proof

  PASS.
- Windowed, run by the director: the exact command sequence per creature for native rest, render, verify, motion
  gates, production capture and the real-time review MP4.

  - Plus a contact sheet of every presented frame per clip at 3×.
