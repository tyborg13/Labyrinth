# rr-19 — Storm Capacitor: independent death clocks on the enemy-phase overlap path (fresh session on rr-17)

## Header
- rr-17 (Storm Capacitor rig) used its three rounds. Its re-review at c8d364c7a
  (`/private/tmp/labyrinth_codex/review/rr-17-c8d364c7a.md`) confirmed four of the round-2 fixes. One Medium finding
  remains, and this unit fixes only that.
- **The finding.** rr-17 gave each dying creature its own death clock on the blocking defeat path
  (`scripts/run_scene.gd` ~23423, `scripts/maquette_cutout_runtime/death_sequence.gd`). The enemy-phase overlap path
  bypasses it:
  - `run_scene.gd` ~13469 calls `CombatPacing.overlap_defeats`.
  - That path gives every dying creature one shared duration and progress (`scripts/combat_pacing.gd` ~541 and ~444,
    `decorate`).
  - Reproduction: Retaliate kills a 1-HP Ember Tick, and its death burst kills an adjacent 1-HP Capacitor. At 0.9 s the
    Tick alone reaches its final frame 53, but paired with the Capacitor it reaches only frame 20. The shared duration
    grows from 0.900 to 2.436 s.
  - The reviewer's runnable probe is copied to `tests/rr19/review_overlap_probe.gd`. Turn it into a registered
    regression (the registration audit rejects orphan `*_probe.gd` helpers), or delete it once your regression covers
    it.
- **Rule (decisions 2026-10-19):** in any multi-kill, each dying creature advances its own declared death sequence. The
  whole sequence lasts as long as the longest one. Every other creature's frames and last-frame time are exactly what
  the base code gives.
- Owned: `scripts/combat_pacing.gd` (`overlap_defeats`, `decorate` and their helpers), the overlap path in
  `scripts/run_scene.gd`, `scripts/maquette_cutout_runtime/death_sequence.gd`, the rr17/rr19 tests.
- Base: rr-17 c8d364c7a. The enemy-pacing work (pace-01/02, owner-reviewed) lives in `combat_pacing.gd`. Every multi-kill
  without a Capacitor, and every non-death pacing value, must stay byte-identical.

## Change
1. Apply the independent per-creature clocks to `CombatPacing.overlap_defeats` and `decorate`, sharing the code the
   blocking path uses. No second implementation.
2. Regressions through `_animate_turn_order_alongside_defeats` with `enemies_active = true`:
   - the review's Tick + Capacitor case (the Tick reaches frame 53 at 0.9 s, as alone);
   - the Capacitor paired with each of the Seer, Thrall, Grub, Tick, Bloomer and Wisp;
   - a multi-kill without the Capacitor that is byte-identical to the base.
3. A mutation that reverts the overlap path to the shared clock must be caught.
