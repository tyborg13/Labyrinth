# ui-03 — Empower, redesigned: the card is the control

## Header
- Owner request (2026-10-19), verbatim: "One thing I'd like us to throw into our work queue is that we need a change to
  how empowered cards work. Right now, it's a tiny button off to the side, it's not very aesthetic, it's hard to find,
  you have to select it with the board targeting arrow. I don't have a prescriptive answer, but it needs to be
  redesigned to look and feel much better."
- Today:
  - The control is a compact `ActionContextEmpower` toggle in the floating `ActionStepTracker`, auto-placed off the
    board, usually bottom-right.
  - While click-targeting, the cursor is hidden, so the player steers the arrow onto it.
  - A drag can never Empower.
  - The choice resets on every selection, there is no glow, and the label always reads "[E]".
  - Code map: `scripts/run_scene.gd` `_empower_toggle_available` :34022, `_add_empower_command` :34044,
    `_toggle_pending_empower` :34059, `_action_step_tracker_state` :13617, `_layout_action_step_tracker_content`
    :13737, `_set_targeting_cursor_suppressed` :8209, `_commit_drag_drop` :7879; engine
    `combat_engine.prepare_player_card(..., "empower")` :702 and `card_keyword_rules.gd`.
- Director mockups:
  - `spec/peer_benchmark/staged/ui-03/mock_states.png`: hover, seal hover, charged and blocked, on the real hand;
  - `spec/peer_benchmark/staged/ui-03/mock_charged_1080.png`: the full 1920x1080 frame.
- Use `$create-labyrinth-ui` and `spec/visual_design_system.md`. Reuse `scripts/ui_socket.gd`, the medallion ring
  shared with the Purge ×2 badge.
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark`. Owned:
  - the Empower UI and input paths in `scripts/run_scene.gd`;
  - the card widget (`scripts/card_widget.gd` and the hand layout);
  - `scripts/input_router.gd` (Empower glyphs);
  - `data/glossary.json` and `data/grimoire.json` (the Empower entries only);
  - `spec/card_keywords.md` (the Empower UI contract);
  - `spec/analytics.md`;
  - the tests below.
- Rules don't change: costs, payment timing and relic interactions (Crown of Surplus, Overclock Coil, Iron Lung) stay
  exactly as they are. One exception is in Change 4: a health cost you can't survive can't be charged.

## Change
1. **The Empower seal.** A `UiSocket` (medallion ring) on the card's top-right frame corner.
   - Size and position: 66 layout px at 100% UI scale, mirroring the Time badge's anchor at the top-left
     horizontally.
   - Icon: `assets/art/icons/empower.png`, nearest filter, 52% of the socket.
   - It appears on the hovered, selected or focused card (the raised card) when that card has Empower, including Crown
     of Surplus' granted Empower.
   - A charged card shows its lit seal at all times, drawn above neighbouring cards.
   - Unlit seals on cards that aren't raised stay hidden.
2. **Seal states** (colours as RGB):
   - *Unlit (offered):*
     - default `ring_tint`;
     - centre fill `#181412`;
     - icon RGB × 0.62 + (30,18,8) × 0.38 (a dim ember, never grey).
   - *Hover or focus:*
     - ring tint (1.25,1.18,1.06);
     - icon RGB × 0.85 + (30,18,8) × 0.15.
   - *Charged:*
     - ring `ACTIVE_RING_TINT`;
     - centre fill `#46200E`;
     - icon at full colour;
     - an ember halo behind the seal: `#FF963C`, alpha 110 at the centre fading to 0 at 0.95 × socket radius;
     - the card gets an ember rim glow outside its frame: three rounded-rectangle bands at 5, 9 and 14 px out,
       alpha 140, 90 and 50 of `#FF963C`, blurred 6 px;
     - the glow is static, with no pulsing.
   - *Charging moment:*
     - the seal scales 1.0 → 1.18 → 1.0 over 0.22 s (ease out, then in);
     - the halo alpha doubles and settles over 0.3 s;
     - reduced motion: an instant state change, no scale or flare.
   - *Blocked* (a health cost ≥ current HP, after Stoneskin under Iron Lung):
     - ring `DISABLED_RING_TINT`;
     - centre `#2E1212`;
     - icon desaturated;
     - a crimson slash `#C8463C`, 3 px at a 66 px socket, from bottom-left to top-right;
     - it can't be toggled.
   - *Locked* (a multi-target card after its first target):
     - shows the current lit or unlit state;
     - no hover response.
3. **Key hint.** An input-aware keycap at the seal's lower right, from the existing input glyph system: `E`, or the
   controller's right-stick glyph. It shows only on the raised or focused card, and is hidden when the seal is blocked.
   Labels never print `[E]` on a controller.
4. **Interaction.**
   - Clicking the seal toggles that card's charge. It never selects or starts dragging the card. Hovering the seal shows
     the action cursor and its tooltip.
   - `E` toggles, in this order of precedence: the dragged card during a drag; the selected card during click-targeting
     or confirmation; otherwise the hovered card. The controller's right stick toggles the focused or selected card.
   - **Charge persists on that hand card** until it is played, discarded or burned, or the turn ends; then it clears.
     Deselecting and reselecting keeps it.
   - A charged card plays empowered on every path: click-target, drag and drop, and targetless confirm. It calls
     `prepare_player_card(..., "empower")`.
   - A drag can now Empower: charge first and then drag, or press `E` or the right stick mid-drag.
   - **Click-targeting:**
     - the cursor is visible whenever the pointer is over the hand or HUD, and the targeting arrow hides while the
       pointer is off the board, so the seal is clicked like any button;
     - toggling keeps the current AoE rotation and force-aim (fix the `_begin_card_preview` reset, :21612–21615);
     - multi-target cards can toggle until the first target is chosen, then the seal locks.
   - Blocked: health cost ≥ current HP. Tooltip: "Not enough health: Empower costs N HP."
   - Remove the `ActionContextEmpower` button and its grid slot. The tracker keeps its other commands (techniques,
     Rotate). The controller prompt bar keeps "Empower" / "Unempower" with the correct glyph.
5. **Text.**
   - Seal tooltip line 1: `Empower: <cost> · <bonus>`, using the existing token summaries (e.g. "Empower: +2 Time ·
     Stagger +4").
   - Seal tooltip line 2, unlit: "Click the seal or press E". On a controller: "Press RS".
   - Seal tooltip line 2, charged: "Charged. Click to cancel." On a controller: "Charged. Press RS to cancel."
   - Glossary `empower` definition: "Pay the listed extra cost when you play this card to gain the listed bonus. Charge
     it with the seal on the card's corner."
   - Grimoire `keyword:empower` body line 1: "An optional extra cost. Charge the card with the Empower seal on its
     top-right corner (or press E; the right stick on a controller). A charged card glows and stays charged until you
     play it or your turn ends." Keep line 2 as it is.
6. **Sound.** No new cue in this unit; existing card sounds stay. A charge sound comes later through an owner
   audition.
7. **Analytics.** Add an append-only event `empower_toggled` with `{card_id, on, source: "mouse"|"keyboard"|"controller",
   phase: "hand"|"targeting"|"drag"|"confirm"}`. `card_played`'s Empower fields stay unchanged. Document it in
   `spec/analytics.md`.
