## Motion (director decisions; all motion from rigid parts, feet stay planted unless the clip leaves the ground)
- **idle** (loop 2.4 s): body breathing (scale-y +2%, 1.2 s period, pivot at the feet); head holds still then snaps to a
  new tilt (±6°, ±1 px) in 2 frames every 0.6–1.1 s (deterministic sequence of 4 poses), like a bird looking around;
  tail flick (up 4°, back down) once per loop; wings settle (±2°, out of phase with each other). No vertical body bob.
- **move** (one board step; use the existing board step duration): crouch 2 frames (body −3 px, wings lift 6°) →
  downbeat launch (wings sweep down 35°, body rises on an arc peaking at 24 px mid-step) → glide (wings spread, tail fans)
  → landing flare (wings up 20°, legs reach forward, tail down) → settle with 2 px squash and recover.
- **dive** (attack `dive`): wings fold back to 70% span, body pitches forward 20°, head leads; at contact wings flare out
  and the body pitches back; recover over 6 frames.
- **talon_rake** (attack): one wing beat lifting the body 6 px, legs swing forward 25° (talons lead), contact, recoil.
- **hover** (Hover/block): two full wing beats in place (±30°), body level, 4 px lift held.
- **hit**: recoil 3 px away from the attacker, head jerks back 8°, wings jolt open 10° then settle.
- **death**: wings go limp (droop 30°), body tilts 25° toward its far side and drops to the ground, tail slumps; final
  frame held.
Timing must sync with the existing attack/effect presentation (contact frame = the effect's impact frame).
