## Change — new primitives (deterministic, rasterisable, Blender-identical, all optional)
1. `plate`: an armour plate = a 2D outline polygon (points in the plate's local u/v, any shape: diamond, trapezoid,
   pointed lame, shield) conformed onto a curved patch (`curve`: cylinder radius about an axis, or ellipsoid patch),
   placed by `at`/`offset`/`rot` (or attached to a joint and oriented along a bone), with `thick` and edge `bevel`, and
   optional `ridge` (a raised centre line of height h along u or v). Sharp corners must stay sharp in the silhouette.
2. `lames`: N stacked plates generated from one outline with per-lame `scale`, `drop` along the bone and `overlap`, for
   pauldrons, tassets, sabatons.
3. `collar`: a closed cloth ring around a neck/shoulder anchor that drapes evenly all the way round (no seam or wings):
   `radius_top`, `radius_hem`, `length`, `front_length` / `back_length` (the hem can be shorter in front), `rag`
   {count, depth, seed}, `folds`, `thick`. Must never flare outward in front beyond `radius_hem`.
4. `crystal`: a faceted crystal blade along a path (`a`→`b`, inherits a bone direction): `sides` (4–6), `max_width`,
   `width_profile` (list of [t, w] along the path), `jag` {count, depth, seed} steps along the edges, sharp `tip`. Used
   for icicle spearheads that follow the shaft.
5. `gem`: an n-sided bipyramid (`sides`, `length`, `width`, `rot`) for inset gems (diamond shape when seen front-on).
6. `hood`: a pointed cowl: a shell with a pointed tip (`tip`, `tip_lean`), a face `opening` (height/width/depth of
   the recess), and a drape that continues down to `drape_z` so it meets the collar.
7. Clay render quality (Blender adapter): keep flat part-label renders unchanged, but give the clay pass cavity +
   ridge shading and sharp normals on `plate`/`lames`/`crystal`/`gem` so plate edges read in the grey reference.
8. Rasteriser/fit support for all new primitives; synthetic tests per primitive (silhouette, determinism, sharp corners
   preserved, collar never wider than radius_hem in front).
