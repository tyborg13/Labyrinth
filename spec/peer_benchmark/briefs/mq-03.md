## Change
1. **Spec v2 (Blender-native)** consumed by a Blender Python builder (`blender/build.py SPEC OUT`):
   - `base`: a body asset from the bundle (e.g. `realistic_body_male`), appended by name.
   - `armature`: generated bones named after the spec joints (pelvis, torso, head, shoulder/elbow/hand ×2, hip/knee/
     ankle ×2); the base mesh is bound with automatic weights; `pose` places joints at the spec joint coordinates; bone
     `scale` per segment adjusts limb/torso thickness; segment lengths follow the joints (so the fit tool can drive
     proportions through joints).
   - `pieces`: accessories built with Blender features, each with a part label:
     - `plate`: outline polygon (2D points) → mesh → Solidify (thick) → Bevel (sharp corners kept) → Shrinkwrap onto the
       body with `offset`, placed at a joint/bone with `rot`; optional `ridge`.
     - `lames`: N plates from one outline with per-lame scale/drop/overlap (pauldrons, tassets, sabatons).
     - `cloth`: a garment simulated with Blender cloth physics against the posed body (collision): kinds `cloak`
       (pinned along a shoulder/neck line), `collar` (closed ring around the neck that drapes evenly — no flaring wings),
       `tabard`/`skirt` (pinned at the belt, split panels), `hood` (pointed cowl pinned around the head/neck with a face
       opening). Params: size/shape of the flat pattern, pin group, frames, stiffness, wind (optional, small),
       `rag` {count, depth, seed} notches cut into the hem BEFORE simulation, thickness via Solidify. Deterministic:
       fixed frame count and settings; cache the baked result keyed by the spec hash.
     - `curve`: a Bezier/poly path with bevel radius and taper profile (spear shafts, straps, belts, chains).
     - `crystal`: a faceted crystal along a path that continues a curve/bone direction (icicle spear head following the
       shaft): `sides`, `length`, `width_profile`, `jag` {count, depth, seed}, sharp tip.
     - `gem`: bipyramid (`sides`, `length`, `width`) inset with `offset` (diamond seen front-on).
   - Part labels: every piece and every body region gets a label colour. Body regions come from the armature weights
     (head, torso, pelvis, upper/fore/hand, thigh/shin/foot per side) so the cutout rig keeps its ownership masks.
2. **Renders** (same camera, sizes and file names as today): `front/rear_parts.png` (flat labels), `front/rear_clay.png`
   (grey studio shading WITH cavity/ridge so plate edges and folds read), `*_solo_<part>.png`, `joints_px.json`.
   Provide a Cycles-CPU path for headless silhouette renders if Workbench is unavailable.
3. **Fit tool**: measure/fit using Blender-rendered silhouettes for v2 specs (fit adjusts joint heights and bone
   scales; never edits pieces' design parameters).
4. **Lancer r3** (`frostglass_lancer/spec_r3.json`) — director's design, build exactly this list, positions near the
   r2 joints, then fit to the source (`fit` block from spec_r2, gate as r2):
   - base `realistic_body_male`; upright stance, feet planted slightly apart (both feet forward), weight even.
   - hood: pointed cowl, tip leaning slightly back, face opening; draping into the collar.
   - collar: ragged fur-like cloth ring around the neck and over the shoulders, evenly round, hem ~0.3 below shoulders.
   - pauldrons: 3 lames each, pointed trapezoid outline, overlapping downward over the upper arm.
   - breastplate: plate with a vertical centre ridge, tapering to the waist; gem: 4-sided diamond gem at mid-chest.
   - belt (curve) + diagonal strap (curve) from the right hip across.
   - tabard/skirt: two split front panels from the belt to just above the knees, ragged hems, and two tassets (2 lames).
   - cloak: heavy cloth pinned across the back of the shoulders, falls to mid-shin, flares and trails toward the
     figure's back/right, ragged notched hem.
   - arms: vambraces (plates) on forearms, gauntlets on hands; far hand grips the spear, near fist at the hip.
   - legs: cuisses on thighs, pointed diamond poleyns on knees, greaves (plates) on shins, sabatons (3 lames) on feet.
   - spear: straight shaft (curve) held in the far hand, nearly upright with the head up-left; crystal head ~1/5 of the
     spear length continuing the shaft direction, 5 sides, 6 jags.
5. Tests: deterministic build (same spec → identical mesh hashes), label coverage, cloth never intersecting the body
   (collision margin), collar symmetry (no part of the collar wider in front than at the back), plate corner sharpness.
