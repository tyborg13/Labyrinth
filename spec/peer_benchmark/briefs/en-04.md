## Briar Weaver `briar_weaver`
1. **Thornwall** (`grow_bramble`, `line3_perpendicular_front_of_hero`, rounds 3): the line of 3 tiles perpendicular to
   the weaver→hero direction, centred on the tile directly in front of the hero (the tile between the hero and the
   weaver's side; diagonal direction → use the row); skip occupied/impassable tiles. Resolves immediately on the
   weaver's activation (not delayed). Bramble = impassable terrain for 3 rounds (counts down with other terrain at the
   end of the enemy turn, after wind); blocks movement, not line of sight; a unit pushed into Bramble takes 2 damage
   and Bleed 1 and stays in place (the Bramble remains). Draw: reuse the terrain renderer with a procedural thorn
   tangle: 5–7 dark brown `#3b2a1c` curved strokes (3 px) per tile with `#6f8f4e` leaf flecks, rising 0.25 s from the
   floor when grown, sinking 0.25 s when expiring. Tooltip: "Bramble: blocks movement for <n> more round(s). A unit
   pushed into it takes 2 damage and Bleeds."
2. **Lash of Thorns**: ranged 3 + Bleed 1, range 3. **Root Mend**: heal the most wounded ally within 3 for 4 (never
   above max; fallback to Lash if no wounded ally).

## Slag Mortar `slag_mortar`
3. **Lob Slag** (`lob_mark`, plus shape, range 3–6, ignores line of sight): marks the hero's tile and its 4 orthogonal
   neighbours (board orthogonals), skipping off-board tiles; delayed-mark language (fire keeps the ember colours), icon
   `lob`, centre medallion "5", arm medallions "3". If the hero is closer than 3, the mortar uses Scatter Shot or Brace.
4. **Slag lands** (forced next intent): centre 5, arms 3 fire damage to any unit; Fire surface on all marked tiles.
   Animation: a glowing slag blob arcs from the mortar's kiln (0.45 s, apex 2.2 tiles above the midpoint), splashes
   (0.25 s ember burst), then surfaces appear.
5. **Brace**: Block 6 + move away 2. **Scatter Shot** (only when the hero is within 2): ranged 3, push 1.
