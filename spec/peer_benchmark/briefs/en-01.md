## Burrow Grub `burrow_grub`
1. **Burrow** (`burrow` action, `mark: hero_tile`): the grub leaves its tile's occupancy (other units can walk through
   and stand on its old tile), becomes `burrowed` and records `erupt_tile` = the hero's tile at resolution. While
   burrowed: it cannot be targeted by single-target cards/attacks; area effects and surfaces on its *current* (old) tile
   still hit it; it doesn't block movement; it is not pushed/pulled. Draw: no body; a churned-earth mound at its old
   tile (dark ochre ellipse 60% of tile width, 6–9 small rubble specks, subtle 1.5 s breathing scale ±3%), the health bar
   above the mound, a delayed mark (earth tint) on `erupt_tile` with the Erupt icon and "6".
2. **Erupt** (forced next intent): the grub surfaces at `erupt_tile`. A unit standing there takes 6 (earth) and is
   displaced to the nearest free adjacent tile (deterministic order: away from the grub's old tile, then clockwise);
   every other unit adjacent to `erupt_tile` is pushed 1 tile directly away (normal collision damage). The tile becomes
   Rubble. The grub gains Expose 2. If `erupt_tile` is impassable terrain, it surfaces on the nearest free tile and only
   pushes. If the grub dies while burrowed (area damage), the mark disappears. Animation: dust burst + the body rising
   over 0.25 s from below the tile (clip the sprite at the floor line), then the push resolution.
3. **Gnaw**: standard move + melee 3.

## Ember Tick `ember_tick`
4. **Burst** (`death_burst` on the enemy definition): when the tick dies for any reason, after its death dissolve
   begins, every unit on the 8 surrounding tiles takes 4 (+ Kindled bonus) fire damage (hero and enemies; uses normal
   block/damage rules) and its tile becomes Fire. Chain: a tick killed by another tick's burst bursts too (resolve
   breadth-first, each burst once). Badge "Bursts <N>" always visible on living ticks; hovering a tick (or the card
   preview killing it) shows a dashed fire-tinted ring on the 8 tiles and the burst damage in the lethal/damage preview
   for each unit there (the player's previews must include burst damage to the hero and enemies).
5. **Kindle** (`kindle` action, amount 2): +2 burst damage until death; stacks to max +4; badge shows the total;
   abdomen glow brighter (draw an extra additive orange glow under the sprite at 35% alpha).
6. **Scuttle**: move 3 + melee 2.
