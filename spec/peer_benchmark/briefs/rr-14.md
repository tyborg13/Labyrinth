# rr-14 — Humanoid rig v2 foundations: case-driven anatomy and foot IK

## Header
- **Why.** rr-10/rr-13 built humanoid rig v2 on the Hoarfrost Seer (owner approved 2026-10-18). Its review found the
  generic modules aren't generic yet (`spec/peer_benchmark/feedback/rr-13_review_4c3f86339.md`, findings 3 and 4;
  `rr-13_review_b576159d4.md`, Low 2). The Arc Thrall (rr-11) is next and needs all of this. This unit makes the shared
  foundation; rr-11 then builds the Thrall on it.
- **Binding spec:** `spec/peer_benchmark/humanoid_cutout_rig.md` (§1 cut table, §3 actions and knee-bend walking, §4
  channels).
- **Base:** `codex/peer-benchmark` with rr-13 merged.
- **Owned:** `tools/humanoid_cutout/**`, `scripts/maquette_cutout_runtime/humanoid_*.gd`, the Seer case data
  `experiments/cutouts/hoarfrost_seer/rr10/**` (moving values in only), `experiments/cutouts/hoarfrost_seer/rr10_*.py`,
  and tests.
- **Invariant:** the Seer stays byte-identical: its assets, rest identity, compiled motion, samples and native-gate
  inputs, except for the binding hashes of files you change. No creature renders differently.

## Change
1. **Foot IK in the runtime** (finding 3). `scripts/maquette_cutout_runtime/humanoid_motion.gd` dispatches only hand
   targets; a synthetic thigh/shin/foot chain (rest lengths 5/5, 2 px foot lift) came out 5/3.6 with no rotation.
   - Generalize the two-bone solve to foot targets on `thigh_<side>` → `shin_<side>` → `foot_<side>`.
   - Preserve both segment lengths; the knee keeps its rest bend side (spec §3); clamp an unreachable target to full
     extension without stretching.
   - Feet under a robe (`hem_<side>` children, the Seer) keep today's behaviour.
2. **Case-driven anatomy** (finding 4). Move everything creature-specific out of the generic modules into each case's
   data, with one schema file documenting it:
   - `gates.py`: required pieces (the robe, hem-parented feet, the far-hand staff) and the production asset binding;
   - `materials.py` and silhouette ownership: held-item and depth roles, the piece vocabulary, joint pairs;
   - `integration.py`: the scale and the identity/density record targets;
   - `tucks.py`: the director's tuck depths (wrist 5 px with rigid cuff rows, foot 4 px, shoulder/armpit continuation
     4 px, sleeve inner edge 3 px, robe top 5 px) become case data with the same values.
   - The Seer's values move into its case unchanged.
3. **A second anatomy as a test fixture.** Add a small synthetic humanoid case shaped like the Thrall (spec §1 Thrall
   row): no robe; visible legs (skinned thigh+shin, rigid feet); a rigid held `tube` on the near arm with a target bone
   `tube_tip` and no near hand; a bare skinned far arm with a rigid `claw`; a skinned `loincloth` from `pelvis`. The
   generic gates, compiler and runtime accept it with no Seer names anywhere. Use tiny generated textures; it's a
   fixture, not art.
4. **Tests.**
   - Foot IK: stance (planted foot, pelvis dips 2 px → the knee flexes, segment lengths preserved), swing (foot lifted
     4 px and carried → the knee bends more than in stance), unreachable target (clamped, no stretch), bend side kept.
   - Renaming the held item (e.g. `staff` → `horn`) in a case works through data, with no `KeyError`.
   - `joint_continuations.py`: direct `domains()` tests of named-cover clipping, garment-bounds clipping and
     connectivity filtering, each failing when its guard is removed (re-review Low 2).
   - The Seer's rr10/rr13 focused tests, gates and headless runtime checks still PASS, and a byte-identity check over
     its assets and compiled motion.
   - Mutation-test the new guards; report caught vs survived.
