# mq-14 — Frostglass Lancer maquette on the current builder (shoulder rule, true turn, rig-v2 delivery)

## Header
- The Lancer is the last roster-wave-1 enemy without a rig. Its paintings are owner-approved
  (`spec/art_identity/jobs/frostglass_lancer/mq4_front` and `mq4_rear`; inbox `lancer-r4-facing`). They were painted
  over the r3/r4 maquette, which predates three rules:
  - the shoulder rule: in the approved front the shoulders sit level on screen, so the far arm would hang from
    mid-chest once rigged, as the Herald's did before mq-12;

  - the true-turn regression.
- Pipeline:
  1. This unit builds the maquette on the current builder, fitted to the approved mq4 paintings.
  2. The director repaints the approved paintings as edits to the new shoulders, as mq-12 did for the Herald and Gloam.
  3. A later rr unit rigs the Lancer on humanoid rig v2.

  No image generation in this unit.
- References for the builder, gates, delivery and curation: the Herald and Gloam (mq-12, mq-13) and batch C (mq-09).
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark`. Owned:

## Change
1. **A Blender-native spec** from real geometry on the CC0 humanoid armature, in the board's 3/4 facing with the
   facing check passing. Proportions follow the approved paintings.
2. **Shoulder rule** (spec §3): shoulders level in 3D; in the front `joints_px.json` the far shoulder sits
   0.15 × |x_near − x_far| above the near one, ±10 px. Report the before (r4) and after values.
3. **True 180° rear** (mq-06), both feet planted: never a mirror.
4. **Fit gate** against `mq4_front/processed.png` and `mq4_rear/processed.png`:
   - IoU ≥ 0.70 front and ≥ 0.65 rear;
   - every label present where the painting shows it, or an explicit occlusion;
   - per-part coverage, including each arm and leg part.
5. **Delivery in the curated format** (as mq-12/mq-13):
   - `delivery/` with front/rear clays (1024), part maps, solos, `joints_px.json`, manifests and the fit overlay;
   - `COMMIT_MANIFEST.txt` (≤ 6 MB; no `.blend`, caches, candidates or determinism copies);
   - `discarded_proof_sha256.json`;
   - a fresh regeneration that matches the clays and part maps.
6. The determinism, cache replay, ownership and mutation gates, and the hash-bound delivery validator, as mq-12.
