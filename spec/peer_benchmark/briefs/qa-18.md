## Change
1. **Fix at the root.** Find every place a committed or stored plan's `enemy_index` is read after the plan was created. Resolve the living enemy by its stable id (or a value that can't go stale) instead, and don't let a stale index reach anything: previews, threat tiles, badges, or resolution. Whatever a dead enemy's plan was, it must simply be gone. `current_plan` already receives the enemy; use it.
2. **Answer in the report: could this produce wrong behaviour in play, not only a logged error?** Check in-range staleness in previews and in real enemy-turn resolution, with a concrete scenario: three enemies with committed plans, kill the first, then look at the second's threat tiles and at what it does on its turn.
