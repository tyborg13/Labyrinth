3. **Arm swing in `move`.** Implement `arm_swing.model = "shoulder_pendulum"` in the shared gait, read from data:
   - the upper arm rotates about the shoulder joint, in screen space toward the facing direction and away from it,
     ±`upper_deg`, opposite its same-side leg;
   - the forearm follows with a `forearm_lag_s` delay and bends by `forearm_bend_deg.forward` at the forward extreme
     and by `back` at the back extreme;
   - the hand is rigid with the forearm.
   - Gate: the hand's screen path per step is an arc. Its peak-to-peak travel must be at least 2.5× rr-20's slide.
     Report the measured px for both hands in both views.
4. **Hands in front.**
   - Find the clip or clips where, in the front view, a hand or forearm crossing toward the camera side draws behind
     the torso. List each with frame numbers and before crops.
   - **Rule:** in the front view, while a hand's projected box overlaps the torso and its arm is moving toward the
     camera side (the near arm always; the far arm when it crosses the torso midline), the forearm and hand draw above
     the torso.
   - Add a per-frame draw-order gate for it.
5. **Gates:** rr-20's full set plus the canonical tear gate, in both views and every clip. Every other figure stays
   byte-identical.
