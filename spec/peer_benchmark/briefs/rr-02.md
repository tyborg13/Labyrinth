# rr-02 — Maquette → cutout pipeline, phase 1 (chainbound gaoler)

## Header
- The gaoler was repainted over a Blender maquette (`spec/art_identity/maquettes/humanoid.py` +
  `maquettes/chainbound_gaoler/spec.json`, rev r3, framed at the drawn size). Approved-by-judge pair:
  front `spec/art_identity/jobs/chainbound_gaoler/mq3_front_v3/processed.png`, rear `.../mq3_rear_v2/processed.png`
  (255×255 canvas, art_scale 1.0). The maquette was rendered from the same board camera, so its part-label maps, per-part
  solo renders and projected joints (`joints_px.json`) describe exactly the parts the painting shows. Phase 1 turns that
  into a cutout case; phase 2 (after the director paints hidden material) integrates it into the game.
- Maquette renders are regenerable: `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -P
  spec/art_identity/maquettes/humanoid.py -- spec/art_identity/maquettes/chainbound_gaoler/spec.json <out_dir>` (writes
  `{front,rear}_parts.png`, `{front,rear}_clay.png`, `{view}_solo_<part>.png`, `joints_px.json`). Raw paintings (1024 px)
  the processed sprites came from: `output/peer-benchmark-2026-10-12/identity_gate/raw/chainbound_gaoler_mq3_front_v3.png`
  and `..._mq3_rear_v2.png` in the main checkout; each job's `processed.json` records `k_eff`, `fit_scale` and
  `placement`, and `art_identity_gate.process_raw` is the exact raw→canvas transform.
- Base: `codex/peer-benchmark`. Owned files: new `tools/maquette_cutout.py`, new `tests/test_maquette_cutout.py`, new case
  `experiments/cutouts/chainbound_gaoler/mq3/` (created with `tools/cutout_workflow.py`), `spec/cutout_workflow.md`
  (a new "From a maquette" section), `spec/art_identity/maquettes/README.md` (cross-link only). Do not modify production
  `assets/units/chainbound_gaoler_cutout/` or game scripts in phase 1.
- Rules: follow `.codex/skills/create-labyrinth-cutout/SKILL.md` and `spec/cutout_workflow.md` (case format, validate,
  render, verify-render). Never generate or paint art; where paint is missing, produce requests (item 4).

## Change
1. **Registration**: render the maquette (front and rear) and map every render (label maps, solo renders, joints) into the
   255 canvas with the same transform `process_raw` applied to that view's raw painting. Then snap ownership to the
   painting: every opaque painted pixel takes the label of the nearest label-map pixel (Euclidean, ties → the label nearer
   the camera); report per part the share of pixels reassigned this way and flag any part above 15 %.
2. **Case**: `init` a new case `experiments/cutouts/chainbound_gaoler/mq3/` with facings front and rear. Parts = the
   maquette labels (`head, torso, pelvis, belt, tabard, coat_near, coat_far, upper_near, fore_near, hand_near, upper_far,
   fore_far, hand_far, thigh_near, shin_near, foot_near, thigh_far, shin_far, foot_far, chain, hook`); joints from
   `joints_px.json` (registered); draw order per view from the maquette's camera depth. Map bone names to the existing
   production gaoler rig (`assets/units/chainbound_gaoler_cutout/{front,rear}.json`, its motion script) wherever the
   topology matches and record the mapping table in the case; parts with no counterpart keep their maquette names.
   Rigid parts use `segment`; cloth (tabard, coat_near, coat_far) uses `skin` weighted to pelvis/thighs as the existing
   gaoler drapes are.
3. **Rest identity**: the case's rest assembly must reproduce each processed painting pixel-for-pixel (alpha and RGB) at
   the native 255 registration — `validate` plus an explicit equality test.
4. **Hidden material requests**: for each part and view, the region the part's solo render covers but the painting does
   not show (occluded by another part) is hidden material the rig needs when limbs move. Write, per part and view, a
   request image (`hidden_requests/<view>_<part>.png`: the part's visible paint plus the hidden region filled magenta
   #FF00FF on #00FF00, then the same at 4× nearest) and `hidden_requests/index.json` (part, view, hidden pixel count,
   bounding box, which motions in the existing gaoler clips expose it). Order by exposure (most-exposed first). Leave
   magenta in the case for unpainted hidden areas so motion renders show the gaps honestly.
5. **First motion proof**: drive the case with the existing gaoler motion clips through the bone mapping (idle, move,
   attack, hurt, death as available) and `render` the reel; magenta gaps are expected and documented.
6. **Tool**: `tools/maquette_cutout.py` makes 1–4 repeatable for any maquette spec + approved pair (CLI: `--spec`,
   `--front-job`, `--rear-job`, `--case`), with tests on a tiny synthetic maquette fixture (registration transform,
   nearest-label snapping, depth order, hidden-region detection).

## Proof
- `tools/cutout_workflow.py validate` and `verify-render` for the case; `tests/test_maquette_cutout.py`; the rest-identity
  test; full suite unaffected.
- For the director: the rest assemblies (front/rear) with part ownership overlays (one flat colour per part) at 3×; the
  hidden-request contact sheet; the motion reel and its key frames (contact sheet of 8 frames per clip). List exact
  windowed commands if `render` needs a window.

## Out of scope
Production integration (phase 2), painting hidden material, changing the gaoler's motion timing, other enemies.
