# rr-18 — Glacial Mirror on humanoid rig v2

## Header
- Roster wave 1. The Glacial Mirror is a humanoid golem of separate boulders.
  - Maquette: mq-08, with level 3D shoulders and the far pauldron at the real shoulder:
    `spec/art_identity/maquettes/glacial_mirror/` and its `delivery/`.
  - Paintings: the director's repaint from those clays, `spec/art_identity/jobs/glacial_mirror/mq8_front` and
    `mq8_rear` (identity gate PASS).
- **Binding spec:** `spec/peer_benchmark/humanoid_cutout_rig.md`. Reference implementations: rr-10/rr-13 (Seer) and
  rr-11/rr-15/rr-16 (Thrall). Reuse their generic modules (`tools/humanoid_cutout/**`,
  `scripts/maquette_cutout_runtime/humanoid_*.gd`), case-data pattern (rr-14) and gates. That includes the knee gate,
  stride/trailing roles, `view_keys`, the dense reach gate with interval minimum and the direction-coverage gate.
- **Director decision: every piece is rigid.** Stone doesn't bend, so §1's skinned arm and leg meshes don't apply.
  Each boulder is a rigid sprite. Every bend sits under an overlapping boulder, with the covered boulder's painted
  continuation as the tuck (§1 tuck and hidden-fill rules apply in full).
- **Protected director data:** `spec/peer_benchmark/staged/rr-18/motion_tracks.json`. Import it byte for byte.
  Unclear semantics → `TODO_DESIGN_<channel>`, and report it.
- Base `codex/peer-benchmark` after rr-16 and mq-08 have merged and the mq8 paintings are committed.
- Owned:
  - `experiments/cutouts/glacial_mirror/**` and `assets/units/glacial_mirror_cutout/**`;
  - the Mirror's registration, density, rest and shadow records;
  - tests;
  - small generic extensions only if the Mirror needs one the Thrall didn't: listed, tested, and the Seer and Thrall
    byte-identical.
- Keep every other creature byte-identical in rendering.
