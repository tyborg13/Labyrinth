## Change
1. **Cut (spec §1):** `head` (rigid); `torso` with the harness straps (rigid); `mortar`, the
   crucible, a rigid held item, child of `torso` at `mortar_pivot` (between the shoulder blades), with its own
   `angle_deg` channel (spec §3 held-item rules; at most 35° from rest); `pelvis`; `apron` as one skinned cloth chain on
   the pelvis (`apron_mid`, `apron_tip`); per arm one skinned bare arm over upper and fore (the iron bracer rigid with
   the forearm texture) and a rigid fist `hand_<side>`; per leg one skinned leg and a rigid foot. Feature: the molten
   slag surface, owned by `mortar` (both views).
   - Hands and feet are two-bone targets; elbows and knees keep their rest bend side.
   - Knees bend toward the facing (§3). Declare the knee policy per leg and view in the case anatomy.
   - `foot_stride` is the camera-side long leg in each view and `foot_trailing` the other.
   - Rest identity is 0 px against both paintings (source space). The production rest is the density-processed rest.
2. **Idle:** the §2 bob. The upper body is torso, head and both arms (all free arms); the lower body stays still.
3. **Production.** Replace the static Slag Mortar on the board, pre-battle and portraits in both views:

   | Intent | Clip |
   |---|---|
   | `lob_slag` (lob_mark) | `lob`; the existing lob mark at its contact |
   | `slag_lands` (lob_land) | `land`; the existing landing VFX at the marked tile at its contact |
   | `brace` (block, then move_away) | `brace`, then `move` per tile |
   | `scatter_shot` (ranged) | `scatter`; the existing VFX and sound at its contact |
   | hit / death | `hit` / `death`, then the existing fade |

   - `move` plays for every movement step, whatever the intent.
   - Remove `slag_mortar` from the static roster lists, the data-driven way rr-17/rr-19 settled. No new creature-name
     branches in shared code.
4. **Gates:** the Thrall/Mirror set in both views:
   - idle rigidity, clean pieces, seams and stray pixels;
   - signed motion and action direction, with coverage for `lob` and `scatter`;
   - the knee gate on every `move` swing, and supports;
   - the dense reach gate (overreach reported, not failed);
   - no green-dominant texel in any presented frame;
   - native checks.
