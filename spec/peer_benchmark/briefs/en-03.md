## Arc Thrall `arc_thrall`
1. **Spawn group** (`spawn_group: 2` on the definition): whenever a composition contains an arc thrall, it contains an
   even number (the generator adds a second thrall if an odd count results, counting against `max_units` by replacing
   the last concrete unit if needed). Summons/splits are unaffected.
2. **Tether** (`tether_beam`): at intent selection, if another living arc thrall shares a row or column with no
   impassable terrain between them and distance ≤ 6, the beam is telegraphed on every tile strictly between them
   (delayed-mark language is NOT used — this resolves on this activation): draw a violet `#a77bf0` dashed line between
   the two thralls' chest points and threat tiles on the tiles between, plus damage previews on units there. On
   resolution every unit on those tiles takes 4 lightning damage + Shock 1 (hero and enemies). If no partner is aligned,
   the thrall first moves up to 3 tiles to the tile that aligns with a partner and puts the hero between them (or, if
   impossible, just aligns), and the beam resolves from the new position; the telegraph shows the move path and the
   resulting beam tiles. If the partner dies or moves out of line before resolution, the beam fizzles (callout
   "Cancelled" via the existing threats-at-rest callouts if present). Beam animation: a 0.3 s jagged violet beam with
   flicker (reuse the existing lightning chain/strike FX).
3. **Arc Jab**: move 2 + melee 3 + Shock 1.

## Gloam Stalker `gloam_stalker`
4. **Fade** (`veil_blink`, destination `behind_hero`, range 4): the stalker becomes `veiled` and blinks (existing blink
   rift FX, not a walk) to the free tile adjacent to the hero opposite the hero's last move direction this round (if the
   hero didn't move: opposite the direction from the hero to the stalker's start tile); if occupied, the adjacent free
   tile closest to that one; if none within 4, it stays and only veils. While veiled: single-target attacks and cards
   can target it only from an adjacent tile; area effects, surfaces, pushes into it and chains hit normally; its body is
   drawn at 35% alpha with a 2 px violet `#8a6bd6` rim and slow veil wisps; health bar and intent stay visible.
5. **Ambush** (forced next intent): melee 7 against the hero if adjacent (re-blinks behind the hero first, range 4, if
   the hero moved away); veil ends after the strike. Telegraph: the normal melee threat tile on the hero.
6. **Slash**: move 2 + melee 4.
