## Change
1. **Mimic hair.** Replace the radial spikes with:
   - A hair mass covering the skull top and sides down to ear height, about 1.25× the skull width.
   - About 16 tufts:
     - lengths varied from 0.3 to 0.9× head height;
     - pointing up, up-back, out to both sides, and down-back over the nape;
     - a little more volume swept toward the back and the far side, like the hero's.
     - No two tufts share a root point, and nothing is radially even.
   - A fringe of 4 tufts falling forward and down over the top of the mask, covering its top ~20%. The mask's crack
     and glow stay visible.
   - Rear: a rounded, spiky mop. Nothing rises more than 0.5× head height above the crown.
   - Everything below the hairline stays as it is: mask, body, limbs, joints, labels.
2. **Mortar loincloth.** Replace the two flat panels with a loincloth that sits on the body in the 3/4 view:
   - A leather belt band around the pelvis on the hip line, following the body's turn, under the existing harness.
   - A front flap:
     - hung from the belt at the body's front centre;
     - its plane perpendicular to the pelvis forward axis (it turns with the body, not world-aligned);
     - width about 0.5× hip width, length to mid-thigh;
     - curving over the thighs, with a ragged hem (keep the `rag` style).
   - A back flap at the body's rear centre, by the same rule, slightly shorter.
   - Both views come from the same posed body. The rear is the 180° turn (mq-06 rule).
   - Everything else is unchanged.
3. **Gates.**
   - Fit IoU against the original art: ≥ 0.70 front and ≥ 0.65 rear, both figures. Report before and after.
   - Mimic: the hair silhouette's top extent above the crown ≤ 0.5× head height (rear), and the fringe overlaps the mask
     top.
   - Mortar: in both views, each flap's projected centre line is within ±4 px of the pelvis centre line (1024 px).
   - The mq-04 facing, collision, ownership and determinism gates, and the mq-06 turn regression.
   - Every other figure's delivery stays byte-identical.
