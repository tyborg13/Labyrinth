## Storm Capacitor `storm_capacitor` (`immobile`, `construct` → never moves, not pushed, anchored vs wind)
1. **Charge**: +1 Charge, max 3. Draw three pips above its health bar (8 px diamonds, empty = `#3a2f4f` outline,
   filled = `#a77bf0` with 40% glow); pips persist; badge tooltip "Charge <n>/3: at 3 it discharges chain lightning."
2. **Discharge** (chosen automatically when Charge = 3; otherwise Charge): chain lightning: 6 damage to the nearest
   unit within 4 (hero or enemy; ties → the hero), then jumps to the nearest unit adjacent (8 tiles) to the last one
   struck that hasn't been struck, up to 3 jumps, −1 damage per jump (6, 5, 4, 3); Charge resets to 0. Telegraph at
   rest when Charge = 3 (its next activation): a dashed violet chain path through the predicted targets with damage
   numbers; the prediction updates with previews (pushes, kills). Animation: 0.12 s per hop, jagged violet arc
   (reuse the lightning chain FX).
3. **Grounded** (passive): whenever it becomes Shocked, it loses 1 Charge (pips update; floating text "Grounded").

## Hollow Mimic `hollow_mimic`
4. **Mirror** (`mirror_last_attack`): at intent selection, look at the last card the hero played this combat that
   dealt damage (an "attack card"); the intent becomes "Mirror: <card name>" with that card's base damage (no relic,
   status or upgrade bonuses), the card's targeting shape aimed at the hero from the mimic's position, range = the
   card's range (if the hero is out of range, the mimic moves up to 2 first; still out of range → Shade Bolt). Element
   of the card applies (surface/status riders are NOT copied — damage and shape only). No attack card played yet →
   Shade Bolt. The intent pill/compass shows the card's attack icon; tooltip "Mirror: it copies the last attack you
   played — <card>: <damage> damage."
5. **Shade Bolt**: ranged 4, range 4. **Recede**: move away 2 + Block 4.
