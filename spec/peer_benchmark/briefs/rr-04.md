## Change
1. **Generic semantic motion compiler** for maquette cutouts: per-part channels map to that part's bone pivoting at its
   maquette joint, with per-view signs derived from the 3D maquette axes (as the shrike does for its channels) so
   "raise" and "lean" mean the same thing front and rear. `body.*` drives the root. Support `alpha` (body and part),
   `squash` with area-preserving width, `forward_mode: fraction_of_step` (board step distance), `loop`, `terminal`,
   `contact` (the action's gameplay contact moment, used for sound/VFX sync exactly like the shrike's dive contact).
2. **Both rigs** from their maquettes + approved paintings: ownership masks from the maquette part renders, hidden
   material requests where parts overlap (same process as the shrike), rest identity 0 px vs the approved processed
   art. Lightning Wisp: veins (`arc_left`, `arc_right`, `crown`) are painted on the cloud — rig them as part of `core`.
3. **Production:** replace the current board art for `bile_bloomer` (Shale Bloomer) and `lightning_wisp` with the new
   rigs; map intents to clips exactly as listed in each clip's `intents` (e.g. `bile_burst:move_toward` → move, then
   `bile_burst:aoe` → burst). Pre-battle (pb-01 may land first) and portraits use the same renderer. Register density,
   rest records and shadow cache as rr-03/qa-06 did.
4. **Real-time review video tool** (`tools/maquette_cutout_review.py` or similar): renders each clip natively at 60 fps
   and writes one MP4 per creature: front (left) | rear (right), 2× nearest-neighbour, dark background #1E1E24, clip
   name top-centre, idle ×3, move ×3, other actions ×2, death once + 1 s hold. (Director's prototype:
   `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-borgerding-workspace-Labyrinth/020b9ac4-7b92-4113-a7d2-bc6e802828dd/scratchpad/shrike_rt_compose.py`.)
5. **Proof gates** (as qa-06 hardened): bone-level signed motion at authored keys + midpoints, both views; production
   capture from real clip progression with ≥6 changing frames per action incl. contact; mutation regressions (flip a
   raise sign, drop a key, freeze idle phase) must fail.
