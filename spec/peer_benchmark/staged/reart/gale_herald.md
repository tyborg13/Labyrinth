Gale Herald: a tall, masked herald with a big blank brass mask for a face, wrapped in heavy ragged grey-blue cloth
banners; two or three broad banner strips stream sideways to the viewer's right as if in a gale (each strip at least
6 blocks wide, ragged ends). It holds a long, thick brass horn raised in its near hand. Stocky limbs, big hands and
boots. Accent: pale storm grey-blue (#8ea3b8) on the banners' worn insignia only; brass is a worn material colour.
