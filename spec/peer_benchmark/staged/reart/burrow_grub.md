Burrow Grub: a hound-sized segmented grub, fat and heavy. Five or six thick body rings of dusky ochre-brown chitin,
each ring a single broad plate with a dark band between rings; the front end ends in a round lamprey mouth ringed
with a few big hooked teeth (each tooth at least 2 blocks wide), turned toward the viewer's lower-left; two pairs of
short stubby digging claws under the front rings. Accent: dull amber (#b98b45) only inside the mouth rim and on the
plate edges. Values dark and earthy, no pale or cream skin.
