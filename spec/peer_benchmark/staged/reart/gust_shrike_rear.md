Gust Shrike, seen from BEHIND (the same bird as the approved front image, the first reference): the same low,
nearly horizontal body posture as the front, now pointing toward the viewer's UPPER-RIGHT, away from the camera. We see
its back and rump; the long fanned ragged tail is nearest to the viewer at the lower-left. The two wings are swept back
and raised together into the same single tall crescent, seen from behind. Its head is turned away toward the
upper-right: we see the back of the skull, with at most a sliver of the beak and the dark mask band. Thick scaled legs
with big talons under the body. Exactly the front's dusky storm-grey, grey-tan and dark brown plumage, the same
grey-brown legs, and pale storm grey-blue only on the leading wing edge. NOT an upright perched pose, NOT a side
profile.
