Slag Mortar: a squat, heavy brute with a huge chest and short thick legs, sooty dark skin and a scorched leather
apron. On its back it carries a soot-black clay kiln shaped like a short mortar barrel, tilted forward over its LEFT shoulder (the creature's own left; viewer's right in the front
view); the kiln's open mouth holds glowing molten slag (molten orange #e8722c is the one accent, only in the
bowl). Big hands, thick forearms with iron bands, a small brutish head low between the shoulders.
