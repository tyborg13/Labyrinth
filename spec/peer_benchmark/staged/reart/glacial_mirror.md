Glacial Mirror: a hulking, top-heavy construct of dark frost-rimed stone with massive shoulders and fists and short
thick legs; set into its chest is one large flat slab of cracked ice like a mirror (pale frost blue #9cc8e6 is the
one accent, in the slab only), angled so we see its face in three-quarter view. A small stone head sunk between the
shoulders. A few broad frost patches; no thin icicles or crystals.
