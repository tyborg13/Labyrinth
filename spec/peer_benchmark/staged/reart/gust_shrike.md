Gust Shrike: a stocky storm-grey raptor the size of a large dog, standing on the ground with wings half-raised; broad
wings made of a few big tattered feather masses (no individual thin feathers), a heavy hooked iron-grey beak, thick
scaled legs with big talons. Head toward the viewer's lower-left. Accent: pale storm grey-blue (#8ea3b8) on the wing
edges only. Dark, dusky plumage.
