Gust Shrike: a lean, fast storm shrike the size of a large dog, standing low on the ground with its body held
nearly horizontal and pointed toward the viewer's lower-left. Its two wings are swept back and raised together into
ONE tall backward crescent shape behind the body (not a symmetric V, not spread to both sides). A long, fanned,
ragged tail trails toward the upper-right. A short heavy hooked iron-grey beak and a bold dark mask band across the
eyes (the shrike's signature). Wings built from three or four big feather masses, no individual thin feathers. Thick
scaled legs with big talons. Dusky storm-grey and dark brown plumage; pale storm grey-blue (#8ea3b8) only on the
leading wing edge.
