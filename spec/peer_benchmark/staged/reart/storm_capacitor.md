Storm Capacitor: a squat, weathered arcane pylon. A thick corner-on plinth of soot-stained dark stone, three blocks
high, with heavy iron staples at its corners. On it, a thick upright cylinder of cloudy dark glass held by three broad
dull, tarnished dark-copper bands with verdigris; a few bold violet lightning strokes trapped inside the glass (violet
#a77bf0 is the one accent). A domed, soot-blackened iron cap with one short thick spike. Grimy, old, dungeon-worn — no
clean or shiny surfaces, no chains, no cables, no thin parts.
