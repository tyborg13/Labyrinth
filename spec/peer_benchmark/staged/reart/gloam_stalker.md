Gloam Stalker: a hunched shade wrapped in heavy torn black veils that hang in a few big ragged masses; a pale, blank
masklike face with dark eye holes; its forearms end in two broad, slightly curved bone blades (each at least 4 blocks
wide). Stocky, crouched and predatory — heavy cloth mass, not thin limbs. Accent: dusky violet (#8a6bd6) as a faint
glow inside the eye holes and along the veils' torn edges only.
