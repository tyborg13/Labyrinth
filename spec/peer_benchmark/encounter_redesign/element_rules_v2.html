Escape the Umbra · encounter design · proposal, revision 2 · 23 October 2026

One rule per element

Each element is a force that reshapes the board on a beat, the way Wind does. Each fires on everyone, you and them alike. Each shows its result a round ahead, so you can step clear and set enemies up for it. Each is built only from things the game already has.

Revision 2, from your note: revision 1 named the primitives (Fire ground, Ice ground) instead of rules. Every rule below says what happens on the beat, what you see beforehand, and how you play it.

What makes Wind work

Five things, and every rule below has all five.

A beat

It fires at a set moment you can count on.

After each enemy turn, or after yours in the Snare.

Everyone

It moves enemies exactly as it moves you.

Only Anchored units stay put.

Shown ahead

You see the result before it happens.

A ghost stands where each unit will land.

Two ways to play

Step clear yourself, and set them up to take it.

Shove a Shrike upwind of a pillar and let the gust slam it.

Old pieces

A new rule made from existing parts.

Push, Collision, Anchored.

The six beats

The wielder is never hit by its own beat. Everyone else is, including its own allies.

Air

The Gust

On the beat, everything not Anchored slides with the wind, farthest downwind first. A slide that's blocked is a Collision: 2 damage per tile lost, to the unit and to what stopped it.

Built from Push, Collision, Anchored. Built today as Wind.

You see
A ghost of every unit where the gust will set it down, and the streams flowing downwind.
Stay safe
Don't stand upwind of a wall, a unit or a hazard. Keep a clear lane downwind.
Set them up
Shove enemies upwind of walls, each other, Fire or a trap, and let the gust finish the job.
Counter
Anchored. A clear lane downwind.
Fire

Wildfire

On the beat, every Fire tile spreads one tile in the blaze's direction. A unit the fire reaches takes 2 at once and 3 at the start of each turn it stays. Ice and stone don't catch.

Built from Fire ground and its damage. The fire Snare already spreads this way.

You see
The tiles that catch next, lit faintly at their edges.
Stay safe
Stay ahead of the front, or behind a firebreak.
Set them up
Push enemies onto the tiles about to catch. Your own Fire spreads on the same beat, so a Fire card on their side of the board grows into them.
Counter
Firebreaks: Ice ground or stone in the fire's path. Fireproof.
Ice

Hard Frost

On the beat, the frost strikes every Ice tile. Anything standing on Ice is Frozen: it skips its next turn and takes triple damage until then. The Ice under each frozen unit is used up.

Built from Ice ground (Chilled while on it), an Ice hit Freezes the Chilled, Freeze.

You see
The Ice rimes white the round before the frost strikes, and the beat sits on the turn clock.
Stay safe
Be off the Ice when it strikes.
Set them up
Push or pull enemies onto Ice just before the beat, then hit them for triple damage. Your Ice cards lay more Ice for the frost to use.
Counter
Fire melts Ice. Stay off it.
Lightning

The Strike

On the beat, lightning strikes the network. Everyone on a connected Electrified tile is hit and Shocked, and the hit jumps on to anyone touching someone it hit.

Built from Electrified ground, Chain, Shock.

You see
The network crackles a round before. Everyone the strike would reach is outlined, chains included.
Stay safe
Stay off the network, and out of reach of anyone on it.
Set them up
Shove enemies onto the network, or next to each other on it, so one strike runs through the whole pack. Your Lightning spreads through the same network.
Counter
Cut the network by laying other ground on one of its tiles. Stand apart.
Earth

Upheaval

On the beat, the marked tiles rise as stone. Anything standing there is thrown 1 tile off it, and a blocked throw is a Collision. The stone blocks movement and sight until it's broken, and broken stone leaves Rubble.

Built from Raise Terrain (Outcrops), Push, Collision, Rubble.

You see
Cracks on the marked tiles, and a ghost where each thrown unit lands.
Stay safe
Step off the line. Or stand where the new wall cuts their ranged lines to you.
Set them up
Push enemies onto the line so it throws them into each other, or so the wall closes them off from their friends.
Counter
Break the stone; it has Health.
Shadow

Eclipse

On the beat, the dark strikes every unit outside Light, enemies too. Between beats, whatever stands in the dark is hidden: no intent, and it can't be targeted.

Built from the Umbra, Light, and the Last Eclipse hit (Light, Vision and Truesight protect you).

You see
The edge of every Light, with the beat counting down on the turn clock.
Stay safe
Be in Light when it strikes.
Set them up
Drive enemies into the dark before the beat. It hits them there, even though you can't see or target them. Pull them back into Light when you want them in reach.
Counter
Light: braziers, your Light cards, Vision and Truesight.

They interlock too: the Gust shoves into Fire, onto Ice and onto the network; Upheaval throws units the same way; Ice is a firebreak, and Fire melts Ice. A deck built around one element is also built to answer it.

What each fight changes

The rule never changes. A fight only sets its dials: who wields it, what shape and direction it takes, how often it fires, how big it gets, and how it ends.

Warband

The captain wields the beat and aims it at you. It fires every round or every other round, and stops when the captain falls.

Umbral Snare

The room wields it, always from the entry, against your escape. It grows every round, and stops when you leave.

Guardian

One dial is set unusually, always on and shown on its boss bar. Its two helpers feed that twist.

Dragon

Every dial goes up across the acts. The cataclysm is the beat at full scale, and you can Break it.

The Ember Hoarder and Last Light stay on hold until you've played the Warband and the Snare. When they're built, they take their row from this table the same way.

Shown the same way, every time

  • The beat is on the turn clock. One token per beat, sitting between turns exactly where it fires, like the Stormcaller's wind today.
  • The result is drawn a round ahead, in one language: ghosts for anything that moves (Gust, Upheaval throws), and lit edges for tiles about to catch, rise, freeze or strike.
  • Six callouts, total. The first time you meet a beat, one short card explains it. Bosses don't need their own; their pre-battle names their beat and their twist.

What changes

  • Retired: Bramble, Ice Pillar and Tether, and the Guardians' custom rules (Gallows, Crag Armour, Vigil). Six named beats replace them.
  • Built work: five Warband orders change (all but the Stormcaller) and four Snare pressures (all but fire and air). Each is a small change on top of what's built.
  • Kept from the boss proposals: Wrath at half health, a cataclysm you can Break, finite Guardian helpers, the next-three intent strip, and Cantor and Roc at their designed pace.
