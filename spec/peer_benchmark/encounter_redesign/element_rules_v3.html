Escape the Umbra · encounter design · proposal, revision 3 · 23 October 2026

One rule per element

Each element is one battlefield rule that fires on a beat, the way the Gust does. Each is one sentence made only of things the game already has, with no exceptions written into it. Fights stay distinct by turning a few dials: what feeds the rule, the pattern it lays, which way it goes and how often it fires.

Revision 3, from your notes. Wildfire loses "Ice and stone don't catch": Fire just replaces what's there, as every elemental tile already does. Hard Frost is now Frostbite, and the Strike is now Lightning Storm, whose reach comes from Chain instead of a new touching rule. Eclipse is replaced by Shadowstep. I also dropped my blanket "the wielder is never hit" rule and the dragons' Break meter: both were rules of their own.

The test every beat passes

One sentence, no exceptions

It reads the same in every fight. If a fight needs an exception, the design is wrong.

Only existing pieces

Push, Fire ground, Chilled, Freeze, Electrified ground, Chain, Outcrops, the Umbra, Blink. Nothing new to learn underneath.

Something to play around

You step clear of it, and you set enemies up so it hits them, the way you shove a Shrike upwind of a pillar.

The six beats

Each plate gives the sentence the game shows, the pieces it's made of, how you play it, and the dials a fight can set.

Air

Gust

On the beat, the wind Pushes every unit 1 tile downwind.

PushCollisionAnchored
Step clear
Don't stand upwind of a wall, a unit or a hazard.
Set up
Shove enemies upwind of walls, each other, Fire or a trap, and let the gust finish the job.
Dials
Which way it blows (fixed, aimed, turning, toward its source) · how far · how often · what calls it.
Fire

Wildfire

On the beat, each Fire tile sets the next tile in the blaze's direction on Fire.

Fire groundplacing ground replaces ground
Step clear
Stay ahead of the front. Fire replaces whatever ground it lands on, so nothing stops it but distance and time.
Set up
Push enemies onto the tiles about to catch. Your own Fire spreads on the same beat, so a Fire card on their side grows into them.
Dials
What lays the Fire (marks, trails, the entry) · which way it spreads (toward you, away from the entry, all four ways) · how often · how long Fire lasts.
Ice

Frostbite

On the beat, an Ice hit strikes every Ice tile, Freezing whoever stands on one.

Ice ground: ChilledIce hit Freezes the ChilledFreeze
Step clear
Be off the Ice when it strikes. Your Fire replaces Ice like any other ground.
Set up
Push or pull enemies onto Ice just before the beat, then hit them for triple damage. Freezing uses up the Ice under them, as an Ice hit always does.
Dials
What lays the Ice (Icefall, trails, Spitters, thresholds) · the pattern · how often it strikes · how hard.
Lightning

Lightning Storm

On the beat, lightning strikes every Electrified tile; some storms Chain on from each hit.

Electrified groundLightning StrikesChainShock
Step clear
Be off the network when it strikes. If the storm Chains, don't stand near anyone on it.
Set up
Push enemies onto the network before the beat. Between beats, their network is yours: your Lightning spreads through it to every enemy on it.
Dials
What lays the network (Thralls, Skybreak, Bell Tenders) · how often it strikes · damage · Chain 0 to 3.
Earth

Upheaval

On the beat, the marked tiles rise as Outcrops, throwing off whoever stands there.

Raise Terraindisplace, as Erupt doesCollisionRubble
Step clear
Step off the marks, or stand where the new wall cuts their lines of fire to you.
Set up
Push enemies onto the marks so they're thrown into each other, or walled away from their friends. Break the stone (it has Health) and it leaves Rubble.
Dials
The pattern (a line, a ring, a lane) · where it's marked · how often · the stone's Health.
Shadow

Shadowstep

On the beat, every enemy hidden in the dark Blinks toward you.

The Umbra: hiddenBlinklanding triggers the ground
Step clear
Keep enemies in sight. One you can see doesn't step, so Light, Vision and Truesight pin them where they are.
Set up
Lay Fire, Ice or network around you before the beat, and let the dark deliver them onto it. Blink only lands on tiles you can see, so they always arrive where you can hit them.
Dials
How dark it is (sight radius, Light sources) · how far they Blink · how often.

Everything follows the primitive as it stands: Fire replaces Ice and Ice replaces Fire. The Gust shoves units onto Fire, Ice or network, and Upheaval throws them the same way. The Stormcaller stays put because a Gale Herald is Anchored, not because of a special rule.

Always on screen

Each fight shows its rule in the panel above the objective: an icon, the name, and its one sentence with this fight's dials filled in. It's the panel the Stormcaller's wind already uses. The beat sits on the turn clock where it fires, and its result is drawn a round ahead: ghosts for anything that moves or Blinks, and lit edges for tiles about to catch, freeze, strike or rise.

The first time you meet a rule, one short card explains it. That's six cards for the whole game.

What gets simpler

  • Six sentences. One per element, the same in every fight, each made of pieces you already know.
  • Retired: Bramble, Ice Pillar and Tether as keywords; the Guardians' custom rules (Gallows, Crag Armour, Vigil); the dragons' Break meter; any "the wielder is immune" rule.
  • Distinct fights come from dials, never from exceptions: what feeds the rule, the pattern, the direction, the beat, the numbers.

What it changes in built work

  • Warband: the Slagmaster's Fire spreads; the Rime Oracle's Icefall leaves Ice and Frostbite strikes it; the Dynamo's Thralls lay network; the Thornmother raises Outcrops; the Prior's hidden followers Shadowstep. The Stormcaller stays as it is.
  • Snare: the doors' Ice gets Frostbite; the Thralls' tethers become network; the shortest way out gets Upheaval; the shadow Snare gets Shadowstep. Fire and air stay as they are.
  • Ordinary enemies follow the same pieces: the Briar Weaver raises Outcrops, the Seer's Icefall leaves Ice, and Arc Thralls lay network.
