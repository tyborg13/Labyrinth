## Design and runtime contract

The Grave Surgeon keeps its wrapped plague-doctor mask, brown hood and robe, bone saw, green vial and belt kit. The editable case is `experiments/cutouts/grave_surgeon/v01`. Its two painted views and their reflections supply four directions. Production owns the selected layouts and paint in `assets/units/grave_surgeon_cutout` and the sampler, rig, renderer and saw feedback in `scripts/grave_surgeon_cutout`. It never loads experiment or tool files. Layout JSON is included in all export presets; PNGs use raw keep imports.

Each actor has one persistent 512px canvas and two loaded rigs around the existing 255px logical body. The existing 0.98 art scale, -14px horizontal art offset, tile selection, HUD anchors and dedicated turn-clock portrait remain. The shared enemy-facing policy points idle toward the player's completed tile; walks face their path, attacks face their target, and support faces its recipient. Pending player movement does not prematurely turn observers. Destination echoes reuse the actor's texture. Hidden actors stop animation work; death freezes and dissolves the same texture before releasing only that actor's renderer. Reduced motion uses the assembled rest art with the same facing policy. Shadows use a cached neutral silhouette without per-frame GPU readback.

The surgeon has its own 19-joint skeleton, with separate upper arms, forearms, wrists, rigid hands/tools, thighs, shins and feet. Each view has 19 paint owners and eight meshes, including a pelvis layer beneath the robe. The chest and shoulder drape belong to the torso. The lower robe is separate from the legs; concealed sleeve and trouser material fills their articulated coverage. Hand/tool paint ownership and invariant source-space grip offsets keep the saw and vial attached throughout the cycle.

Idle translates the complete upper body together by at most 1.2 source pixels over 1.8 seconds. It adds no local rotation, scale or skew; counter-translation fixes all leg transforms. Walking uses 56px stride, 62% support and 90.3226px source travel per 0.68-second cycle, with a 5px passing-foot lift. Phase follows actual route distance. Rigid soles remain parallel to the board and their world support points stay fixed during stance.

The 0.70-second saw action has a small pullback, one forward jab at 42%, a shallow toothed rake and recovery. The rear wrist turns its down-carried blade toward the upper-right target. This action has its own pose and compact scrape effect; the feet stay planted. The existing melee result boundary maps to the authored contact pose. Triage and Field Brace use restrained 0.48-second vial-hand responses while their existing result popup plays. Support effects identify the recipient with `actor_key` and the surgeon with `source_actor_key`; only the source performs the gesture. A self-heal fallback keeps its existing outcome.
