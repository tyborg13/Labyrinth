# Brainstorming: Transformational Relics and Radiance Progression

Status: exploratory future-game idea; not approved for implementation

This note records possible design directions, not a final feature set, balance
contract, or commitment to the example mechanics and values below.

## Transformational Relics and Build Synergy

Many current relics follow a useful condition-to-reward pattern. That
archetype should remain available, but it should not define the entire relic
pool. A broader direction is to make more relics run-bound modifiers that
transform an existing ability or gameplay package, changing how it can be
built around rather than only increasing its numerical rewards.

Abilities are permanent across runs while relics belong to the current run.
Even with that difference in duration, they should share a design language:
both can establish recognizable packages, and run-specific relics can remix,
bridge, or redirect the permanent abilities the player brings into a run. The
goal is surprising package combinations and richer interplay between systems,
not simply larger bonuses for satisfying familiar conditions.

### Illusion Explorations

Possible Illusion-focused relic transformations include:

- Making illusions respond differently to melee hits and ranged hits, so the
  enemy's attack type changes the value or behavior of the decoy.
- Making illusions explode like elemental traps when triggered or destroyed,
  connecting the Illusion package to elemental and trap packages.
- Giving illusions their own Umbra vision, allowing their placement to affect
  what can be seen in darkness as well as how enemies choose targets.

These are examples of different directions, not a proposal to put every effect
on one relic or to adopt any particular trigger and tuning.

### Blink Exploration

A possible Blink-focused relic could allow up to two additional tiles of
range, at a cost of one health for each additional tile used. This would make
Blink more than a stronger movement action: it would connect mobility to
low-health and self-damage packages and ask the player how much life to trade
for position in a specific moment.

The exact range cap, health cost, eligibility, and stacking rules remain open
design questions.

## Radiance Versus Umbra Progression

Deepening Umbra is compelling pressure, but players do not yet have enough
broad, reliable ways to build counterplay. A player who misses a narrow group
of dedicated light cards can be overwhelmed, while taking too many specialized
light cards can dilute the rest of the deck.

As a future design umbrella, **Radiance** can encompass:

- increasing vision;
- gaining true vision or true sight;
- creating light;
- clearing Umbra from affected spaces; and
- lowering the room's Umbra level.

Treating these as related Radiance tools would create multiple strengths and
forms of darkness counterplay rather than making one dedicated light-card
package the only dependable answer.

### Possible Future Pass

A broader Radiance pass could explore several complementary progression
sources:

- More run relics that reward or modify light, vision, true sight, and Umbra
  reduction. Example directions include conditionally lowering room Umbra or
  granting an always-active plus-one vision.
- A Radiance or light path in the permanent ability tree, giving players a way
  to develop darkness counterplay across runs.
- More cards, including attacks, with smaller Radiance riders. These cards
  could advance a normal combat plan while also contributing a modest amount
  of vision, light, or Umbra control.

The desired outcome is that players can steadily assemble darkness counterplay
without diluting their deck or overcommitting to a narrow light-card package.
Umbra should remain threatening; the opportunity is to give that threat a
broader and more interesting set of counters, not to make it automatic or
irrelevant.

## Open Design Questions

Before any implementation is approved, a future design pass would still need
to resolve:

- which transformations belong on relics versus permanent abilities or cards;
- how these effects stack and how often they can appear in a run;
- whether Radiance needs player-facing terminology beyond the existing vision,
  true sight, light, and Umbra language; and
- how to preserve the danger of Umbra while making counterplay sufficiently
  broad and reliable.
