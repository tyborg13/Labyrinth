# Wishlist cut — editorial contract

The promise is tactile card-driven tactics in an atmospheric labyrinth: position enemies, make the room do damage, illuminate danger, and build a stronger run. The first six seconds must communicate a real card action and a visible tactical result without sound.

Valve recommends predominantly gameplay from the player’s perspective, retaining useful HUD elements, and communicating quickly because a viewer may decide within ten seconds or watch muted. Steam’s microtrailers sample the first store video at multiple points, so meaningful game imagery should occupy most of the cut. Source: [Steamworks trailer guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer).

The visual baseline is informed by official store media for [Shogun Showdown](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2084000/Shogun_Showdown/), [Alina of the Arena](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1668690/Alina_of_the_Arena/), and [Into the Breach](https://store.steampowered.com/app/590380/Into_the_Breach/). The useful comparison is clarity: strong actor silhouettes, an immediately identifiable action control, and an outcome that remains readable at store-player size. This is an editorial assessment, not a claim of measured conversion or equal commercial quality.

Source cut points and sound cues live in `SHOTS` in `src/Trailer.tsx`. `sourceCue` maps each source event through its source trim and playback speed to an edit frame. The initial pass was aligned with dense freshly encoded source frames; recheck after further game-animation changes. Frame counts in `START` account for the two small dissolves.

Review the source before composition for clipped HUD, hidden option headers, premature room changes, readable card commitment, correct combat results, and complete equipment destination. Inspect full-resolution master frames at every action, frames on both sides of each boundary, a complete contact sheet, and normal-speed playback with sound. Repeat muted at roughly 800×450 to check the store-page reading experience. Confirm 1920×1080/30 fps, H.264/AAC stereo, no black gaps, no frozen padding, and no audio clipping.
