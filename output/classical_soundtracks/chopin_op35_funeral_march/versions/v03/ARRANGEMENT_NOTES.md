# v03 arrangement notes — melody-first death loop

## User feedback addressed

v02 delayed its most interesting material and suppressed too much of the source melody. v03 preserves v01 and v02, but changes the audition order to:

1. **B — source measures 93–98:** opens immediately on the former second march excerpt.
2. **A — source measures 85–86:** moves the small stripped march excerpt between B and the Trio.
3. **C — source measures 49–53:** retains the Trio release.

## Melody restoration without the famous motif

B restores all 28 `Grave Cello / Funeral Cantus` lead events from source measures 93–98 at 82% of their v01 MIDI velocity. Its supporting Ashen Violin, Hollow Viola, and bass are also closer to their v01 dynamics. The eight Veiled Violin unison doubles remain suppressed so the restored line is expressive without becoming oversized.

B does not contain the famous opening/reprise sequence. The deterministic builder rejects that exact onset/pitch signature on every v03 melodic track.

A remains intentionally stripped: both the Grave Cello lead and Veiled Violin double are absent, leaving a brief inner-harmony descent. That passage now functions as contrast and connective tissue instead of an opening that players must wait through.

## Revised transitions

- A one-beat loop pre-roll duplicates B's quiet opening sonority and is consumed by the render crossfade, so audible playback starts directly with B.
- **B → A:** two beats. The restored lead resolves on the first beat; the second thins to A's inner B-flat-minor sonority.
- **A → C:** two beats. A's inner voices decay while D-flat is held as a common tone; the Trio's upper tone and bass enter softly as a pickup.
- **C → B:** two beats. The Trio resonance falls away, then the B opening sonority returns inside the one-beat loop overlap.

All bridge pitches are asserted against adjacent selected v01 windows. There are no new pitch classes, transpositions, octave doublings, or reharmonizations.

## Sound and duration

The canonical `classical_dark_fantasy_v1` bank is unchanged. Percussion is even lighter than v02: one subdued opening drum, two bone pivots, and widely spaced ash breaths. The MIDI is 59 beats at fixed 66 QPM; after its one-beat overlap, the intended audible loop is approximately 52.727 seconds.

v03 remains an audition only. It is not approved, promoted, or routed to the game.
