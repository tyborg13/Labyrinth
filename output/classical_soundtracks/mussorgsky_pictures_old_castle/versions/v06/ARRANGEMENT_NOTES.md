# Arrangement notes - The Old Castle main-menu v06

## Controlled comparison with v04

- v06 removes the v05 intro completely and returns to v04's 62-measure, 132.857-second structural loop at 84 QPM.
- All five source-derived v04 tracks retain the same event count, onset, gate, pitch, velocity, and voice assignment.
- The v04 four-hit 6/8 percussion pattern is restored event-for-event, including all 248 pitches, onsets, gates, and velocities.

## Audible low-mid Umbra veil

- `Umbra Veil / Low-Mid G-sharp-D-sharp Breath` retains v05's seven long phrase arcs but moves them one octave higher to G-sharp3 and D-sharp4, where they can be heard separately from the bass foundation.
- The G-sharp spans each source-derived phrase; a quieter D-sharp enters later to create a slow density swell. These remain established tonic and dominant pitch classes from the piece and add no countermelody.
- The veil uses the canonical `hollow_viola` sample, 1.2-second attack and 1.4-second release, centered pan, slow restrained vibrato, and 0.22 render gain. Its MIDI velocities range from 24-40, compared with v05's 14-25 at 0.10 gain.
- This is deliberately audible enough to judge. If its color works but its level is too strong, the next change can be a single gain adjustment without touching the successful v04 foundation.
