# Active Tactical Loop - Version 2 Notes

## Purpose

This version tries all three proposed follow-up directions while preserving every version-1 artifact unchanged:

1. condense the most active material into a 4-6 minute tactical loop;
2. render it through a legally clean, project-generated SNES-style sample bank with restrained echo;
3. make the cello more threatening while thinning the two upper voices.

The structural MIDI lasts 4:31.3. The seamless audio loop lasts 4:28.7 because one four-quarter measure is overlapped in the final crossfade.

## Musical transformations

- Tempo remains a steady 92 QPM.
- The MIDI has five monophonic musical tracks and no percussion.
- Violin I retains every selected source note.
- Violin II retains every selected source note, including the active inner motion on both passes.
- Upper velocities are reduced by 6 and 9 units respectively, and their gates are shortened to 80% and 74%.
- Viola retains every selected event at slightly reduced velocity and 94% gates.
- Cello retains every selected event, gains 10 velocity units, and receives gates up to 108% subject to monophony.
- The bass shadow follows eligible arranged cello notes exactly one octave lower. No independent bass line was composed.
- No new pitch class, percussion part, countermelody, or replacement harmony was introduced.

No selected upper-string event is removed. “Thinner” now means quieter, shorter-gated, lower-gain, and darker in timbre while preserving all 953 Violin I and 792 Violin II events. All 406 cello events are retained; 396 are low enough to receive the octave-below shadow.

## Procedural sample bank

`procedural_bank/` contains five mono 16 kHz WAV samples generated by `scripts/build_active_tactical_loop.py`:

- veiled violin;
- ashen violin;
- hollow viola;
- grave cello;
- undercrypt bass.

Each WAV contains a synthesized bowed attack followed by a sample loop. The generator uses only mathematical sinusoidal partials, deterministic integer phase seeds, a periodic high-harmonic bow texture, and 9-11-bit signal quantization. It does not use a recording, ROM extraction, commercial sample, SoundFont, sample pack, or model-generated audio.

During rendering, these stored WAVs are pitch-shifted and looped like a small console-era sample bank. Track-specific vibrato, velocity response, release envelopes, panning, and gain are applied after sample playback. A five-sample moving-average reconstruction filter removes brittle high-frequency imaging from the low-rate pitch shifts while preserving their grain. This is an SNES-inspired renderer, not bit-exact SPC700/BRR hardware emulation.

See `PROCEDURAL_BANK_PROVENANCE.md` and `procedural_bank/bank_manifest.json` for provenance, generator parameters, loop coordinates, and SHA-256 hashes.

## Restrained echo and loop seam

The renderer adds a dark cross-stereo echo after the dry mix:

- 96 ms at gain 0.120;
- 192 ms at gain 0.052;
- 288 ms at gain 0.022;
- a nine-sample moving-average darkening filter before the taps.

The echo is circular so its quiet tail wraps naturally instead of being chopped at the loop point. A one-measure, 2.609-second equal-power overlap blends the final A return into the opening A. The audio is then rotated by that measure, placing the blended seam at the end of the file. Consequently, the Ogg/FLAC begins one measure later than the structural MIDI and lasts 412 rather than 416 quarters.

The verifier decodes each delivered container and measures its actual first-to-last sample delta rather than trusting the pre-encode mix. With the complete upper-string motion restored, the decoded FLAC deltas are approximately 0.000244 left and 0.000488 right; lossy Vorbis raises them to approximately 0.007078 left and 0.009120 right. Both remain below ordinary adjacent-sample changes of approximately 0.029. The Ogg/FLAC files can therefore be looped directly without adding another crossfade. The structural MIDI retains explicit `LOOP_START`, section, and `LOOP_END` markers; a MIDI-based engine should reproduce the documented one-measure crossfade itself.

## Rebuild

From this POC directory, after installing `requirements.txt` in Python 3.12+:

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/build_active_tactical_loop.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_active_tactical_loop.py
```

Pass `--skip-audio` to generate only the version-2 MIDI and procedural bank. Rebuilding version 2 does not write any version-1 file.

## Audition caveats

- The chosen joins are intentionally compact and must be judged by ear for long-session comfort.
- Procedural looped strings have less bow and articulation nuance than a commissioned live sample bank.
- The public-domain score check remains structural and representative rather than a scholarly critical-edition audit.
- The current task does not wire this loop into Godot or choose where it should play.
