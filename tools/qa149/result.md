- **`scripts/post_combat_reward_sequence.gd`** owned the interrupted playback. On the base, line 148 created the unfurl Tween bound to the run-side VICTORY label. Line 153 retained the Tween in the coroutine waiting on its `finished` signal. Freeing the bound label could discard the Tween without emitting that signal.
- **`scripts/run_scene.gd`** owned the suspended callers. On the base, line 15276 awaited the static victory sequence, line 22420 awaited the victory wrapper during card resolution and line 21336 awaited card resolution from the confirm handler. The verbose output groups the surviving states under the interrupted wait chain. Dropping the strong Tween reference in isolation eliminated the Tween reports and left two function states per interrupted win. Explicitly unwinding the sequence while the scene was still valid eliminated those states too.
- **Process owners** remained available to all scenes. The title's packed RunScene cache lives on the SceneTree root at `scripts/menu_run_preload.gd:128`. Shared texture caching is by asset path, for example at `scripts/asset_loader.gd:501`. The shutdown state is on the root at `scripts/application_shutdown.gd:74`. These process owners produced no exit resource paths. The sample-time orphan-node totals were stable, including the 42 on A and 62 on C, and none were listed as leaked nodes.

## 3. Fix

The VICTORY overlay relays each existing frame, timer or Tween completion through one signal that RunScene can also cancel. RunScene emits cancellation at the start of `_exit_tree()`, before its suspended callers lose their owner. Canceled playback unwinds card, movement, enemy-round and resumed-reward continuations before they reach reward finalization. The input handlers that can be resumed by this unwind skip detached-scene focus and viewport work. The normal completion path still runs on the original signals with the original values and order.
