# Tooltip loop audit

The [contact sheet](loop_audit_sheet.png) shows four frames per recording, sampled from the first frame to the last at intervals as equal as the stored frame index allows. It covers all 27 registry rows in the tip-06 worktree and the three tip-07r recording versions from `3c689faaf`. The two Collision rows are distinct recordings of the same ID. All 30 assets matched the SHA-256 in their recording sidecars.

The table inventories the installed footage and uses the recorded state to identify pieces between samples or behind darkness. Discharge and Swap therefore describe the original recordings that are awaiting replacement. Worldspine is judged against its new Raise Terrain placement. Its existing pulse remains part of the recording as directed.

**Yes** marks a piece used by the stated keyword or its source-specific sentence. **No** marks a contextual actor, an incidental piece or a result the sentence does not promise. Where a registry row serves multiple sentences, the verdict identifies which sentence uses the piece. Marks and landing ghosts count as visible rule cues. Hit flashes and loss numbers are feedback for the listed interactions. The floor column records the captured biome art. Any floor other than Vyraketh differs from Detonate's floor. Ambient biome particles are decorative and are distinct from a Light source or Umbra. No clip inventory contained a loot prop, NPC or brazier.
