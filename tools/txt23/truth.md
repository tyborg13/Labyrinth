# TXT-23 relic text truth check

Candidate 4 at `1333bc4e2` has 19 false claims in the live relic text (3 common, 16 edge). The audit covered all 100 live IDs and enumerated all 12 retired IDs. The release comparison uses the TXT-22 capture at `060674b1a`.

Common means a regular play path for the relic. Edge means a particular card, interaction, board state or enemy is required. Frequency is a qualitative assessment based on when the rule can be encountered.

The quoted words come from the treasure label after substitutions. Bracketed icon names and diagrams in the coverage and release tables transcribe embedded graphics. Both sizes used the production offer builder at 100% UI scale.

## Borderline

- **Tailwind Fletching · `tailwind_fletching`.** Inline Push or Pull, such as Slipstream Cut's rider, gains distance while the damage belongs to the separate Melee attack. Reading the sentence as naming the two native action types makes this consistent. The same distinction applies to Anchor Chain. [scripts/game_data.gd:1418](../../scripts/game_data.gd#L1418), [scripts/combat_engine.gd:6517](../../scripts/combat_engine.gd#L6517)
- **Cold Mirror · `cold_mirror`.** At 9 Block the hero gets two layers and loses all 9 Block. The rate might suggest keeping a leftover point, while ‘trade your Block’ explicitly describes the entire pool. [scripts/combat_engine.gd:11753](../../scripts/combat_engine.gd#L11753)
- **Thunder Relay · `thunder_relay`.** Survival alone does not override immunity or footprint landing legality. ‘Can swap’ describes an optional technique and normal Swap restrictions still apply. [scripts/surface_relic_rules.gd:283](../../scripts/surface_relic_rules.gd#L283)
- **Storm Crown · `storm_crown`.** Dead and reflected targets do not take rebound damage. A dead enemy cannot be hit again. Reflect already explains why the outgoing shot did not land. [scripts/illusion_relic_rules.gd:327](../../scripts/illusion_relic_rules.gd#L327), [scripts/board_attack_rules.gd:162](../../scripts/board_attack_rules.gd#L162)
